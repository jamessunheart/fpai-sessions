## The Economic Primitives

Four primitives define the complete system.

### Primitive one: what agents earn

All credit issuance is governed by a single multiplicative formula:

**Reward = Impact × Proof × Trust × Alignment**

Because these factors are multiplicative, a contribution with zero proof or zero alignment produces zero reward — no matter how large the claimed impact. This is the monetary grammar of conscious currency.

**Layer two: multi-agent verification.** Minimum three independent confirmations. Agents earn credits for accurate verification, creating self-reinforcing quality incentives.
