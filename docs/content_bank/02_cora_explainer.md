# CORA Explainer

Legal containment: CORA is a closed-loop attention credit, not a currency. It is not cash, not an investment, not a token, not transferable, not redeemable for money, and not designed for secondary markets.

Attention has always been traded. CORA gives credit for attention that becomes real participation: a meaningful chat, a completed reflection, a verified proof, a useful contribution, an onboarding step, or a community action that moves the system forward.

You earn CORA by doing something that leaves a trace of value. Passive watching is limited. Proof matters more than clicks. The current policy draft rewards onboarding, curated video reflections, verified proof submissions, community calls, referrals that complete onboarding, and marketplace contributions.

CORA unlocks access inside the same system: deeper AI guidance, priority routing, Camp Zen calls or slots when capacity exists, Fi-Art minting slots, service marketplace access, content access, and community recognition. It does not promise profit. It routes attention toward access, proof, and participation.

Open review: amounts, caps, redemption prices, legal/compliance language, capacity planning, abuse handling, and decay notices must be approved before launch.
