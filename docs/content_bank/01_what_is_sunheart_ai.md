# What Is Sunheart AI?

Sunheart AI is not another AI tool. It is a routing layer for the Full Potential OS: a system that notices where attention is going, turns that signal into the next action, and keeps the work moving without James or any user carrying the whole map in their head.

The core pattern is simple. You scan what is real, speak the clearest signal, and the system routes it: memory, decision, proof, offer, build, person, or next step. AI holds context. Humans stay upstream where their attention actually changes the outcome.

Full Potential OS is the operating form underneath it. It remembers state, routes intent, refreshes surfaces, and learns from proof. The point is not more answers. The point is fewer dropped signals and cleaner action.

Scan. Speak. Get routed.
