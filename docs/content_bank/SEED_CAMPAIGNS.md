# Seed Campaigns

These campaigns are canonical source scaffolds for `BeaconCampaign.create()`. They are organic-first. No auto-posting, no paid spend, and no platform-specific formatting belongs in this content bank.

## Campaign A — CORA Attention Hook

Concept: Give attention. Earn CORA. Unlock access.

Best for: cold audiences, curiosity-driven, broad reach.

Flow: ad/QR -> 60-second video -> reflection question -> first CORA earn -> chat onboarding.

First experiment flow: one short video + one reflection + TG entry + CORA reward + route.

Content asset: `docs/content_bank/02_cora_explainer.md`.

Guard: organic first. Paid only when chat-start rate is greater than 30% of clicks.

## Campaign B — AI Routing Hook

Concept: ChatGPT answers. Sunheart AI routes your life into action.

Best for: founders, builders, creators, AI-aware audiences.

Flow: ad -> "get your routing map" -> One Chat -> Agent Passport started.

Content angle: side-by-side comparison: AI gives answers; FPOS gives next steps.

Content asset: `docs/content_bank/01_what_is_sunheart_ai.md`.

Guard: organic first through founder communities, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X.

## Campaign C — Camp Zen Proof Hook

Concept: Come scattered. Leave routed.

Best for: retreat, conscious business, and seeker audiences.

Flow: ad/content -> Camp Zen immersion landing -> chat qualification -> call/application.

Tone: experiential, embodied, not AI-heavy.

Content asset: `docs/content_bank/04_camp_zen_explainer.md`.

Guard: organic first through Instagram and community posts.

## First Experiment

Campaign A uses `docs/content_bank/02_cora_explainer.md`. Run organic only. Do not turn on paid spend until 5 organic chat-starts are tracked.
