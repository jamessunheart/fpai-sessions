# Vision — What We're Building and Why

## The Problem

Human potential is fragmented. People have gifts, vision, and capacity that never gets expressed because the infrastructure of daily life — coordination, decisions, tasks, matching, money — consumes them.

Sunheart has lived this. Downloads outrun execution. The cocoon breaks. The genius stalls. The same problem exists for every executive, every conscious entrepreneur, every person trying to build something meaningful.

## The Solution — Three Layers

### Layer 1: Personal Operating System (Build for Sunheart First)
An AI Chief of Staff loaded with full context — philosophy, frameworks, projects, people, priorities — managing a pool of human assistants on Sunheart's behalf.

- Sunheart speaks a download
- AI Chief of Staff converts it to tasks
- Assistant pool executes
- Decisions distilled to yes/no
- Sunheart stays in the cocoon

### Layer 2: Full Potential Network (The Product)
What works for Sunheart gets offered to others.

- Public-facing AI agent proactively meets people
- Builds their Full Potential Profile through ambient conversation
- Activates personalized AI assistant (AI Executive Suite)
- Optionally matches them with a human assistant
- Connects them to the CORA Credits network
- AI-to-AI matching finds collaborators, clients, services, opportunities

### Layer 3: The Economic Engine (The Long Game)
Once enough people are profiled and activated, their AI assistants match them to each other — settled in CORA Credits. Circulation economics at scale.

- Velocity ~5.0 vs capitalism's ~1.0
- Leakage ~0.5% vs capitalism's ~18%
- Value compounds for participants instead of extracting to owners
- Grounded in 90 years of proven precedent (WIR Bank, Twin Oaks, Dancing Rabbit)
