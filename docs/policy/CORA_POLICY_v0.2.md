# CORA Policy v0.2

Draft status: review-only. James must review and approve this policy before any CORA feature, onboarding flow, Fi-Art flow, marketplace flow, or retention loop references it. This document is a product policy draft, not legal advice, and it requires compliance review before launch.

## 1. What CORA Is

CORA is a closed-loop attention credit for the CORA system: it is earned through meaningful engagement, contribution, and verified participation, then spent on access inside the same system. CORA is not a currency, not an investment, not redeemable for cash, and not designed to leave the CORA environment. It exists to route attention, proof, and participation toward real access, experiences, and community contribution without becoming a financial instrument.

## 2. What Earns CORA

Earn rules are intended to reward proof of participation, not passive accumulation. Suggested amounts below are provisional and must be reviewed against real redemption capacity before launch.

| Earn event | Suggested CORA | Event cap | Daily cap contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| First meaningful chat session / onboarding completion | 100 | Once per account | 100 |
| Watching a full curated video/reflection | 20 | 2 per day | 40 |
| Completing a proof by submitting a verifiable action | 50 | 3 per day | 150 |
| Inviting someone who completes onboarding / referral proof | 150 | 2 per week | 150 |
| Participating in a community call | 75 | 1 per day | 75 |
| Contributing a skill or offer to the marketplace | 200 | 2 per month | 200 |
| Weekly consistent engagement streak | 150 | 1 per week | 150 |

Default cap: a user may earn no more than 300 CORA per day across all event types. Event-specific caps apply even if the daily cap has room remaining. Abuse, duplicate accounts, unverifiable proofs, or automated engagement can void pending earnings.

## 3. What Backs CORA

CORA is backed by real access, experiences, and goods available inside the CORA ecosystem, not by financial instruments, cash reserves, equity, debt, tokens, commodities, or promises of appreciation. The backing pool should be capacity-planned so issued CORA maps to deliverable access.

Backing assets:

- AI session credits.
- Camp Zen access.
- Fi-Art minting slots.
- Community call seats.
- Service hours.
- Content access.

## 5. Issuance Caps

Initial issuance caps:

- Max CORA per user per day: 300.
- Max CORA per event type: governed by the table in Section 2.
- Soft total CORA in circulation target: 1,000,000 active CORA until redemption data proves capacity can support more.
- Per-account lifetime review trigger: 10,000 earned CORA or any suspicious earn pattern.

Policy review is triggered when any of the following happens:

- Redemption fulfillment rate drops below 90% over a rolling 30-day period.
- More than 25% of attempted redemptions are delayed or waitlisted.
- Average user balances rise for 30 days while redemption volume falls.
- Marketplace prices need to rise more than 20% to protect capacity.
- Abuse, duplicate-account, referral-farming, or unverifiable-proof patterns increase materially.
- Legal or compliance review changes the allowable presentation or mechanics.

## 6. What CORA Cannot Do

This section is mandatory containment. Product copy, onboarding copy, marketplace copy, community copy, and support replies must not contradict it.

- CORA cannot be converted to cash or fiat.
- CORA cannot be traded on secondary markets.
- CORA cannot be used as investment language.
- CORA cannot be sold directly.
- CORA is not a security, not a token, not a cryptocurrency.
- CORA expires after 12 months of account inactivity.
- CORA is non-transferable between accounts.

Additional containment:

- CORA does not represent equity, debt, ownership, dividends, profit share, yield, interest, or governance rights.
- CORA has no guaranteed market value, exchange value, floor price, resale value, or appreciation expectation.
- CORA cannot be pledged, collateralized, lent, borrowed, staked, pooled, or used to raise funds.
- CORA balances may be corrected, reversed, frozen, or removed for fraud, abuse, duplicate accounts, policy violations, or compliance reasons.
- CORA is issued and spent only under the current reviewed policy; no user-facing feature may promise future redemption menus or future pricing.

## 7. Decay and Renewal

CORA remains active while a user is engaged. If an account is inactive for more than 90 consecutive days, CORA decays at 10% per month until the user re-engages or the balance expires. Re-engagement through a meaningful chat session, verified proof, community call participation, or marketplace contribution resets the decay clock.

Hermes should send decay notices before expiry or material decay events:

- Day 75 of inactivity: reminder that decay begins after 90 days.
- Day 90 of inactivity: notice that monthly decay has started.
- 30 days before 12-month inactivity expiry: expiry warning.
- 7 days before 12-month inactivity expiry: final expiry warning.

After 12 months of account inactivity, remaining CORA expires and returns to the system reserve. Expired CORA has no cash value and no refund value.
