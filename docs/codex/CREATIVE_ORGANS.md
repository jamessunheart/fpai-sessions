## Why FPOS Tracks These Organs

FPOS must be able to locate the creative process, distinguish its surfaces, and
notice when its recorded understanding becomes stale. Awareness does not grant
permission to generate, spend, notify, publish, deploy, or approve creative work.

## Organ Map

### Music Magic — production organism

Music Magic process: artifact-gated production pipeline.

Canonical root: `/Users/jamessunheart/MusicMagic/`.

Each song is one production artifact under `productions/<song_slug>/`. Its
ledgers are the source of truth. The default process is:

`intake → score → storyboard → stills/proof → Sunstream selection → animation → QA → release`

Before work resumes, agents read the shared root instructions, production
registry, exact production manifest, source receipt, phase log, and completion
record. A verified completion record and `do_not_restart_production` outrank
stale intermediate state.

### Sunstream — founder review organ

Sunstream function: founder review surface.

The canonical per-production review surface is generated by `mm_sunstream` from
the production ledgers and storyboard. It exposes the pipeline, timed scenes,
audio review, A/B choices, cost/time, and gate state. The richer v1 application
remains a design and feature source until its features are ported.

Sunstream is where machine-prepared creative work meets James's review. It does
not turn a finished-looking artifact into an approved, released, or public one.

### Sundance — dance-production review organ

Sundance function: private dance-production workspace.

Canonical interface root:
`/Users/jamessunheart/MusicMagic/interfaces/sundance_v1/`.

Sundance holds the private set library and dance-production review state. Its
project pages carry exact reviews, energy/timeline evidence, and local review
audio for ecstatic journeys and dance editions. AI Director recommendations
remain separate from James's listening or dance-test confirmation,
performance-readiness decision, release approval, and publication state.
