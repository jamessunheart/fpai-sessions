## PREAMBLE

The Commons Ministry is established as a specialized ministry of the Church of Consciousness, operating within the Cora Nation Private Membership Association. Its purpose is the regenerative circulation of resources and the meeting of members' genuine needs through conscious stewardship.

The Ministry operates the TRUST Token system and the Commons Reserve Fund, using AI-assisted governance within human-defined guardrails to serve the community.

---

## ARTICLE I: PURPOSE AND MISSION

### Section 1.1 - Primary Purpose
The Commons Ministry exists to:
1. Facilitate the regenerative circulation of resources among PMA members
2. Meet members' genuine needs (survival, stability, growth) when abundance allows
3. Recognize and track member contributions through the TRUST Token
4. Steward the Commons Reserve Fund for the benefit of all members
5. Model an alternative to extractive financial systems

### Section 1.2 - Mission Statement
*"To build infrastructure for human flourishing through conscious circulation of resources, where members contribute to a shared commons and receive support for genuine needs."*

### Section 1.3 - Guiding Principles
1. **Needs-Meeting Over Profit** – Resources flow to meet real needs, not to generate returns
2. **Contribution Over Accumulation** – Active participation is valued over passive holding
3. **Community Over Individual** – The commons serves all members, not individual gain
4. **Participation Over Passivity** – Benefits require active contribution
5. **Transparency Over Opacity** – Operations are visible to members
6. **Regeneration Over Extraction** – Systems heal and grow, not deplete

## ARTICLE III: TRUST TOKEN

### Section 3.1 - Nature and Purpose
The TRUST Token is an internal membership token that:
- Tracks member contributions to the commons
- Determines eligibility for needs-based ministry benefits
- Provides governance voting power
- Grants access to ministry programs

TRUST is NOT:
- A security or investment
- A currency or money
- Redeemable for cash
- A promise of financial returns

### Section 3.2 - Earning TRUST
Members earn TRUST through contribution:

| Activity | TRUST Earned |
|----------|-------------|
| Service to others (verified) | 10 per hour |
| Governance participation | 5 per vote |
| Art/content creation | Variable |
| Referrals | 50 per member |
| Financial contribution | 1 per UC |
| Community building | Variable |

### Section 3.3 - Proof of Contribution
To be eligible for ministry benefits, members must maintain active participation:
- Minimum contribution score: 100 per quarter
- Tracked and verified by ministry systems
- Prevents passive holding without participation

### Section 3.4 - Operational Policy
Full operational details are defined in:
`docs/legal/token/TRUST_SACRED_CIRCULATION_POLICY.md`

## ARTICLE IV: COMMONS RESERVE FUND

### Section 4.1 - Fund Purpose
The Commons Reserve Fund exists to:
- Hold resources for needs-meeting
- Provide stability through safety reserves
- Enable ministry benefits when abundance allows
- NOT to generate financial returns for members

### Section 4.2 - Fund Custody
The Fund is held within the Sunheart Private Trust:
- Trustee retains legal custody and fiduciary duty
- AI systems manage within Trustee-approved parameters
- Church council provides oversight

### Section 4.3 - Fund Sources

| Source | Allocation |
|--------|------------|
| UC protocol fees | 30% |
| $FI transaction fees | 30% |
| LLC profits | 30% |
| Member contributions | 80% |
| Treasury Arena yields | 30% of surplus |
| Service margins | 20% |

### Section 4.4 - Fund Allocations
When Fund has abundance beyond safety reserves:

| Category | Allocation | Purpose |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Survival | 40% | Food, shelter, health |
| Stability | 25% | Debt relief, emergency |
| Growth | 20% | Education, tools |
| Contribution | 10% | Contributor recognition |
| Infrastructure | 5% | Commons infrastructure |

### Section 4.5 - Hard Guardrails

| Guardrail | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Minimum reserve ratio | 120% of committed |
| Maximum daily change | 5% |
| Emergency freeze | THS < 0.8 |
| Human override | Always available |
| Maximum single allocation | 5% of monthly budget |

## ARTICLE V: MINISTRY BENEFITS

### Section 5.1 - Eligibility
To be eligible for ministry benefits, members must:
1. Hold TRUST tokens
2. Meet Proof of Contribution requirements (100+ quarterly score)
3. Demonstrate genuine need
4. Submit request through approved process

### Section 5.2 - Benefit Types
Ministry benefits are provided as:
- Services (AI, creative, educational)
- Support (housing, health, emergency)
- Access (programs, resources, community)
- Recognition (contributor status, governance)

Benefits are NEVER provided as:
- Cash or currency
- Cryptocurrency
- Financial returns or yield

### Section 5.3 - Request Process
1. Member submits request via ministry platform
2. Personal AI steward assesses need and eligibility
3. Global AI checks commons health and fairness limits
4. If approved, benefit is provided in-kind
5. If denied, member receives explanation and appeal path

### Section 5.4 - Appeals
Members may appeal benefit decisions to:
1. Ministry Council (first level)
2. Church leadership (final level)

## ARTICLE VII: COMPLIANCE AND LANGUAGE

### Section 7.1 - Legal Posture
The Ministry is designed to NOT be a security or investment scheme:
- TRUST is earned, not purchased
- Benefits require active participation
- No financial returns are promised or expected
- All activity is private, member-to-member

### Section 7.2 - Forbidden Language

| Forbidden | Use Instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| Investment | Contribution |
| Profit, returns, yield | Blessings, abundance |
| Dividend | Ministry benefit |
| ROI | Blessings received |
| Guaranteed | When abundance allows |

### Section 7.3 - Required Disclaimers

## ARTICLE VIII: AMENDMENTS

### Section 8.1 - Amendment Process
This Charter may be amended by:
1. Proposal by Ministry Council or members (20% of voting power)
2. Church council review and approval
3. Member vote (30% quorum, 66% approval)
4. 30-day notice period before implementation

### Section 8.2 - Non-Amendable Provisions
The following cannot be amended without Church council supermajority:
- Primary purpose (Article I)
- Hard guardrails (Article IV, Section 4.5)
- Human override rights
- Member fundamental rights

---

## ARTICLE IX: DISSOLUTION

### Section 9.1 - Dissolution Authority
The Commons Ministry may only be dissolved by:
- Church council supermajority vote
- With 90-day notice to members

### Section 9.2 - Asset Distribution
Upon dissolution:
1. All outstanding ministry benefits are fulfilled (if possible)
2. Remaining Commons Reserve Fund returns to Sunheart Trust
3. TRUST tokens become void
4. Members receive final accounting
