## PREAMBLE

The Stewardship Ministry is established as a specialized ministry of the Church of Consciousness, operating within the Cora Nation Private Membership Association. Its purpose is to facilitate conscious wealth stewardship through education, community, and trading software tools that embody aligned abundance principles.

The Ministry operates Aria Trading Services, providing members with AI-powered trading software as a spiritual practice of conscious wealth stewardship, using technology within human-defined guardrails to serve the fellowship.

---

## ARTICLE I: PURPOSE AND MISSION

### Section 1.1 - Primary Purpose
The Stewardship Ministry exists to:
1. Teach and demonstrate conscious wealth stewardship principles
2. Provide trading software tools for members to practice aligned abundance
3. Facilitate community around regenerative economics
4. Model conscious approaches to resource growth
5. Support members in developing healthy relationship with wealth

### Section 1.2 - Mission Statement
*"To awaken conscious wealth stewardship through education, community, and tools that embody aligned abundance principles, where technology serves spiritual practice."*

### Section 1.3 - Guiding Principles
1. **Consciousness Over Computation** – Technology serves spiritual awareness, not replaces it
2. **Education Over Speculation** – Members learn, not gamble
3. **Community Over Competition** – Members support each other's growth
4. **Alignment Over Accumulation** – Right relationship with wealth matters
5. **Transparency Over Opacity** – Operations are visible to members
6. **Regeneration Over Extraction** – Wealth grows to serve, not deplete

### Section 1.4 - What This Ministry Is NOT
The Stewardship Ministry is NOT:
- An investment adviser or wealth manager
- A securities broker or dealer
- A money transmitter
- A commercial financial services business
- Offering guaranteed returns or profits

## ARTICLE III: GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

### Section 3.1 - Oversight Hierarchy
```
Church of Consciousness (508c1a)
    │ Ultimate spiritual authority
    ▼
Sunheart Private Trust
    │ Holds Aria IP and pooled capital (Tier 2)
    │ Trustee retains fiduciary duty
    ▼

    │ Membership container
    ▼
Stewardship Ministry Council
    │ Day-to-day governance
    ▼
Aria Stewardship LLC (Wyoming)
    │ Operations and liability containment
    ▼
AI Trading Systems (Aria)
    │ Execution within guardrails
    ▼
Members (Stewards and Trustees)
```

### Section 3.2 - Ministry Council
The Ministry Council is responsible for:
- Setting ministry policy within Church guidelines
- Overseeing Aria trading systems
- Reviewing strategy parameters
- Responding to member concerns
- Recommending charter amendments

**Composition:**
- Minimum 3 members
- Appointed by Church council
- Must include at least one with trading experience
- Must include at least one with spiritual/pastoral role

### Section 3.3 - Aria Stewardship LLC
The LLC handles operational matters:
- Software development and maintenance
- Member account management
- API integrations with exchanges
- Compliance documentation
- Financial operations

**Ownership:** Aria Stewardship LLC is **wholly owned by Sunheart Private Trust**, consistent with the Full Potential entity structure where all operating LLCs are Trust-owned. See `FI-Art/legal/FI_ART_LEGAL_STRUCTURE.md` for the canonical entity architecture.

**Liability:** Operating activities are contained within the LLC, protecting Trust and Church assets.

### Section 3.4 - AI Trading Systems
Aria serves as automated trading software to:
- Execute predefined strategies
- Manage risk according to parameters
- Monitor market conditions
- Alert members to significant events
- Provide performance reporting

AI systems operate under human-defined guardrails and can be stopped at any time.

## ARTICLE IV: ARIA TRADING SERVICES

### Section 4.1 - Service Description
Aria Trading Services provides:
- Automated trading software
- Strategy execution on member accounts
- Risk management systems
- Performance reporting
- Emergency stop controls

### Section 4.2 - Service Tiers

**Tier 1: Steward (Connected Account)**

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Custody | Member retains full custody |
| Access | Member's own exchange account (e.g., Hyperliquid) |
| Control | Member can disable at any time |
| API Keys | Member provides; encrypted storage |
| Risk | Member bears all trading risk |
| Cost | 100 UC/month subscription |

**Tier 2: Trustee (Pooled Participation)**

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Custody | Sunheart Private Trust |
| Access | Member contributes to Fellowship Pool |
| Control | Pro-rata share; quarterly redemptions |
| API Keys | Trust-controlled master account |
| Risk | Shared across pool participants |
| Cost | 10% of gains (in UC credits) |

### Section 4.3 - Strategy Library
Available trading strategies include:
- TRUE LEVEL 10 (adaptive regime-aware strategy)
- DCA Enhanced (dollar-cost averaging with enhancements)
- Mean Reversion (buy dips, sell spikes)
- Trend Following (with-trend entries)

Strategies are documented with:
- Entry/exit rules
- Risk parameters
- Expected behavior in different market conditions
- Historical performance (with disclaimer)

### Section 4.4 - Risk Management
Built-in safeguards:
- Maximum position size limits
- Stop-loss on every trade
- Daily loss limits
- Drawdown protection
- Emergency stop capability

### Section 4.5 - Trading Software Disclaimer
**ARIA IS TRADING SOFTWARE, NOT INVESTMENT ADVICE.**

- No personalized recommendations
- No analysis of member's personal situation
- No fiduciary relationship
- No guaranteed performance
- Past performance does not indicate future results
- MEMBERS MAY LOSE ALL CAPITAL

## ARTICLE V: FINANCIAL STRUCTURE

### Section 5.1 - Payment in UC Credits
All payments are made in UC (Universal Credits):
- 1 UC = $1.00 USD (internal exchange rate)
- UC cannot be redeemed for cash
- UC is internal membership token

### Section 5.2 - Fee Structure

**Tier 1: Steward**
- 100 UC per month subscription
- Billed monthly
- Prorated for partial months
- Service suspended if insufficient UC

**Tier 2: Trustee**
- 10% of gains (performance fee)
- Calculated quarterly
- No fee if no gains
- Deducted before distribution

### Section 5.3 - Revenue Flow
```
Member pays UC Credits
    │
    ▼
Aria Stewardship LLC
    │ Operations
    ▼
Sunheart Private Trust
    │ Asset holding
    ▼
Church of Consciousness
    │ Mission funding
    ▼
Ministry Programs
    (Education, Community, Tools)
```

**Canonical Reference:** See Token Strategy (`docs/protocols/TOKENS_STRATEGY.md`) for complete token ecosystem including UC, TRUST, and $FI relationships.

### Section 5.4 - Fellowship Pool (Tier 2)
The pooled fund:
- Held by Sunheart Private Trust
- Managed by Aria trading systems
- Pro-rata ownership tracked per member
- Quarterly valuation and reporting
- Quarterly redemption windows (30-day notice)

## ARTICLE VI: MEMBER RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

### Section 6.1 - Member Rights
Members have the right to:
- Access trading software per their tier
- View their account performance
- Disable trading at any time (Tier 1)
- Request redemption at quarterly windows (Tier 2)
- Receive performance reports
- Participate in education and community programs
- Appeal decisions
- Withdraw from the Ministry

### Section 6.2 - Member Responsibilities
Members are responsible for:
- Reading and understanding all disclosures
- Only committing capital they can afford to lose
- Maintaining security of their API keys (Tier 1)
- Participating in good faith
- Using approved language
- Not misrepresenting services to others
- Complying with Ministry policies

### Section 6.3 - Membership Agreement
Participation requires signing:
- Cora Nation PMA Membership Agreement
- Stewardship Ministry Trading Addendum
- Risk Disclosures acknowledgment

## ARTICLE VII: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### Section 7.1 - Internal Resolution
Disputes shall first be addressed through:
- Direct communication with Ministry support
- Review by Ministry Council

### Section 7.2 - Binding Arbitration
If internal resolution fails:
- Binding arbitration under AAA rules
- Single arbitrator
- Location: Online or Phoenix, Arizona
- Decision is final

### Section 7.3 - Limitations
Members waive:
- Right to jury trial
- Right to class action
- Claims exceeding stated liability limits

### Section 7.4 - Liability Limits
Maximum liability:
- Tier 1: UC credits paid in previous 12 months
- Tier 2: Member's remaining pro-rata share

## ARTICLE VIII: COMPLIANCE

### Section 8.1 - Legal Posture
The Ministry is structured to NOT be:
- An investment adviser (software tool, not advice)
- A securities offering (no profit expectations required)
- Money transmission (crypto-to-crypto, internal tokens)

### Section 8.2 - Regulatory Protections
- First Amendment (religious practice)
- Private membership (not public offering)
- Software tool (not advisory service)
- Internal tokens (not money)

### Section 8.3 - Forbidden Language

| Forbidden | Use Instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| Investment advice | Trading software |
| Guaranteed returns | May lose capital |
| Profit expectations | Spiritual practice |
| Financial planning | Wealth stewardship |
| Wealth management | Conscious stewardship |

### Section 8.4 - Required Disclaimers
All Ministry materials must include:
- "Trading software, not investment advice"
- "Capital at risk - you may lose all funds"
- "Past performance not indicative of future results"
- "Private membership, not public offering"

## ARTICLE X: DISSOLUTION

### Section 10.1 - Dissolution Authority
The Ministry may only be dissolved by Church council supermajority with 90-day notice.

### Section 10.2 - Asset Distribution
Upon dissolution:
1. All trading positions closed
2. Tier 1: API keys returned/deleted
3. Tier 2: Pool distributed pro-rata to members
4. Remaining assets to Sunheart Trust
5. Final accounting to all members
