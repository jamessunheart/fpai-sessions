## Regulatory Strategy

### Securities (SEC)
**Position:** NOT a security per Howey Test
- No expectation of profit from others' efforts
- Utility token / service credits
- Active participation required
- Private membership (not public offering)

### Money Transmission (FinCEN)
**Position:** NOT money transmission
- Crypto-to-crypto (no fiat conversion)
- Internal tokens (UC, TRUST)
- Religious/educational exemption
- No custody of user funds (Tier 1)

### Investment Advice (Advisers Act)
**Position:** NOT investment advice
- Software tool (automated execution)
- No personalized recommendations
- No fiduciary relationship
- User controls enable/disable
