# Cora Nation Private Membership Association
## Stewardship Ministry Participation Addendum (Trading Services)

**Addendum to the Cora Nation PMA Membership Agreement**

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the undersigned ("Member") is a member in good standing of the Cora Nation Private Membership Association ("PMA");

WHEREAS, the Church of Consciousness has established the Stewardship Ministry as a ministry arm dedicated to conscious wealth stewardship and aligned abundance practices;

WHEREAS, the Stewardship Ministry offers trading software tools ("Aria Trading Services") to assist members in practicing conscious wealth stewardship;

WHEREAS, Member desires to access Aria Trading Services as part of their spiritual practice of aligned abundance;

WHEREAS, this Addendum supplements, and does not replace, any other addendums signed by Member (e.g., TRUST Token Addendum, $FI Token Addendum);

NOW, THEREFORE, Member agrees to the following terms:

---

## ARTICLE I: ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

### Section 1.1 - Understanding of Aria Trading Services
Member acknowledges that:

1. **Nature of Service:** Aria Trading Services is AUTOMATED TRADING SOFTWARE, not investment advice, financial planning, or wealth management services.

2. **Software Tool:** Aria is a software tool that executes predefined trading strategies. It does NOT provide personalized investment recommendations.

3. **Not Investment Advice:** Aria does NOT analyze Member's personal financial situation, goals, or risk tolerance. Member makes their own decision to enable or disable the software.

4. **No Fiduciary Relationship:** The Stewardship Ministry, Church of Consciousness, and Aria are NOT fiduciaries. No advisor-client relationship exists.

5. **Educational Purpose:** Trading software is offered as part of spiritual education in conscious wealth stewardship, demonstrating aligned abundance principles.

### Section 1.2 - Understanding of Service Tiers
Member acknowledges understanding of the available service tiers:

**Tier 1: Steward (Connected Account)**
- Member connects their OWN exchange account (e.g., Hyperliquid)
- Member retains full control and custody of their funds
- Member can disable trading at any time
- Cost: UC credits (subscription)
- Member bears ALL trading risk

**Tier 2: Trustee (Pooled Participation)**
- Member contributes capital to Fellowship Pool
- Pool is held by Sunheart Private Trust for member benefit
- Pro-rata share of pool performance
- Quarterly redemption windows
- Cost: Percentage of gains (in UC credits)
- Shared risk with other participants

### Section 1.3 - Understanding of Risks
Member acknowledges that:

1. **Capital at Risk:** Trading involves substantial risk of loss. YOU MAY LOSE ALL OR PART OF YOUR CAPITAL.

2. **No Guaranteed Returns:** There are NO guaranteed profits, returns, or performance levels. Past performance does NOT indicate future results.

3. **Technology Risk:** Software may malfunction, experience downtime, or execute trades incorrectly. Internet connectivity issues may affect performance.

4. **Market Risk:** Cryptocurrency markets are volatile. Prices can move rapidly against positions.

5. **Leverage Risk:** If leverage is used, losses can exceed initial capital.

6. **Operational Risk:** Exchange outages, API failures, or other technical issues may result in losses.

## ARTICLE II: WAIVERS

### Section 2.1 - Waiver of Investment Advice Claims
Member WAIVES any claim that Aria Trading Services constitutes "investment advice" under federal or state law, including the Investment Advisers Act of 1940.

### Section 2.2 - Waiver of Securities Claims
Member WAIVES any claim that participation in Trading Services constitutes purchase of a "security" under federal or state securities laws.

### Section 2.3 - Waiver of Profit Expectations
Member WAIVES any claim based on expectation of guaranteed profits, returns, or performance. Member understands trading may result in TOTAL LOSS of capital.

### Section 2.4 - Waiver of Fiduciary Claims
Member WAIVES any claim that Stewardship Ministry, Church of Consciousness, Sunheart Private Trust, or Aria have fiduciary duties to Member.

### Section 2.5 - Release of Claims
Member RELEASES the Church of Consciousness, Cora Nation PMA, Stewardship Ministry, Sunheart Private Trust, Aria Stewardship LLC, and all related parties, officers, directors, employees, and AI systems from any and all claims arising from:
- Trading losses
- Software malfunctions
- Market movements
- Exchange failures
- API errors
- Any other trading-related events

## ARTICLE III: MEMBER OBLIGATIONS

### Section 3.1 - Good Faith Participation
Member agrees to:
- Use Trading Services as spiritual practice of conscious wealth stewardship
- Understand that trading is inherently risky
- Only commit capital they can afford to lose
- Maintain security of their exchange API keys
- Review and understand trading strategies before enabling
- Monitor their account regularly

### Section 3.2 - API Key Security (Tier 1 Only)
For Connected Account members:
- Member is responsible for generating and managing API keys
- Member should enable only necessary permissions (trading, not withdrawal)
- Member should use IP whitelisting when available
- Member must notify Ministry immediately if keys are compromised
- Member understands Ministry encrypts but does not guarantee key security

### Section 3.3 - Capital Contribution (Tier 2 Only)
For Pooled Participation members:
- Member contributes capital voluntarily
- Member understands capital is held by Sunheart Private Trust
- Member understands redemptions are processed quarterly
- Member understands pro-rata share may increase or decrease
- Member understands performance fees are paid in UC credits

### Section 3.4 - Prohibited Representations
Member agrees NOT to:
- Represent Trading Services as guaranteed income opportunity
- Promise returns, yields, or profits to others
- Solicit non-members to join for trading returns
- Use forbidden language (guaranteed returns, risk-free, passive income)
- Misrepresent the nature of the service

## ARTICLE IV: PAYMENT IN UC CREDITS

### Section 4.1 - Understanding of UC Credits
Member acknowledges that:

1. **Internal Token:** UC (Universal Credit) is an internal membership token, not legal tender or cryptocurrency.

2. **Fixed Exchange:** 1 UC = $1.00 USD for internal purposes only.

3. **No Cash Redemption:** UC credits cannot be redeemed for cash.

4. **Payment for Services:** UC credits pay for Trading Services as internal fellowship exchange.

### Section 4.2 - Fee Structure
Member agrees to the following fee structure:

**Tier 1: Steward**
- Subscription: 100 UC per month
- Billed monthly to Member's UC balance
- Service suspended if insufficient UC balance

**Tier 2: Trustee**
- Performance Fee: 10% of gains
- Calculated quarterly
- Deducted from Member's share before distribution
- No fee if no gains

## ARTICLE V: AI TRADING SYSTEMS

### Section 5.1 - Nature of AI Trading
Member acknowledges that:

1. **Automated Execution:** Aria is an AI-powered system that automatically executes trades based on programmed strategies.

2. **No Human Review:** Individual trades are NOT reviewed by humans before execution.

3. **Strategy Parameters:** Trading strategies have defined parameters for entry, exit, position sizing, and risk management.

4. **Learning Systems:** Some strategies may adapt based on market conditions and past performance.

5. **No Guarantee:** AI systems are NOT infallible. They may make poor trades or fail entirely.

### Section 5.2 - Consent to AI Trading
Member CONSENTS to:
- AI systems executing trades on their behalf (Tier 1) or with their contributed capital (Tier 2)
- AI systems accessing their exchange account via API (Tier 1)
- AI systems making trading decisions without human approval
- AI systems applying risk management rules automatically

### Section 5.3 - Emergency Controls
Member understands they may:
- Disable Aria trading at any time (Tier 1)
- Request emergency stop via Telegram or dashboard
- Remove API keys to immediately halt trading (Tier 1)
- Request redemption at quarterly windows (Tier 2)

## ARTICLE VI: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### Section 6.1 - Internal Resolution
Disputes shall first be addressed through internal PMA resolution processes.

### Section 6.2 - Binding Arbitration
If internal resolution fails, disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration:
- Arbitration under AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules
- Single arbitrator selected by mutual agreement
- Location: Online or Phoenix, Arizona
- Decision is final and binding

### Section 6.3 - Waiver of Rights
Member WAIVES:
- Right to jury trial
- Right to participate in class action
- Right to pursue claims in court (except to confirm arbitration award)

### Section 6.4 - Limitation of Liability
Maximum liability of Church of Consciousness, Cora Nation PMA, Stewardship Ministry, Sunheart Private Trust, and Aria Stewardship LLC shall be limited to:
- Tier 1: UC credits paid in previous 12 months
- Tier 2: Member's remaining pro-rata share of pool
