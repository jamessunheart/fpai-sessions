# Cora Nation Private Membership Association
## Commons Ministry Participation Addendum (TRUST Token)

**Addendum to the Cora Nation PMA Membership Agreement**

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the undersigned ("Member") is a member in good standing of the Cora Nation Private Membership Association ("PMA");

WHEREAS, the Church of Consciousness has established the Commons Ministry as a ministry arm dedicated to the regenerative circulation of resources and the meeting of members' genuine needs;

WHEREAS, the Commons Ministry utilizes an internal membership token known as the "TRUST Token" to track contribution and participation among members;

NOW, THEREFORE, Member agrees to the following terms:

---

## ARTICLE I: ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

### Section 1.1 - Understanding of TRUST Token
Member acknowledges that:

1. **Nature of TRUST:** The TRUST Token is an internal membership token used exclusively among PMA members to track contribution and eligibility for ministry benefits.

2. **Not a Security:** TRUST is NOT a security, investment contract, or financial instrument. Member is NOT purchasing TRUST with expectation of profit.

3. **Earned, Not Purchased:** TRUST is earned through contribution (time, service, donations, art, referrals), NOT purchased as a speculative asset.

4. **No Guaranteed Value:** TRUST has no monetary value and cannot be redeemed for cash.

### Section 1.2 - Understanding of Ministry Benefits
Member acknowledges that:

1. **Needs-Based Support:** Ministry benefits meet genuine needs, NOT provide financial returns.
2. **Not Guaranteed:** Benefits depend on commons abundance and active participation.
3. **In-Kind Only:** Benefits are services/support, never cash.
4. **Participation Required:** Proof of Contribution required for eligibility.

---

## ARTICLE II: WAIVERS

### Section 2.1 - Waiver of Securities Claims
Member WAIVES any claim that TRUST constitutes a "security" under federal or state law.

### Section 2.2 - Waiver of Investment Expectations
Member WAIVES any claim based on expectation of profit, financial returns, or passive income from TRUST.

### Section 2.3 - Release of Claims
Member RELEASES the Church of Consciousness, Cora Nation PMA, Commons Ministry, Sunheart Private Trust, and all related parties from claims arising from TRUST or ministry benefits.

---

## ARTICLE III: MEMBER OBLIGATIONS

### Section 3.1 - Good Faith Participation
Member agrees to:
- Treat TRUST as sacred membership resource, not speculative asset
- Maintain active contribution (Proof of Contribution)
- Request benefits only for genuine needs
- Use approved language (no forbidden terms)

### Section 3.2 - Prohibited Representations
Member agrees NOT to:
- Represent TRUST as investment opportunity
- Promise returns, yields, or profits
- Solicit non-members
- Use forbidden language (investment, profit, yield, dividend, ROI, guaranteed)

---

## ARTICLE IV: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Disputes shall be resolved through:
1. Internal PMA resolution
2. Binding arbitration
3. Member waives jury trial and class action rights

---

## ARTICLE V: AI STEWARDSHIP

Member acknowledges that AI systems serve as "Trustee Assistants" within human-governed guardrails, and consents to AI processing of contribution data and needs assessments.

---

By signing, Member acknowledges:
- TRUST is NOT an investment
- NO expectation of profit
- Benefits require active participation and are NOT guaranteed
