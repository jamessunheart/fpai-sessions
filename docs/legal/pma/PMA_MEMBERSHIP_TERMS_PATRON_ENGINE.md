## 1. Purpose and Private Association Frame

These Patron Engine Membership Terms supplement, and do not replace, the Cora Nation Private Membership Association Agreement. They define how a member may enter the Patron Engine, receive present-tense member utility, and participate in the private association without receiving equity, investment rights, profit rights, or any promised financial return.

The Patron Engine exists to let members support and participate in the Cora Nation / Full Potential ecosystem through private membership access, events, stays, sessions, tools, community participation, and patron gifts. It follows the Patron Engine principle:

**Sell the standing thing. Gift the growing thing. Let the story do the recruiting.**

The Patron Engine must not be used to pre-sell future projects, advertise compounding value, raise investment capital, promise appreciation, or offer member returns.

---

## 2. Parties

These terms are entered into by:

1. **Cora Nation Private Membership Association** or its designated membership steward, acting only inside the private association relationship; and
2. **The Member**, an individual accepted into good standing under the Cora Nation PMA Membership Agreement.

Only members in good standing may use Patron Engine access. A non-member must complete the PMA admission path before receiving Patron Engine member access.

---

## 3. Two Separate Doors

The Patron Engine has two legally and operationally separate doors. A checkout, invoice, receipt, onboarding screen, or member record must identify which door is being used.

### 3.1 Membership Access Door

The Membership Access Door is a private membership access transaction. The member pays or contributes for access to specific present-tense utility that is already standing or clearly available at the time of purchase.

Examples of acceptable standing utility include:

- a specified gathering, dinner, donor party, workshop, or retreat seat;
- a stay, visit, or experience that can be scheduled under existing Zen Village capacity;
- access to a private member call, session, tool, learning room, or build-in-public show that already exists;
- a defined community membership period with named current benefits.

Membership access payments are not represented as tax-deductible donations unless counsel approves that treatment in writing for a specific structure. They are not investments, securities, partnership interests, loans, revenue shares, or purchases of equity.

### 3.2 Patron Gift Door

The Patron Gift Door is a voluntary gift, donation, or patron contribution to support the mission. A patron gift may receive gratitude, acknowledgment, community thanks, or invitations that are allowed by counsel, but the gift itself must not be sold as a purchase of financial upside, ownership, access to future returns, or guaranteed future utility.

If the Patron Gift Door is presented on the same page as Membership Access, the page must state that gifts are separate from access purchases. A person must be able to understand whether they are buying current utility, making a gift, or doing both in separate line items.

---

## 4. Standing Utility Rule

Cora Nation may only sell member access to utility that is standing, scheduled, or otherwise presently deliverable. Future ecosystem growth may happen, and members may benefit from a richer community over time, but that future growth is not the thing being sold.

The following claims are prohibited in Patron Engine terms, sales pages, onboarding, receipts, and member communications:

- that membership will appreciate in value;
- that member contributions will generate profit, yield, dividends, income, upside, passive income, or return on investment;
- that gifts or access payments will be pooled to generate financial returns for members;
- that a member will receive equity, governance control of assets, liquidation proceeds, revenue share, or ownership in Cora Nation, Sunheart Private Trust, any Brand LLC, or any future vehicle;
- that a member is buying early access to a future security, token, pool, instrument, company, or appreciating asset.

Allowed language may describe present benefits, private association participation, mission support, gratitude, community belonging, and the fact that the store of offerings may grow without promising that growth as consideration.

## 5. Member Rights

Subject to the PMA Membership Agreement, these terms, capacity, safety rules, and the specific tier or access item purchased, a Patron Engine member in good standing may receive:

1. **Private community participation:** access to private member spaces, gatherings, circles, ceremonies, learning rooms, or calls that are open to the member's tier or access item.
2. **Standing utility:** the specific event, stay, session, tool access, or experience identified at checkout or in the member's written access summary.
3. **Patron acknowledgment:** non-financial gratitude, recognition, or story acknowledgment, when the member consents and when acknowledgment does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, investment, or control.
4. **Records access:** a clear record of the member's own payments, gifts, access items, attendance, fulfillment status, and membership standing.
5. **Privacy and confidentiality:** treatment of member information under the PMA privacy rules and any applicable records protocol.
6. **Withdrawal:** the right to withdraw from membership under the PMA Membership Agreement and these terms.
7. **Internal dispute process:** access to the internal PMA dispute and restoration process before external escalation, subject to the civil-rights and counsel-access carve-outs in the PMA Membership Agreement.

No Patron Engine right is a right to money, profit, equity, assets, securities, token appreciation, or return of patronage.

---

## 6. Member Duties

A Patron Engine member agrees to:

1. remain in good standing under the PMA Membership Agreement;
2. use the Patron Engine as private association participation, not as an investment program;
3. avoid representing Patron Engine access as a financial opportunity, security, ownership stake, passive income path, or guaranteed future benefit;
4. keep private member communications confidential unless marked public or approved for sharing;
5. honor event, stay, tool, retreat, and community safety rules;
6. provide accurate identity and contact information for membership records;
7. disclose conflicts if the member enters a steward, council, vendor, sponsor, or compensated role;
8. comply with payment, refund, cancellation, and transfer limits stated for the specific access item.

---

## 7. Patronage Flow

Each Patron Engine transaction should be recorded in a member-access ledger with at least:

- member identity and membership status;
- door used: `membership_access`, `patron_gift`, or separate line items for both;
- access item or gift purpose;
- amount, date, payment method reference, and receipt id;
- fulfillment status for any access item;
- any non-financial acknowledgment consent;
- refund, cancellation, credit, or transfer status;
- steward responsible for fulfillment.

The flow is:

1. **Member enters or confirms PMA standing.**
2. **Member chooses a door.** Access purchase, patron gift, or both as separate line items.
3. **Member receives written terms.** The checkout or invoice states what is being bought now, what is a gift, and what is not promised.
4. **Cora Nation fulfills standing utility.** The member receives the event, stay, session, access, tool, or community participation sold.
5. **Surplus supports the mission.** After costs, resources may support more offerings, operations, art, village, tools, or community infrastructure, but no member has a claim on that surplus.
6. **Records close the loop.** The ledger tracks fulfillment and any issue, refund, cancellation, or pastoral dispute.

Patronage records are membership records only. They are not capital accounts, equity ledgers, profit ledgers, loan ledgers, securities registers, token balances, or redemption obligations.

---

## 8. Payments, Refunds, Cancellations, and Transfers

Specific access items may define their own cancellation windows, refund rules, capacity limits, and transfer rules. Unless a specific written access item says otherwise:

- access fees are paid for named current utility;
- unused access may be rescheduled or credited only at Cora Nation's discretion and within capacity;
- patron gifts are not refundable except in cases of processing error, duplicate charge, fraud, or a written refund commitment approved by a steward;
- member access is personal and may not be sold, assigned, pledged, traded, or transferred for value;
- no refund, credit, or cancellation rule creates any right to profit, appreciation, interest, penalty yield, or future upside.

If a member withdraws or is removed for cause, future access may be cancelled according to the applicable access-item terms and PMA discipline procedures. Withdrawal does not create a right to any share of community assets, patron gifts, surplus, goodwill, intellectual property, or future revenue.

## 9. No Equity, No Return, No Securities

The member expressly acknowledges and agrees:

1. Patron Engine membership does not grant ownership, equity, stock, units, partnership interest, trust beneficial interest, revenue share, profit share, liquidation right, voting control over assets, or management authority in Cora Nation, Sunheart Private Trust, any Brand LLC, or any related project.
2. Patron Engine payments and gifts do not create a debt, loan, deposit, investment contract, security, commodity interest, managed account, pooled investment, franchise, or brokered transaction.
3. No member should expect profit, appreciation, income, yield, dividends, capital gains, token upside, or return on investment from membership, access payments, patron gifts, or future community growth.
4. Any future entity, Brand LLC, token, fund, trust, cooperative, or securities offering would require separate counsel-approved documents and cannot be implied by these terms.
5. The member has not relied on any statement promising financial return, asset ownership, investment performance, or future appreciation.

These clauses survive withdrawal, termination, refund, cancellation, and amendment.

---

## 10. Brand LLC and Third-Party Separation

Cora Nation membership is separate from any Brand LLC, vendor, retreat operator, software provider, property host, artist, or third-party service. A member may separately buy from, work for, donate to, invest in, or contract with another entity only under that entity's own documents.

Patron Engine access must not be used to imply that PMA membership grants rights in a Brand LLC or that payments to Cora Nation are investments in any operating company.

---

## 11. Communications Rules

Patron Engine copy, onboarding, receipts, and steward scripts must:

- name the standing utility being offered now;
- distinguish access purchases from gifts;
- avoid return-promising, ownership, securities, or passive-income framing;
- avoid "compounding utility value" as a sold benefit;
- state that future growth is mission support, not member consideration;
- route legal, tax, securities, charitable-deduction, and investment questions to qualified professionals;
- keep public copy draft-tagged until James and counsel approve it.

Suggested safe phrasing:

> "Your membership access supports the mission and gives you the specific current access described here. Future offerings may grow as the community grows, but no financial return, ownership, or future benefit is promised."

## 12. Member Records, Privacy, and Review

Cora Nation should preserve member access records, patron gift records, fulfillment records, acknowledgments, disputes, refunds, and withdrawal notices in a private membership record system. Access to records is limited to the member, authorized stewards, counsel, auditors, and others permitted by PMA policy or law.

Before Patron Engine launch, the following must be reviewed:

1. this terms draft;
2. the checkout/invoice language;
3. receipts and acknowledgment language;
4. refund/cancellation terms for each access item;
5. the member-access ledger schema;
6. public or semi-public page copy;
7. any donation or tax-deductibility representation;
8. any tier name that could imply rank, investment priority, asset claim, or financial upside.
