## SECTION 1: GENERAL TRADING RISKS

### 1.1 Market Risk

Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile. Prices can:
- Move rapidly in either direction
- Experience sudden crashes or spikes
- Be influenced by news, regulation, or market manipulation
- Behave unpredictably, even for experienced traders

**RISK:** You may lose a significant portion or ALL of your trading capital due to market movements.

### 1.2 Volatility Risk

Cryptocurrency volatility far exceeds traditional markets:
- Daily price swings of 10-20% are common
- Flash crashes can occur within minutes
- Overnight gaps can result in significant losses
- Volatility can increase without warning

**RISK:** High volatility means rapid losses are possible.

### 1.3 Leverage Risk

If you use leverage (borrowed funds):
- Gains are amplified
- BUT losses are also amplified
- Losses can EXCEED your initial capital
- Liquidation can occur rapidly

**RISK:** With leverage, you can lose MORE than you deposited.

### 1.4 Liquidity Risk

In certain market conditions:
- It may be difficult to exit positions
- Slippage may result in worse execution prices
- Large orders may move the market against you
- Some assets may become illiquid

**RISK:** You may not be able to exit positions at desired prices.

## SECTION 2: TECHNOLOGY RISKS

### 2.1 Software Risk

Aria is automated trading software that:
- May contain bugs or errors
- May malfunction or crash
- May execute trades incorrectly
- May fail to execute trades when needed

**RISK:** Software failures can result in unexpected losses.

### 2.2 Connectivity Risk

Trading depends on:
- Internet connectivity
- API connections to exchanges
- Server uptime
- Network stability

Any of these can fail, resulting in:
- Missed trades
- Delayed execution
- Inability to close positions
- Stop-losses not triggering

**RISK:** Connectivity issues can prevent proper trade execution.

### 2.3 Exchange Risk

Third-party exchanges:
- May experience outages
- May have API failures or rate limits
- May reject orders
- May have security breaches
- May become insolvent

**RISK:** Exchange problems can result in losses or inability to trade.

### 2.4 Cybersecurity Risk

Despite security measures:
- API keys could be compromised
- Systems could be hacked
- Unauthorized trades could occur
- Data breaches could occur

**RISK:** Security breaches can result in total loss of funds.

## SECTION 3: STRATEGY RISKS

### 3.1 Strategy Performance Risk

Trading strategies:
- May perform poorly in certain market conditions
- May have extended losing periods (drawdowns)
- May fail to adapt to changing markets
- May not perform as historically backtested

**RISK:** Strategies may lose money, even if historically profitable.

### 3.2 Backtesting Limitations

Historical backtests:
- Do NOT guarantee future performance
- May be overfit to past data
- Cannot account for all market conditions
- May not include realistic execution costs

**RISK:** Past performance is NOT indicative of future results.

### 3.3 Model Risk

AI and algorithmic models:
- Are based on assumptions that may be wrong
- May not capture all relevant factors
- May fail in unprecedented market conditions
- May make systematic errors

**RISK:** Model failures can result in significant losses.

### 3.4 Regime Change Risk

Markets change over time:
- Strategies that worked may stop working
- Market structure can shift
- Correlations can change
- New market participants change dynamics

**RISK:** Strategies may become unprofitable as markets evolve.

### 5.1 Co-Investment Risk

In the pooled fund:
- Your capital is combined with others
- You share in collective gains AND losses
- Other participants' actions affect you
- Large redemptions can impact performance

**RISK:** Pooled structure introduces shared risk.

### 5.2 Redemption Risk

Pooled fund redemptions:
- Are processed quarterly only
- Require 30-day advance notice
- May be limited if liquidity is constrained
- May be suspended in extreme conditions

**RISK:** You may not be able to withdraw when you want.

### 5.3 Valuation Risk

Pool valuations:
- Are calculated periodically
- May not reflect real-time values
- Depend on exchange pricing
- Could contain errors

**RISK:** Your pro-rata share may differ from actual value.

### 5.4 Concentration Risk

The pool may:
- Hold concentrated positions
- Have correlated exposures
- Be exposed to single-asset risk

**RISK:** Concentration can amplify losses.
