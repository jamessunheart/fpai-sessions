# Entity Trading Addendum

**CORA NATION PRIVATE MEMBERSHIP ASSOCIATION**  
**STEWARDSHIP MINISTRY - ENTITY TRADING SERVICES**

**Addendum Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** December 26, 2025  
**Document Status:** Active

---

## PREAMBLE

This Entity Trading Addendum ("Addendum") supplements the Cora Nation PMA Membership Agreement and extends trading service access to authorized entities (Trusts, LLCs, and Churches) operating within the Full Potential ecosystem.

This Addendum recognizes that spiritual communities, ministry organizations, and family offices may benefit from conscious wealth stewardship education and tools, and provides a framework for such entities to participate in trading services.

---

## ARTICLE I - DEFINITIONS

### Section 1.1 - Entity Types

For purposes of this Addendum:

**"Trust"** means an irrevocable private trust established for lawful purposes, with a designated trustee authorized to act on behalf of the trust beneficiaries.

**"LLC"** means a Limited Liability Company formed under state law and operating for lawful purposes within the Full Potential ecosystem.

**"Church"** means a religious organization recognized under 508(c)(1)(a) or 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or a ministry arm thereof.

**"Entity Account"** means a trading account established for an Entity rather than an individual Member.

### Section 1.2 - Authorized Representatives

**"Primary Administrator"** means the individual Member designated as the primary authorized representative for an Entity Account, responsible for account management, API key custody, and user authorization.

**"Authorized User"** means any Member granted permission by the Primary Administrator to execute trades and manage positions on behalf of the Entity.

---

## ARTICLE II - ENTITY ACCOUNT ESTABLISHMENT

### Section 2.1 - Eligibility Requirements

To establish an Entity Account, the following must be satisfied:

1. **Entity Verification:** Documentary proof of entity existence (trust deed, articles of organization, church charter, etc.)
2. **PMA Membership:** The Primary Administrator must be an active Cora Nation PMA Member in good standing
3. **Trading Addendum:** The Primary Administrator must have signed the individual Trading Addendum
4. **Authority Documentation:** Proof of Primary Administrator's authority to act on behalf of the Entity

### Section 2.2 - Account Limits

Entity accounts are subject to the following limits based on entity type:

| Entity Type | Daily Trade Limit (UC) | Max Position Size | Can Provide Liquidity |
|-------------|------------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Trust | 50,000 UC | 40% of balance | Yes |
| LLC | 25,000 UC | 35% of balance | Yes |
| Church | 100,000 UC | 50% of balance | Yes |

These limits are established for prudent stewardship and may be adjusted by the Stewardship Ministry with reasonable notice.

---

## ARTICLE III - GOVERNANCE AND AUTHORIZATION

### Section 3.1 - Primary Administrator Responsibilities

The Primary Administrator shall:

1. Maintain custody of all API keys and credentials
2. Authorize and de-authorize users as appropriate
3. Ensure all authorized users have signed individual PMA Membership Agreement and Trading Addendum
4. Monitor entity account activity
5. Report any unauthorized access immediately
6. Maintain accurate records of authorization decisions

### Section 3.2 - Multi-User Authorization

The Primary Administrator may authorize additional Members to trade on behalf of the Entity. Each Authorized User:

1. Must be an active PMA Member in good standing
2. Must have signed individual Trading Addendum
3. Acts as agent of the Entity, not the Primary Administrator personally
4. Is subject to removal at Primary Administrator's discretion

### Section 3.3 - Trading Authority Limits

Individual Authorized Users may be subject to trading authority limits set by the Primary Administrator, including:

- Maximum position sizes
- Permitted trading pairs/symbols
- Time-based restrictions
- Approval requirements for large trades

Such limits are the responsibility of the Entity and Primary Administrator to establish and enforce.

1. All trading decisions made on behalf of the Entity

1. Trading activities conducted through the Entity Account
2. Actions of Authorized Users
3. Disputes between the Entity and its beneficiaries/members
4. Tax matters related to trading profits or losses

## ARTICLE V - FEES AND COMMONS CONTRIBUTION

### Section 5.1 - Fee Structure

Entity Accounts are subject to the same fee structure as individual accounts:

**Tier 1 (Steward):** Subscription fee of 50-200 UC per month  
**Tier 2 (Trustee):** Performance fee of 10% of gains in UC

### Section 5.2 - Commons Contribution

30% of all Entity trading fees flow to the Commons Reserve Fund, supporting ministry programs and member needs.

### Section 5.3 - TRUST Earning

Entity trading activity contributes to the TRUST scores of:

1. The Entity (if applicable)
2. The Primary Administrator
3. Authorized Users (proportionally to activity)

TRUST earned through entity trading supports eligibility for ministry benefits.

---

## ARTICLE VI - API KEY SECURITY

### Section 6.1 - Key Custody

The Primary Administrator is the sole custodian of Entity API keys. Keys may NOT be:

1. Shared with non-authorized individuals
2. Stored in unsecured locations
3. Transmitted via unsecured channels
4. Used for purposes outside this agreement

### Section 6.2 - Key Rotation

Entity accounts should rotate API keys:

1. When an Authorized User is removed
2. Upon any suspected compromise
3. At least quarterly for best practices

### Section 6.3 - Breach Notification

Any actual or suspected key compromise must be reported to Stewardship Ministry within 24 hours.

---

## ARTICLE VII - TERMINATION

### Section 7.1 - Voluntary Termination

An Entity may terminate its trading account by:

1. Closing all open positions
2. Withdrawing all funds
3. Providing written notice to Stewardship Ministry
4. Ensuring all fees are paid

### Section 7.2 - Involuntary Termination

Stewardship Ministry may terminate an Entity Account for:

1. Breach of this Addendum
2. Fraud or misrepresentation
3. Failure to maintain required documentation
4. Failure to pay fees
5. Conduct harmful to the ministry or membership

### Section 7.3 - Effect of Termination

Upon termination:

1. All trading activity must cease immediately
2. Open positions should be closed within 24 hours
3. Remaining funds remain with the Entity
4. TRUST scores are not affected
5. This Addendum survives regarding indemnification obligations
