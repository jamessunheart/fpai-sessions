## ARTICLE III: OWNERSHIP AND UNITS

### Section 3.1 - Unit Calculation

Your ownership is represented by Units:
- Units = Contribution / NAV per Unit at contribution time
- NAV (Net Asset Value) = Total Pool Value / Total Units
- Units are fractional and divisible

### Section 3.2 - Example

```
Pool Value: $100,000
Total Units: 10,000
NAV per Unit: $10.00

Your Contribution: $5,000
Your Units: 5,000 / 10 = 500 Units
Your Ownership: 500 / 10,500 = 4.76%
```

### Section 3.3 - Unit Value Fluctuation

- Unit value rises and falls with Pool performance
- No guarantee of maintaining or increasing value
- You may receive back less than you contributed

### Section 3.4 - Ownership Records

- Units are tracked in ministry systems
- Quarterly statements provided
- You may request unit balance at any time

## ARTICLE V: VALUATION

### Section 5.1 - Valuation Frequency

Pool NAV is calculated:
- Daily (for informational purposes)
- Quarterly (for official statements)
- At contribution/redemption events

### Section 5.2 - Valuation Methodology

NAV = (Total Assets - Liabilities - Fees) / Total Units

Assets valued at:
- Last available exchange price
- Mark-to-market for open positions
- Fair value estimates if market data unavailable

### Section 5.3 - Valuation Disputes

If you dispute a valuation:
1. Submit written inquiry within 10 days
2. Council reviews and responds within 20 days
3. Council determination is final

## ARTICLE VI: FEES

### Section 6.1 - Performance Fee

- **Rate:** 10% of gains
- **Calculation:** Quarterly
- **Basis:** New high water mark

### Section 6.2 - High Water Mark

- Performance fee only on gains above previous high
- If Pool loses value, no fee until value recovers
- Resets if you fully redeem and re-contribute

### Section 6.3 - Example

```
Quarter 1: Pool grows from $100 to $120
Gain: $20
Fee: $20 × 10% = $2

Quarter 2: Pool drops from $120 to $110
Gain: $0 (below high water mark of $120)
Fee: $0

Quarter 3: Pool grows from $110 to $130
New gain above HWM: $130 - $120 = $10
Fee: $10 × 10% = $1
```

### Section 6.4 - Fee Payment

- Fees deducted from Pool
- Paid in UC credits to Stewardship Ministry
- Reduces your unit value proportionally

### Section 6.5 - No Management Fee

- No fixed management fee
- Performance fee only
- Trading costs (exchange fees) borne by Pool

## ARTICLE VII: REDEMPTIONS

### Section 7.1 - Redemption Windows

Redemptions are processed:
- **Quarterly** (end of March, June, September, December)
- With **30 days advance notice** required

### Section 7.2 - Redemption Process

1. Submit redemption request via dashboard
2. At least 30 days before quarter end
3. Pool calculates your redemption value at quarter end NAV
4. Proceeds distributed within 10 business days of quarter end

### Section 7.3 - Partial Redemptions

- You may redeem any number of units
- Subject to minimum remaining balance (if applicable)
- Proportionally reduces your ownership

### Section 7.4 - Full Redemptions

- You may redeem all units
- Removes you from Pool participation
- May re-contribute subject to normal process

### Section 7.5 - Redemption Form

Redemption proceeds paid as:
- USDC to your specified wallet address
- Or re-contribution to your Tier 1 account

### Section 7.6 - Redemption Limits

In extraordinary circumstances:
- Redemptions may be limited to [X]% of Pool per quarter
- Pro-rata reduction if requests exceed limit
- Unfilled requests queue to next quarter

### Section 7.7 - Suspension of Redemptions

Redemptions may be suspended if:
- Market conditions make valuation unreliable
- Liquidity is insufficient
- Legal/regulatory events require it
- Ministry Council determines suspension necessary

Suspension limited to 90 days unless extended by Council.
