# ZEND UC CREDITS — SPECIFICATION

**Version:** v1.0  
**Component:** ZEND Wallet Core  
**Status:** Build-ready  

---

## 1. Purpose

ZEND UC Credits are the internal usage credit system that powers ease, delegation, rewards, and experience inside ZEND MONEY.

They exist to:
- Reduce friction
- Enable auto-delegation (“Send for Me”)
- Redistribute value
- Reward participation
- Lift the psychological burden of sending money

**UC Credits are not money.**

---

## 2. Definition

**UC = Usage Credits**

ZEND UC Credits represent prepaid service capacity + privileges inside the ZEND ecosystem.

They function as:
- Permission
- Priority
- Fuel for ease

---

## 3. What UC Credits Are / Are Not

### UC Credits ARE
- Prepaid service balance
- Non-financial rewards container
- Access unlock mechanism
- Delegation enabler
- Experience gateway

### UC Credits ARE NOT
- Cash
- Stored monetary value
- Yield-bearing
- Investment product
- Transferable currency
- Redeemable for fiat

---

## 4. Core Functions

ZEND UC Credits power the following:

### 4.1 Send for Me (Auto-Delegation)
- Higher UC balance → fewer confirmation steps
- Faster approvals
- Lighter cognitive load

### 4.2 Sponsored / Free Sends
- UC Credits offset or eliminate send costs
- Ads/sponsorships convert into UC

### 4.3 Priority Handling
- AI first
- Human concierge when needed
- High-value or sensitive sends escalate automatically

### 4.4 Rewards & Experiences
- Meditation partner invites
- Raffle entries (e.g. Zen Village)
- Exclusive moments
- Access-based perks (never discounts)

---

## 5. Accrual Logic

UC Credits increase through:
- User preload
- Platform rewards
- Sponsored activity
- Fee redistribution
- Promotional grants

**Important rule:** UC Credits may increase in capacity and privilege, but never promise financial appreciation.

---

## 6. Consumption Logic

UC Credits are:
- Consumed when services are used
- Boosted when rewards apply
- Never automatically converted to money
- Never moved outside ZEND

Consumption is transparent and logged.

---

## 7. Fee Redistribution Rule

Any fees generated within ZEND:
- Infrastructure costs deducted
- Remaining value converted into UC Credits
- UC Credits redistributed as:
  - Sponsored sends
  - Rewards
  - Experiences
  - Priority access

Fees do not extract. They circulate.

---

## 8. Security & Trust Model

UC Credits contribute to security by:
- Enabling delegation without custody
- Reducing rushed decision-making
- Supporting intent-based confirmation
- Triggering human review when thresholds are crossed

The interface is the security. UC Credits earn trust over time.

## 10. Legal & Compliance Position

UC Credits are legally positioned as:
- Prepaid service credits
- Comparable to:
  - Cloud credits
  - Concierge retainers
  - Loyalty points
  - Usage tokens

ZEND:
- Does not custody funds
- Does not transmit money
- Does not promise returns
- Does not create stored monetary value

Money remains peer-to-peer. UC remains internal.

---

## 11. Design Principles (Non-Negotiable)

- Calm over control
- Clarity over complexity
- Permission over friction
- Experience over extraction

UC Credits should feel like relief, not accounting.

---

## 12. One-Paragraph Summary

ZEND UC Credits are the internal fuel that makes ZEND MONEY effortless. They enable delegation, reduce friction, redistribute value, and unlock experiences—without ever becoming money themselves. UC Credits keep money movement fast on the outside, while ease, trust, and reward live on the inside.
