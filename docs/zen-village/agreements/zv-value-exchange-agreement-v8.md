## Preamble

Zen Village is a 45-acre retreat and wellness center in Chirripó, Costa Rica, operating as a node of CORA Nation (a 508(c)(1)(A) faith-based organization).

This is **value-for-value exchange** — transparent, mutual, witnessed circulation of gifts. Not employment. Not charity. Sacred reciprocity made operational.

The cycle: **Receive → Commit → Prove → Witness → Seal → Circulate**

---

## Section 1 — About CORA Nation & The Two Ledgers

**CORA** is a community of people building post-extraction economies. **CORA Nation** is the broader faith-based organization holding this network together. Zen Village is one node within it.

Two ledgers operate within this system:

**Zen Village Work Credits (ZWC)** — labor-based credit earned through witnessed hours at Zen Village. Used to settle Zen Village invoices. Local to Zen Village. Only approved volunteers and stewards can earn or hold ZWC.

**CORA Credits** — performance-based currency of the broader CORA Nation network. Earned through priorities, excellence, peer services, and cash purchase. Used for premium experiences across the network.

Neither ledger is cashable by Zen Village or CORA Nation. Members may privately trade either at any negotiated rate, including for cash, in arrangements where Zen Village is not a party.

## Section 2 — The Volunteer Bargain

### The System in 4 Lines

1. **Zen Village assigns you a tier** (Shared/Glamping or Private Room)
2. **Work 20 hours/week + post daily proof of work** → $400 ZWC applied to your invoice
3. **Deliver approved priorities** → ZV may award a bonus (ZWC to close gap, or CORA for premium)
4. **If short on hours or priorities** → cover gap in $ or CORA from wallet

### Tier Assignment

Tiers are **assigned by Zen Village**, not selected by the volunteer. Considerations include:
- Current availability of spaces
- Volunteer's track record and tenure
- Match between volunteer capabilities and village needs

New volunteers typically begin in Shared / Glamping. Private Room access is earned through sustained delivery over time and granted at the discretion of stewards.

## Section 4 — The Credit Mechanic

### Hours (Guaranteed)

**Contribution credit rate: $20 ZWC per hour.**
**Weekly commitment: 20 hours of witnessed organized work.**
**Result: $400 ZWC applied directly to your invoice each week.**

Hours must include:
- Daily proof of work (WhatsApp check-ins or equivalent)
- Witnessed or substantiated through verifiable proof
- Aligned with weekly priorities or steward-directed needs

ZWC beyond 20 hours/week is not standard. Discretionary overage may be available when ZV has specific needs (events, surge projects). This is at steward discretion, not guaranteed.

### Priorities & Bonuses (Discretionary)

Each Monday, you and your witness co-set 3 weekly priorities (P1, P2, P3). Each Sunday Seal, witness reviews delivery.

When approved priorities are completed with strong proof, **Zen Village may award a bonus** in one of two forms:

1. **ZWC bonus** — closes any remaining invoice gap (typical for Private Room volunteers who delivered well)
2. **CORA bonus** — credited to your wallet for premium experiences (typical for Shared volunteers, or extra for Private after gap closed)

**Bonuses are explicitly discretionary.** Strong consistent delivery typically results in bonus awards. Weak delivery, missed priorities, or shortcuts in proof may result in no bonus. ZV does not commit to fixed bonus amounts — the system rewards actual contribution as judged by ZV at the Sunday Seal.

### Gap Coverage (If Bonuses Don't Fully Cover)

When hours fall short of 20, or priorities aren't delivered, OR ZV doesn't award sufficient bonus to close the invoice gap, the remainder is covered by:
- **Cash** (USD or colones equivalent)
- **CORA from your wallet**
- **Any combination** of cash and CORA

Persistent shortfalls signal that the assigned tier may not match current contribution. Stewards may initiate a tier change conversation.

### The Math by Tier

**Shared Room / Glamping ($400 weekly):**
- 20 hrs work → $400 ZWC → invoice fully covered
- Bonuses (when awarded) → CORA only, banks to wallet for premium experiences

**Private Room ($600 weekly):**
- 20 hrs work → $400 ZWC → $200 invoice gap remains
- Strong priority delivery → ZV typically awards $200 ZWC bonus to close gap
- Additional CORA bonus possible on top, banked to wallet
- Weak delivery → gap paid in cash or CORA from wallet

## Section 6 — Approved Priorities & Daily Proof Protocol

### Setting Priorities

Each Monday's setting includes:
- 3 specific weekly priorities (P1, P2, P3)
- Witness co-approval — priorities must be aligned with ZV needs
- Time estimates for completion
- Expected proof format (deliverable, witnessed outcome, etc.)

### Daily Proof Protocol — The Gate for ZWC

**ZWC is earned only against verified daily proof of work.** Hours claimed without proof do not count.

Each work day (Monday through Saturday, unless otherwise scheduled with the witness), the volunteer posts to the designated WhatsApp channel:

1. **Top work priority / purpose** for that day — what the volunteer is focused on accomplishing
2. **Time frame worked** — start and stop times, or total hours, on the priority
3. **Progress made** — what specifically advanced during the work
4. **Before / after photos** when applicable — especially for physical work (builds, repairs, land work, cleanups, organizing, gardening, animal care, etc.)

For non-physical work (writing, design, code, planning), photos may be replaced by:
- Screenshots of work completed
- Links to deliverables (docs, drafts, edits)
- A clear description of what was produced

**The witness reviews daily posts.** Verified hours accumulate through the week. Sunday Seal tallies total verified hours and applies ZWC to the weekly invoice (up to the $400 maximum for 20 verified hours).

**Missed days are zero credit, not retroactive.** If a volunteer fails to post for a day, those hours do not credit to ZWC — even if the work was actually done. The proof is the credit mechanism. Persistent missing posts trigger a steward conversation about tier fit.

### Approval Standards

ZV maintains discretion on what qualifies for bonus:
- Was the priority completed as defined?
- Was the proof strong (deliverable, witnessed, verifiable)?
- Did the work contribute meaningfully to village outcomes?

Approval is the witness/steward's call, with founding steward review for significant judgments.

## Section 7 — CORA Credits (Detail)

### What CORA Is

CORA Credits are internal currency of the broader CORA Nation ecosystem. Issued through Zen Village (and other nodes) for performance bonuses, peer services, cash purchase, and certain ecosystem contributions.

**Exchange rate: 1 CORA = $1** of value inside the CORA Nation ecosystem.

### How CORA Is Earned

| Path | Notes |
|---|---|
| Priority/excellence bonus | Awarded at ZV discretion based on actual contribution |
| House-wide good standing | Small bonus when common areas kept well |
| Peer service offered | Member-to-member, at negotiated rate |
| Service to paying guest | Steward-facilitated, 70% to provider |
| Cash purchase | $1 = 1 CORA (anyone) |
| Future venture donation realized | 1:1 with cash donated |

### How CORA Is Spent

| Use | Notes |
|---|---|
| Pay invoice gap | 1 CORA = $1 |
| Pay another member for a service | At their posted rate |
| Sauna add-on sessions | Beyond included weekly access |
| Future ZV stay nights | 75 CORA = 1 night |
| Sparking sessions | When available, ~150 CORA |
| Coherence cohort discount | 400 CORA → $400 off |
| Premium foods, smoothies, etc. | Beyond standard meals |
| Transfer to another member | Any amount, any reason |
| Cross-node services | Other CORA Nation offerings as available |

### What ZV / CORA Nation Will Not Do

- Convert CORA Credits to cash
- Guarantee cash value for CORA Credits
- Operate any currency exchange
- Pay CORA out as wages or any cash distribution

### Peer-to-Peer Transfers

Members may privately transfer CORA Credits between wallets for any purpose, at any rate they negotiate. This includes selling CORA to other members for cash (private arrangement). Zen Village provides wallet infrastructure but is not party to cash-side arrangements. Members handle their own agreements and tax implications.

All wallet-to-wallet transfers are logged. Transfers above 500 CORA in a single transaction are flagged for steward visibility (awareness only, not approval).

### Wallet Expiry

CORA Credits inactive in a wallet for 24 months may be reclaimed by the Community Fund.

## Section 11 — Sales Commissions, Content Production & Branded Use

The principle: **ZWC pays for the work · CORA pays for the result.** Time spent producing content already counts toward the 20-hour standard (ZWC offset on the weekly invoice). Additional CORA flows only when content, outreach, or production produces real outcomes that move Zen Village toward sustainability.

Three earning paths, ranked by impact:

### 11.1 — Sales Commissions (Real Revenue Moved)

The volunteer who traceably drives a sale of any ZV or CORA Nation product earns a commission. The volunteer chooses payment form at time of issuance:

- **15% in cash** (paid via standard ZV cash processes), OR
- **20% in CORA Credits** (higher percentage reflects the value of keeping credits inside the ecosystem)

**Products with commissions enabled:**

| Product | Volunteer earns if traceably driven |
|---|---|
| Camp Zen Booking ($400–$3000) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Full Potential Retreat ($1999) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Sparking session ($1500) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Paid coaching ($500–$2000) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Coherence Course *(structure locked; activates when course goes live)* | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Any other ZV / CORA Nation product | 15% cash / 20% CORA |

**Traceable attribution required.** A commission is paid only when the sale can be clearly attributed to the volunteer through one of:
- A unique referral link or voucher code
- A logged direct conversation or introduction
- Direct involvement in the sales process documented by ZV

Commissions can stack — a single piece of content or relationship can drive multiple sales, and each verified sale earns its own commission.

Disputed attribution is decided by the founding steward.

### 11.2 — Verified Traction Bonus

Content with measurable reach earns a CORA bonus once verified via platform analytics 30 days post-publication. Pre-approved content only.

| Reach (verified) | CORA |
|---|---|
| 25k+ verified views on a single piece | 150 |
| 5k–25k verified views | 75 |
| 1k–5k verified views/reach | 25 |

Reach below 1k earns no traction bonus regardless of effort invested.

### 11.3 — Use in Branded Products

Content selected by Zen Village for incorporation into actual products (The Show, marketing materials, sales decks).

| Use | CORA |
|---|---|
| Full Show episode contribution (major creative role) | 250 |
| Scene / segment used in The Show | 200 |
| Show Reel approved for marketing use | 150 |
| Content used in team marketing / sales materials | 25 |

Show content requires founding steward sign-off in addition to standard approval.

### 11.4 — What Does Not Earn CORA

This list is explicit because the previous framework created gaming risk. CORA earnings are reserved for real impact. The following do not earn additional CORA beyond standard ZWC for work hours:

- Content posted without concept pre-approval
- Long-form thought leadership without verified traction or a driven sale
- Random social posts not part of a pre-approved campaign
- Personal content tagged with ZV without ZV approval
- Content without subject consent
- Below-quality work as judged by the content steward
- Bringing in new volunteers (volunteers are an operational cost, not revenue — only sales and revenue drive commission CORA)
- Routine documentation or activity without verifiable impact

### 11.5 — Approval Flow

All content production follows this sequence:

1. **Concept pre-approval** with content steward — confirms topic, format, brand fit, subject consent, and which earning tier(s) the work could reach
2. **Production check-ins** for larger pieces
3. **Subject consent verified** for anyone appearing in content (Section 13 — Media & Consent applies)
4. **Publication** with attribution tracking where applicable
5. **30-day verification window** for traction bonuses (Tier 2) and ongoing tracking for commissions (Tier 1)
6. **CORA issued** to volunteer's wallet as outcomes verify; commission cash issued through standard payout cycle

### 11.6 — Rights & Attribution

- Zen Village owns ecosystem usage rights for all content produced through this exchange
- Creator retains full attribution in any published version
- Creator retains portfolio rights with founding steward permission
- External commercial use by the creator (beyond portfolio) requires separate licensing agreement with Zen Village

### 11.7 — Stacking Across Tiers

A single piece of content or outreach effort may earn across multiple tiers. Each tier verifies and pays independently.

*Example:* A volunteer-produced video that ZV approves, is selected for marketing materials (25 CORA, Tier 3), reaches 5k–25k verified views (75 CORA, Tier 2), and drives one Full Potential Retreat sale (15% of $1999 = $300 cash OR 20% = 400 CORA, Tier 1).

Total earnings: $300 + 100 CORA · OR 500 CORA all-in (volunteer's choice of payment form on commission).

The system explicitly does not reward activity without impact; it explicitly does reward impact that compounds.

## Section 12 — Community Standards & Restoration

You agree to:
- Honor the land, the practices, and the people of Zen Village
- Engage in good faith with the priorities and proof system
- Respect the privacy and integrity of fellow participants
- Refrain from harmful substances on the land (alcohol in moderation per house norms; no recreational drugs unless part of a ceremonial container explicitly approved by stewards)
- Repair what you break, restore what you disrupt

### Restoration Fees (in CORA)

| Slip | Fee |
|---|---|
| Dishes / plates left out | 2 CORA each |
| Wearing someone's clothes without permission | 10 CORA / item |
| Same, returned damaged | 25 CORA + replacement cost |
| Taking communal item into private space | 10 CORA / day until returned |
| Vehicle returned dirty | 10 CORA |
| Breaking a communal item | Replacement cost (cash if repurchased) |

If a volunteer has no CORA balance, restoration fees may be paid in cash or worked off through additional hours at steward discretion.

**Common areas:** No individual fines. House-wide good standing at Sunday Seal earns each housemate a small CORA bonus. Repeated breach of communal trust = tier change or relationship conversation.

## Section 14 — Exit Conditions

This agreement is at-will. Either party may terminate with reasonable notice (typically 1 week, longer for resident stewards).

**Upon exit:**
- All earned ZWC remains in your wallet for 12 months (then reclaimed by Community Fund)
- All earned CORA remains in your wallet for 24 months (then reclaimed by Community Fund)
- Outstanding fees and invoice balances are due in cash or CORA before departure
- Surplus ZWC may be traded peer-to-peer with other approved volunteers prior to departure
- Surplus CORA travels with you across the CORA Nation network

## Section 15 — Volunteer vs. Local Paid Workers

The contribution credit rate ($20/hour) and CORA system in this agreement apply to **volunteers and stewards engaged in faith-based community service** under CORA Nation governance.

Local paid staff at Zen Village work under **separate cash-wage employment terms** in colones, distinct from this agreement. They are not part of this agreement, though may receive symbolic CORA Credit recognition.

These are two different economic relationships operating through different mechanisms.
