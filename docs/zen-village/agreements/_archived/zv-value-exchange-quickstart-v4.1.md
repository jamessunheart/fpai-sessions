## About CORA

**CORA** is a community of people building post-extraction economies — places, practices, and businesses where value circulates instead of leaking out. **CORA Nation** is the broader faith-based organization that holds this network together (508(c)(1)(A)).

**Zen Village is one node within CORA Nation.** Other nodes are coming online over time — service businesses, courses, retreats, and communities all running on shared principles.

**CORA Credits are the currency of this network.** They are:
- Earned through contribution, service, and conscious participation
- Spent on stays, services, goods, and exchanges across the network
- Held in your personal CORA Wallet (1 CORA = $1 inside the ecosystem)
- Not cashable through Zen Village or CORA Nation — but real value because they buy real things across a growing ecosystem

When you earn CORA Credits at Zen Village, you're earning credits in a network — not just a retreat center. The Sacred Trinity behind it: **Community** (witnessing), **Chat** (priorities and proof), **Currency** (CORA, circulating).

## How CORA Credits Work

**1 CORA = $1** of value inside the Zen Village / CORA Nation ecosystem.

**Where CORA flows:**
- INTO your wallet: earned through work, content, excellence, peer service offered, or bought with cash
- OUT of your wallet: paying ZV invoices, member-to-member service exchanges, approved goods/services
- BETWEEN members: peer-to-peer wallet transfers (any purpose, any rate negotiated privately)

**What Zen Village and CORA Nation do not do:**
- ❌ Do not convert CORA back to cash
- ❌ Do not guarantee a cash value for CORA
- ❌ Do not facilitate external currency conversion

**What members can do privately:**
- ✓ Trade CORA between wallets at any rate they negotiate
- ✓ Sell their CORA to other members for cash (private arrangement, between them)
- ✓ Gift CORA to other members
- ✓ Pay each other for services in CORA

Peer-to-peer arrangements are between members. Zen Village provides the wallet infrastructure but is not party to private cash arrangements. Members handle their own private agreements and any tax implications.

## Caveman Clarity — Three Paths

### Path 1 — Default
Show up 4 hrs/day, work organized toward your 3 priorities, witness logs the time. Stay drops to floor.

### Path 2 — Efficient
Hit all 3 priorities early with strong proof. Witness can waive remaining hours. Same floor, less time spent, plus CORA bonuses.

### Path 3 — Content Substitution
Make approved premium content (polished video, podcast, Show Reel). One Show Reel substitutes for ~4 hours of work. Combined with your remaining hours, still hits the floor.

**What you can't do:** go below the floor. The floor is sacred — real costs always show up.

## The Trust Curve

The longer you're here, the more credit can offset.

| Week | Max reduction available |

## If You Make Content

Two paths stack together:

**Time-based:** Hours producing content count toward your 4 hrs/day at $25/hour, same as any priority work.

**Output-based — Premium content earns invoice offset + CORA:**

| Premium Content | Invoice Offset | CORA Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Polished video (60s+, edited) | $25 (= 1 hr work) | 100 |
| Podcast episode (recorded + edited) | $50 (= 2 hrs work) | 150 |
| Content used in The Show (vetted) | $100 (= 4 hrs work) | 200 |

**Standard content — CORA only:**

| Standard Content | CORA Bonus |
|---|---|
| Instagram post / story | 10 |
| Raw footage capture | 25/hour |
| Short edited clip (15–60s) | 40 |
| Photo essay (10+ curated) | 50 |
| Blog post (800+ words) | 75 |

**Key principle:** Premium content offsets HOURS of work — never the floor itself. ZV always recovers floor minimum.

**Approval gate:** Concept pre-approved with your witness or content steward. Final pieces reviewed before bonus issued. Show content claiming invoice offset requires founding steward sign-off.

**Rights:** ZV owns ecosystem usage rights. You get full attribution + portfolio rights with permission. External commercial use requires separate licensing.

**Subject consent:** Anyone appearing in your content must explicitly consent.

## About This vs. Cash Wages

This is **value exchange, not employment.** The $25/hour rate is internal accounting for invoice reduction — not a wage.

Local paid staff at Zen Village work under **separate cash-wage employment terms** in colones, distinct from this agreement. They are not part of the volunteer value-exchange system, though they may receive symbolic CORA Credit recognition.

Two different economic relationships. Don't compare directly.
