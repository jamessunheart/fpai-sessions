## Preamble — The Frame

Zen Village is a 45-acre retreat and wellness center in Chirripó, Costa Rica, operating as a node of CORA Nation under faith-based community governance.

We do not offer **free stays**. We do not offer **labor in exchange for board**.

We offer **value-for-value exchange** — a transparent, mutual, witnessed circulation of gifts.

This agreement makes that exchange visible. It is meant to dignify both sides: you receive real value, and you contribute real value. Nothing is hidden. Nothing is performative.

This is not employment. This is not charity. This is **sacred reciprocity made operational**.

## About CORA

**CORA** is a community of people building post-extraction economies — places, practices, and businesses where value circulates instead of leaking out. **CORA Nation** is the broader faith-based organization (508(c)(1)(A)) that holds this network together.

Zen Village is **one node** within CORA Nation. Other nodes are coming online over time — service businesses, courses, retreats, and aligned communities all running on shared principles.

**CORA Credits are the currency of this network.** They are earned through contribution, service, and conscious participation. They are spent on stays, services, goods, and exchanges across the network. They cannot be cashed out by Zen Village or CORA Nation — but they hold real value because they buy real things across a growing ecosystem.

When you earn CORA Credits at Zen Village, you are earning credits in a network — not just at a retreat center.

The system reflects the **Sacred Trinity** at the heart of CORA Nation:

| Trinity | Expression in This System |
|---|---|
| **Community** | A named witness holds your weekly exchange |
| **Chat** | Priorities and proof organize what's committed and what's delivered |
| **Currency** | CORA Credits — circulating value across the network |

The cycle is simple:

**Receive → Commit → Prove → Witness → Seal → Circulate**

## Section 3 — What You Commit

### The 4-Hour Daily Baseline

Your standard commitment is **up to 4 hours of agreed contribution per day, approximately 5 days per week** (roughly 20 hours per week).

Hours beyond the 4-hour daily baseline require **either**:
1. **Pre-approval** by your witness for a specific scope of work, OR
2. **Exceptional proof at Sunday Seal** demonstrating that the additional time delivered impact worth recognizing.

**Unapproved overage does not generate additional credit.** Approved overage that goes above and beyond is recognized through CORA Credits (not invoice reduction). This protects both sides — you cannot grind to a free stay, and we cannot quietly extract beyond what was agreed.

### Priorities × Proof

Each week, you co-set **three priorities** with your assigned witness:

- **Priority 1 (P1):** The week's most important commitment. If only this gets delivered, the week was a success.
- **Priority 2 (P2):** Secondary commitment.
- **Priority 3 (P3):** Tertiary commitment.

Each priority has up to three action steps. Each action step has a proof artifact — photo, video, written note, witnessed completion, receipt, deliverable, or tangible outcome.

> **No proof, no credit. Weak proof, partial credit. Strong proof, full credit.**

You are not committing to fill hours. You are committing to deliver outcomes that serve Zen Village and your own growth.

## Section 5 — The Credit Mechanic

**Priorities are the primary commitment. Hours substantiate the work. CORA Credits reward surplus.**

The goal of this exchange is not to reward time alone. Each week, you and your witness co-set 3 priorities that define the value you are creating during your stay. Hours substantiate that exchange when the work is organized, witnessed, and aligned with those priorities.

The math is simple: **20 hours of organized witnessed work at $25/hour equals $500 in weekly invoice offset**, bringing each tier to or near its floor. Priority completion adds CORA Credit bonuses on top.

### How You Pay Your Invoice

Your weekly invoice (after offsets and floor) can be paid in:
- **Cash** (USD, colones, or equivalent)
- **CORA Credits** at 1 CORA = $1
- **Any mix of cash and CORA**

Both flow into Zen Village Treasury. You choose how to settle.

### The Floor Is Sacred

You can never owe less than the floor — even with maximum contribution, premium content offsets, and CORA Credits applied. The floor reflects real operational costs that always show up.

What this means:
- Hours of work can offset value down to the floor
- Premium content offsets substitute for hours of work (not the floor)
- The floor must always be paid — in cash or CORA, but always paid

### The Base Bargain

**Contribution credit rate: $25/hour equivalent.**

**4 hours/day × 5 days = 20 hours/week × $25 = $500 weekly invoice offset.**

*Note: During Week 1 and Week 2, the Trust Curve (below) may cap how much of the invoice can be reduced even with full hours.*

Hours must be:
- Aligned with your weekly priorities
- Agreed in advance with your witness
- Witnessed or substantiated through proof
- Productive (real contribution, not just clocked time)

Hours beyond 4/day require either pre-approval or exceptional proof at Sunday Seal. Approved overage hours are credited as **25 CORA Credits per extra hour (flat rate)**, not additional invoice reduction.

### Math by Tier

| Tier | Weekly Value | 20-hour Offset | Floor | You Pay | CORA Surplus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Private room | $600 | −$500 | $100 | **$100** (zero-out) | — |
| Shared room | $530 | −$500 | $75 | **$75** (floor) | $45 |
| Communal / dorm | $440 | −$500 | $50 | **$50** (floor) | $60 |

Choosing a humbler tier means a smaller bill and more CORA Credits.

### The Three Paths

**Path 1 — Default.** Show up 4 hrs/day organized toward priorities, witnessed. Full $500 offset granted.

**Path 2 — Efficient.** Hit all 3 priorities early with strong proof. Witness can waive remaining hours and relax reporting requirements. Full $500 offset still granted plus priority CORA bonuses plus time freedom.

**Path 3 — Substantiation.** Didn't hit priorities? You can still earn the offset by substantiating the full 20 hours of organized work. Hours fill in for missing outcomes. Stay covered, no priority CORA bonus.

**If neither:** Less than 20 hours of substantiated work **and** priorities not hit = clear deficit. You pay the difference at $25/hour for each missing hour.

### Onboarding Curve

The longer you're here, the more credit can offset.

| Week | Maximum Invoice Reduction |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 50% (you are learning the land) |
| Week 2 | 75% (you are proving trust) |
| Week 3+ | Up to full reduction above the floor |

In Week 1, even 20 hours of work is capped at 50% of your weekly value. By Week 3+, the same hours bring you to the floor.

### Minimum Weekly Floor

| Stay Tier | Floor |
|---|---|
| Camping / communal / dorm | $50/week |
| Shared bed / room | $75/week |
| Private room | $100/week |

The floor cannot be reduced by credit unless a steward consciously waives it. Real costs always show up.

### CORA Credit Bonuses (separate from invoice offset)

| Achievement | CORA Credits |
|---|---|
| P1 fully delivered with strong proof | 100 |
| P2 fully delivered with strong proof | 75 |
| P3 fully delivered with strong proof | 50 |
| Approved hours beyond 4/day | **25 CORA per extra hour (flat)** |
| Excellence / exceptional impact | Witness discretion |
| Tier surplus (Path 1, smaller tier) | Auto-converted |
| Specialized skills ZV would otherwise pay for | Case-by-case |

**Weekly CORA earning cap: 250 Credits.** Above the cap, contributions are recognized as "honor entries" on a public roster — recognized socially, not banked materially. This protects the CORA economy from oversupply.

CORA Credits do not reduce your invoice. They are redeemable only against approved goods, services, and experiences within the CORA Nation ecosystem (see Section 6).

### The Formula

```
hourly_credit = hours_logged_or_substantiated × $25

# If priorities hit fully and witness waives remaining hours:
hourly_credit = max($500, hours_logged × $25)

max_invoice_reduction = value_stack − floor   (capped by onboarding curve)

invoice_reduction = MIN(hourly_credit, max_invoice_reduction)

final_amount_due = value_stack − invoice_reduction

# CORA Credits (separate from invoice; capped at 250/week)
cora_credits_raw =
    priority_completion_bonuses
  + overage_hours × 25
  + tier_surplus
  + witness_excellence

cora_credits_earned = MIN(cora_credits_raw, 250)
honor_entries       = MAX(cora_credits_raw − 250, 0)
```

## Section 6 — CORA Credits

CORA Credits are the internal currency of the CORA Nation ecosystem. They are issued through the Zen Village node by your witness at Sunday Seal.

**Exchange rate: 1 CORA = $1** of value inside the ecosystem.

### Where CORA Credits Flow

CORA Credits flow into your wallet from:
- Priority completion bonuses (see Section 5)
- Approved overage hours (25 CORA per hour, flat)
- Tier surplus when contribution exceeds invoice ceiling
- Witness-discretion excellence recognition
- Specialized skill contributions ZV would otherwise pay for
- Premium content production (see content section above)
- Cash purchase by guests, supporters, or volunteers seeking additional payment flexibility (1 CORA = $1)

CORA Credits flow out of your wallet to:
- Paying your weekly ZV invoice (any portion)
- Member-to-member service exchanges within the ZV community
- Approved goods, services, and experiences (see redemption menu below)

**Weekly earning cap: 250 CORA Credits.** Above the cap, additional contributions are recognized as honor entries on a public roster — banked socially, not banked materially. This protects the CORA economy from oversupply.

### Approved Redemption Menu

| Item | CORA Cost | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Coconut | 5 | 1/day |
| Smoothie / juice | 10 | 1/day |
| Add-on massage session | 100 | 2/month |
| Future ZV stay night (offset) | 75 | up to 7 nights/year |
| Sparking session (when available) | 150 | 1/quarter |
| Coherence cohort discount | 400 = $400 off | once per cohort |
| Thrift item | 5–30 | no cap |
| Member-offered services | At posted service price | per service |
| Cross-node services (other CORA nodes) | TBD | as they come online |

Redemption availability depends on the current offerings of CORA Nation nodes. The menu may be expanded by steward agreement.

### Strict Boundaries — What Zen Village and CORA Nation Will Not Do

Zen Village and CORA Nation **will not**:
- Convert CORA Credits to cash or any fiat currency
- Guarantee a cash value for CORA Credits
- Facilitate external currency conversion or operate any currency exchange
- Pay out earned CORA Credits as wages, dividends, or any cash distribution
- Treat CORA Credits as compensation, payment, or remuneration in any legal sense

CORA Credits exist only as internal ecosystem accounting. Their value is what they buy within the network — not a claim against Zen Village or CORA Nation for cash or external value.

### Peer-to-Peer Transfers

Members **may** privately transfer CORA Credits between their own wallets for any purpose, at any rate they negotiate among themselves. This includes:

- Gifting CORA to another member
- Paying another member for a service or item
- Selling CORA Credits to another member for cash (private arrangement)
- Pooling CORA for community events or shared experiences

Zen Village provides the wallet infrastructure that allows wallet-to-wallet transfers. Zen Village and CORA Nation are **not party** to any private arrangements involving cash, services, or other value attached to such transfers.

**Members are solely responsible for:**
- Their own private agreements regarding cash or other value exchanged off-ledger
- The valuation rate they agree to use
- Any tax implications arising from such trades in their home jurisdiction
- Disputes between members over such trades (Zen Village does not mediate cash-side disputes)

All wallet-to-wallet transfers are logged in the dashboard for transparency and audit. Transfers above 500 CORA in a single transaction are flagged for steward visibility (awareness only, not approval). Automated trading systems or third-party exchange integrations are not permitted without explicit governance approval.

This structure gives CORA real liquidity for members who need it, while keeping Zen Village and CORA Nation outside of currency exchange regulations and preserving the faith-based community character of the system.

### Wallet Expiry

CORA Credits held in a wallet inactive for 24 months may be reclaimed by the Community Fund. "Active" means any transaction — earning, spending, or transferring — within the prior 24 months.

### About Volunteer Value Exchange vs. Local Paid Workers

The contribution credit rate ($25/hour) and CORA Credit system in this agreement apply to **volunteers and stewards engaged in faith-based community service** under CORA Nation governance.

Local paid staff working at Zen Village under cash employment terms operate under **entirely separate arrangements** with colones-denominated wages. They are not part of this agreement. They may receive symbolic CORA Credit recognition for ecosystem inclusion, but their primary compensation is cash, paid through standard Costa Rica employment channels.

These two relationships serve different needs and operate through different mechanisms. They are not directly comparable, and the value of one should not be measured against the other.

## Section 10 — Community Standards & Restoration

You agree to:

- Honor the land, the practices, and the people of Zen Village
- Engage in good faith with the priorities and proof system
- Respect the privacy and integrity of fellow participants
- Refrain from harmful substances on the land (alcohol in moderation per house norms; no recreational drugs unless part of a ceremonial container explicitly approved by stewards)
- Participate in Sunday Zen Out unless excused
- Engage in conflict resolution through CORA Nation's faith-based governance process if disputes arise

### Restoration Fee Schedule

When community standards are not met, **restoration fees** are logged against the week's invoice. These are not punishments — they are transparent costs of repair. The dollar amounts make impact visible; the language keeps it dignifying.

| Breach | Restoration Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plates / dishes left out | $2 per occurrence | Behavioral deterrent |
| Wearing someone else's clothing without permission | $10 per item | Return clean + laundered |
| Same, returned damaged or dirty | $25 + replacement cost | |
| Moving communal item into private space without approval | $10/day until returned | Daily accrual until restored |
| Breaking a communal item | Full replacement cost (or repair cost if repairable) | Reported immediately = no additional fee |
| Returning a borrowed item damaged | Repair or replacement cost | Steward discretion |
| Late vehicle return | Next-tier rate kicks in | See Section 8 |
| Vehicle returned dirty or with less gas than taken | $10 + missing-gas amount | |

### Common Area Cleanliness — Positive Incentive

Common areas are not subject to individual restoration fees. Attribution is too hard and risks creating blame culture. Instead:

1. **Standing priority option:** "Maintain communal cleanliness" is always available as a P3. Anyone in the house can claim it for the week and earn credit for doing the work.
2. **House-wide good-standing bonus:** At Sunday Seal, the witness gives a simple yes/no assessment of how common areas were kept that week. If yes → everyone in the house receives a small bonus ($10–15 in CORA Credits or invoice reduction). If no → no bonus, brief house-level conversation about what's happening.
3. **Pattern of "no" assessments** = house meeting, not fines.

Collective care, collective recognition.

### Escalation Path for Communal-to-Private Use

The daily fee runs *alongside* the tier escalation:

1. **First instance:** $10/day until returned + witness conversation
2. **Second instance:** $20/day until returned + formal review with second steward + final written warning in the ledger
3. **Third instance** (or any single egregious case): grounds for immediate termination per Section 12

The agreement is plain: *moving communal items into private use without permission is a breach of community trust. Repeated occurrences are grounds for early termination.*

## Section 11 — Good Standing & Trust Progression

The same architecture that tracks breaches also tracks **good standing**.

Volunteers who:
- Maintain communal cleanliness without being asked
- Return borrowed items promptly
- Respect shared property and space
- Leave places better than they found them
- Show up consistently to community rhythms
- Treat fellow members with dignity

…accumulate good-standing recognition that:
- Accelerates the onboarding curve (faster progression from 50% → 100% reduction available)
- Expands witness latitude for priority co-setting
- Generates CORA Credits at the witness's discretion
- Unlocks services-marketplace eligibility ahead of week 3
- Builds the foundation for steward consideration if longer stay is desired

The system isn't surveilling for failure. It's witnessing both directions of the exchange.

### Zen Village Thrift

Items left behind by departing volunteers and residents are washed, tagged, and posted in a "Zen Village Thrift" section of the dashboard. Available items can be purchased at posted prices ($5–25 typically) in either USD or CORA Credits.

The Thrift system is designed to:
- Reduce the temptation to borrow without permission (purchase becomes the easy path)
- Create affordable access to needed items (cloud-forest evenings get cold)
- Recirculate value within the community
- Provide a small revenue stream that flows back to operations
