## About CORA & Zen Village

**Zen Village** is a node within **CORA Nation** — a faith-based community (508(c)(1)(A)) building post-extraction economies where value circulates rather than leaks out.

The system uses two ledgers:
- **Zen Village Work Credits (ZWC)** — earned through hours of witnessed work, used to settle your invoice
- **CORA Credits** — performance-based currency used for premium experiences across the broader CORA Nation network

Both ledgers are non-cashable by Zen Village. Members may trade them privately.

---

## The System in 4 Lines

> **1. Zen Village invoices you weekly for your membership** ($400 Shared / $600 Private)
>
> **2. Offset your invoice** through 20 hours of work ($400 work credit) + priorities (ZV may award bonus credits to close any gap)
>
> **3. Earn CORA Credits** through additional contribution — premium currency for the three menus (Food / Services / Access)
>
> **4. If short** on hours or priorities → pay the gap in $ or CORA from your wallet

That's the whole thing.

Think of it like a membership subscription. ZV charges you weekly. You offset that charge through your work and priority delivery. Going above and beyond earns you premium credits (CORA) to unlock the bonus menus.

## How the Exchange Works

### Hours (Guaranteed via Daily Proof)

- Work 20 hours per week — but **ZWC is earned only against verified daily proof**
- 20 hrs × $20/hr = $400 ZWC applied to your invoice (max possible)
- This is the guaranteed portion — show up, do the work, post proof, get credit

### Daily Proof Protocol — How ZWC Actually Gets Earned

Each work day, post to the designated WhatsApp channel:

1. **Top work priority / purpose** for the day (what you're focused on)
2. **Time frame you worked** (start/stop or total hours)
3. **Progress made** — what specifically advanced
4. **Before / after photos** when applicable (especially physical work, builds, cleanups)

**No daily post = no ZWC for that day.** Hours claimed without proof do not count.

The witness reviews posts daily for verification. Verified hours accumulate through the week. Final tally and ZWC application happens at Sunday Seal.

### Priorities (Sets You Up for Bonus)
- Each Monday, set 3 weekly priorities with your witness (P1, P2, P3)
- Priorities are specific, measurable, aligned with what the village needs
- Each Sunday Seal, witness reviews delivery
- **Strong delivery typically results in a ZV bonus** — but bonus amount and form (ZWC or CORA) is at ZV's discretion

### Bonuses (Discretionary)
ZV may award a bonus in either form:
- **ZWC bonus** → closes any invoice gap (typical for Private Room volunteers who delivered)
- **CORA bonus** → goes to your wallet for premium experiences (typical for Shared volunteers; or extra for Private volunteers after gap closed)

**Bonuses are not guaranteed.** They reflect actual contribution as judged by ZV. Strong consistent delivery builds track record — which is also how you earn the Private Room tier over time.

### Gap Coverage (If Bonuses Don't Fully Close It)
If you're short on hours OR priorities aren't delivered to ZV's satisfaction:
- Pay the gap in **cash** (USD or colones equivalent)
- Or pay in **CORA** from your wallet (1 CORA = $1)
- Or combination of both

The gap is a natural consequence of incomplete contribution — not a punishment. It also signals when a tier change might be appropriate.

## CORA Wallet — Peer Trades & Network Use

Your CORA wallet holds bonuses earned through priority delivery, peer service income, and any cash purchases. The Bonus Access table above shows what CORA buys within Zen Village.

**CORA also flows across the network:**
- **Peer-to-peer trades** — Members can trade CORA with each other at any rate they negotiate privately. ZV provides wallet infrastructure but is not party to private cash arrangements between members. Members handle their own tax implications.
- **Pay other members for services** at their posted rates (massage, coaching, classes, cooking)
- **Gift to other members** in any amount
- **Cover an invoice gap** if you fall short on hours or priorities (1 CORA = $1)
- **Save for future use** — across Zen Village and other CORA Nation nodes as they come online

**What Zen Village and CORA Nation will not do:**
- Convert CORA to cash
- Guarantee a cash value for CORA
- Operate any currency exchange

## How Volunteers Earn CORA Beyond Hours

**ZWC pays for the work · CORA pays for the result.** Time spent producing content counts toward your 20 work hours (ZWC offset on your invoice) — that already covers your effort. Additional CORA flows only when content produces real outcomes.

Three earning paths, ranked by impact on Zen Village sustainability:

### 1. Sales Commissions — Real Revenue Moved

Volunteer's choice of payment on any sale they traceably drove:

- **15% in cash** OR
- **20% in CORA** (higher % to keep credits in the ecosystem)

| Product | Commission |
|---|---|
| Camp Zen Booking ($400–$3000) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Full Potential Retreat ($1999) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Sparking session ($1500) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Paid coaching ($500–$2000) | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Coherence Course *(not live yet — structure locked)* | 15% cash / 20% CORA |
| Any other ZV / CORA Nation product | 15% cash / 20% CORA |

**Requires traceable attribution:** referral link, voucher code, or logged conversation. Commissions can stack — the same content can drive multiple sales.

### 2. Verified Traction — Audience That Earns Future Sales

Analytics-confirmed reach, verified 30 days post-publication. Pre-approved content only.

| Reach | CORA |
|---|---|
| 25k+ verified views on a single piece | 150 |
| 5k–25k verified views | 75 |
| 1k–5k verified views/reach | 25 |

### 3. Use in Branded Products — Selected by ZV

Content incorporated into actual ZV products — Show, marketing, sales materials.

| Use | CORA |
|---|---|
| Full Show episode contribution (major creative role) | 250 |
| Scene/segment used in The Show | 200 |
| Show Reel approved for marketing | 150 |
| Content used in team marketing/sales materials | 25 |

*Show content requires founding steward sign-off.*

### What Does NOT Earn CORA

- ❌ Content posted without concept pre-approval
- ❌ Long-form thought leadership without verified traction or driven sale
- ❌ Random social posts not part of an approved campaign
- ❌ Personal content tagged ZV
- ❌ Content without subject consent
- ❌ Bringing in volunteers (volunteers are a cost; only sales/revenue drive CORA)
- ❌ Activity without verifiable impact

### How One Piece of Content Can Stack

A volunteer-created piece could earn across all three tiers:
- 5k verified views → 75 CORA (Tier 2)
- Drives a Full Potential Retreat sale → $300 cash OR 400 CORA (Tier 1)
- ZV selects it for marketing materials → 25 CORA (Tier 3)
- **Total: $300 + 100 CORA · OR 500 CORA all-in**

The system rewards real impact, not activity.

### Approval & Verification Flow

1. **Concept pre-approval** with content steward (topic, format, subject consent, brand fit)
2. **Production check-ins** for larger pieces
3. **Publication** with attribution tracking where applicable
4. **30-day verification** for traction bonuses (platform analytics)
5. **CORA issued** as outcomes verify

**Rights:** ZV owns ecosystem usage rights. Creator retains attribution and portfolio rights with permission. External commercial use requires separate licensing.
