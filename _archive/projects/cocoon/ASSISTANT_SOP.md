> One page. Read once. Execute daily.

---

## Your Single Mission

**Help get the first cocoon built and into use as fast as possible.**

That's it. Nothing else.

---

## Decision Filter (Use Before Every Action)

Ask yourself:

> "Does this get a real prototype built faster?"

- **Yes** → Proceed
- **No** → Escalate to James

Do not optimize. Do not redesign. Do not ideate.

---

## What a Cocoon IS

A one-person recovery cocoon is:

- A physical enclosure that goes over a person lying on a mat
- Creates a calm, enclosed environment for a 12-minute reset session
- May be inflatable, soft-fabric framed, or modular sections (head/torso/feet)

Inside:
- Low, diffused LED lighting (red/amber/warm tones)
- Off-the-shelf components only
- Optional sound/vibration via existing devices (e.g., Woojer mat)
- No screens, no complex controls

---

## What This is NOT

- Not medical equipment
- Not therapy
- Not diagnostic
- Not treatment
- Not regulated health tech

**Always describe as:** wellness infrastructure for relaxation and nervous-system calming.

**Never use:** medical or therapeutic language.

---

## What "Local Fabricator" Means

You're looking for Miami builders who:
- Work out of warehouses
- Physically build custom objects
- Do art installations, scenic builds, soft structures, inflatables
- Can make one-off prototypes quickly

Examples:
- Scenic/event fabrication shops
- Art installation builders
- Upholstery or marine canvas shops
- Inflatable or soft-goods fabricators

**NOT:** product designers, agencies, startups, factories

---

## What to Escalate

Escalate to James when:
- Someone asks to change the product
- You're unsure if something speeds up the prototype
- A decision involves money beyond quotes
- Someone wants to discuss strategy or vision
- You need to commit to anything binding

## Weekly Report Format

Every Friday, deliver:

1. **What moved forward** (2-3 bullets)
2. **What's blocked** (if anything)
3. **What needs a decision** (if anything)
4. **Your recommendation** (2-3 sentences max)

Keep it short. No strategy.
