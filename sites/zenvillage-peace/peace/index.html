what we're building

The Manifesto

The weekend was the prototype. The Manifesto is the form.
Coherent Champions of CHRIST — a practice of becoming trustworthy with power.

the practice

C · Coherence
alignment between thought, word, action, technology, consequence
H · Healing
healing individuals, relationships, communities, ecosystems
R · Regeneration
building systems that restore more life than they consume
I · Intelligence
guided by wisdom, humility, discernment, and care
S · Service
our gifts in service to the flourishing of life
T · Truth
sought courageously, held compassionately

This is not a religion of superiority. It is a practice of becoming trustworthy with power.

the covenant

The World Peace Agreement

  • I agree to practice peace in thought, word, and action.
  • I agree to reduce unnecessary suffering.
  • I agree to seek understanding before hatred.
  • I agree to repair where I have caused harm.
  • I agree to protect life, truth, beauty, and future generations.
  • I agree to become trustworthy with intelligence, influence, and resources.
  • I agree that peace must become visible through action.

Signed not in perfection, but in sincere participation.
