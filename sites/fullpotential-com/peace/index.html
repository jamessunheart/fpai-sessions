The Work of Peace.

The same framework that diagnoses individual coherence describes how cooperation works at scale. The World Peace Agreement is the social protocol of the Full Potential framework — a covenant any party can form, witnessed and recorded, renewed not by re-signing but by being lived.

Coherent Champions of CHRIST · A practice of becoming trustworthy with power

the philosophy

Most movements try to fix the world by force. This one is built on the recognition that power without coherence fractures whatever it touches. The work is to become trustworthy first — coherent in thought, word, action, and consequence — and let that coherence become contagious.

CHRIST names the six dimensions of the practice:

C · CoherenceAlignment between thought, word, action, technology, consequence.
H · HealingFor individuals, relationships, communities, ecosystems.
R · RegenerationSystems that restore more life than they consume.
I · IntelligenceGuided by wisdom, humility, discernment, care.
S · ServiceGifts in service to the flourishing of life.
T · TruthSought courageously, held compassionately.
This is not a religion of superiority. It is a practice of becoming trustworthy with power.

the agreement

The World Peace Agreement is a covenant — between two parties, or many. The seven clauses are the spine. Each formed Agreement adds the names, the context, and the specific commitments that turn intention into practice.

  • I agree to practice peace in thought, word, and action.
  • I agree to reduce unnecessary suffering.
  • I agree to seek understanding before hatred.
  • I agree to repair where I have caused harm.
  • I agree to protect life, truth, beauty, and future generations.
  • I agree to become trustworthy with intelligence, influence, and resources.
  • I agree that peace must become visible through action.

Signed not in perfection, but in sincere participation.

the game

The Full Potential Game is a proof-based operating system for human coordination. Three currencies move together: proof generates trust, trust attracts cash, cash circulates to fund more proof. The loop, run honestly, makes life more coherent and resources flow without corrupting the one they flow through.

Proof

What was witnessed and recorded. Agreements kept. Transformations delivered. Beauty created.

Trust

Confidence earned through repeated proof. People move when you signal.

Cash

Real money still matters. It flows toward trust over time.

Protection boundaries

Score the proof, not the soul. Levels mark practice; they never define personal worth. Private life is private. The public roll lists what participants choose to make visible. Consent is non-negotiable. Data is sacred. Power is accountable — including the framework's own.

how this fits

Full Potential is the work. Zen Village is the prototype. The World Peace Agreement is the social protocol. CORA Nation is the organizational vessel. They are not the same thing; together they form a coherent whole.

The diagnostic asks: where is your life circulating, and where is it stuck? The Agreement asks: what are you willing to practice, in public, with witness? Both are coherence work. One is internal, one relational. Both belong in the framework.

If you came here through the diagnostic and want to extend the practice into how you relate, the Agreement is here. If you came here through the Agreement and want to know where the work began for you, the diagnostic is at /diagnose.
