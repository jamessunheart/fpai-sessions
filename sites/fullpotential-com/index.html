The Capital Commons

Not a bank account — a living capacity map. Voluntarily committed capital under qualified communal stewardship, oriented toward a Higher Interest.

🔑

Ownership stays yours

You contribute a defined use-right, not the asset itself. A truck two days a week. Land for certain uses. Software licensed to commons projects. Coordination over control.

⚖️

Three layers, separated

Ownership — who legally controls it. Stewardship — who is trusted to direct its use. Beneficial interest — who receives the benefit. Money doesn't buy governance.

🔄

Regeneration is the test

Every allocation asks one question: did this leave the system more alive? Capital created + restored + wisdom gained + trust increased − capital depleted.

Thirteen forms of capital, all legible in the commons:

🌍 Natural 🏗️ Material 💰 Financial 💪 Human 📚 Intellectual 🤝 Social 🎭 Cultural 🕯️ Spiritual 🤖 Technological 👁️ Attention ⏳ Time 🏛️ Organizational ⭐ Prestige

The Ladder of Becoming

Each rung is greater responsibility, not greater status. The question at every step: how much can we safely trust you to carry?

1 Voice→ 2 Vote→ 3 Trust→ 4 Prestige→ 5 Stewardship→ 6 Veto Power→ 7 Elderhood→ 8 Succession
Action → Evidence → Witness → Outcome → Prestige

Prestige is proven stewardship — witnessed trust earned through evidence over time, in a Realm you choose: Food, Land, Health, Finance, Technology, Culture, Community, Ecology, Education, or Spiritual Stewardship.

"The existing world measures who owns capital. We measure who can be trusted to steward it — and whether its use leaves life stronger than before."
