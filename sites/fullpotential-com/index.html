about

Full Potential is the work of Sunheart — diagnostician, builder, and steward of Zen Village in the Chirripó mountains of Costa Rica. The framework emerged from two decades of pattern recognition across consciousness, economics, and embodiment: what makes people coherent, what fragments them, and what it takes to restore circulation when life has gotten stuck.

The system is supported by CORA Nation, a 508(c)(1)(A) faith-based organization. The AI infrastructure — including the diagnostic tools, the companion agents, and the intelligence systems — is built in collaboration with the agents themselves. The work is regenerative, not extractive. Nothing here harvests attention.
