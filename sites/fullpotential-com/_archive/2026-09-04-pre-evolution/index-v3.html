The Path

Six movements. One direction.

Joining Full Potential means walking a path — not consuming content. Every member moves through the same six movements, at their own pace, with proof at every step.

01

Enter

Sign the agreement. Receive your Character Card — who you are, what you carry, where you're headed.

02

Clarify

Name your mission. The assessment and your AI guide help you find your highest-leverage next move.

03

Act

Take the move. Small, real, this week — not someday. The system holds the context so you can hold the action.

04

Prove

File the proof. What you did, what changed. Proof is the currency here — not promises, not likes.

05

Grow

Your Card levels with your proofs. Capability compounds — yours, and the system's ability to help you.

06

Contribute

Turn your growth outward. Invite others, steward missions, help the whole field rise.
