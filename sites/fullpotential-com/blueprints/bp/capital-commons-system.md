# Capital Commons System

**Status:** live canon · **Version:** 1.0 (2026-09-04) · **Realm:** Governance & Capital

A regenerative governance and capital operating system built on trust. Coordination over control.

## Core design

- **Higher Interest** — power and capital are tested against truth, love, life, dignity, future generations, ecological health, regeneration.
- **Voice** — everyone begins with the right to speak, propose, challenge, be witnessed. Prestige never erases Voice.
- **Realms** — domains of stewardship (Food, Land, Health, Finance, Technology, Culture, Community, Ecology, Education, Spiritual). One Primary Realm per person at a time.
- **Ladder of Becoming** — Voice → Vote → Trust → Prestige → Stewardship → Veto Power → Elderhood → Succession.
- **Prestige** — proven stewardship: witnessed trust earned through evidence over time. Loop: Action → Evidence → Witness → Outcome → Score Update.
- **Capital Commons** — voluntarily committed capital (13 types: natural, material, financial, human, intellectual, social, cultural, spiritual, technological, attention, time, organizational, prestige) under qualified communal stewardship.
- **Use-rights over forced transfer** — ownership stays private; use is coordinated via Capital Intake records.
- **Three layers separated** — Ownership (legal control) · Stewardship (trusted direction) · Beneficial interest (who benefits). Capital does not buy governance.
- **Mutual credit** — circulation layer; claim on future contribution, not money-fied prestige.
- **Three ledgers** — Capital Ledger · Mutual Credit Ledger · Prestige Ledger.
- **Ostrom locks** — 8 commons protections (boundaries, fitted rules, participation, monitoring, graduated sanctions, cheap conflict resolution, self-organization, nested polycentrism).
- **Regeneration test** — Capital Created + Restored + Wisdom + Trust − Depleted. Did this allocation make the system more alive?
- **Succession** — the torch is passed, never possessed.

## Creed

Create privately. Contribute voluntarily. Steward communally. Regenerate together.
