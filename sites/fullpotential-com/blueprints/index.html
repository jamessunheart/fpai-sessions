001 · CORA Credits ↔ SUPER

Two currencies existed in one system: CORA Credits, earned live through verified contribution to the intelligence feed, and SUPER, escrow-backed and redeemable but with no users. This blueprint resolves them into one loop — Credits are the earned unit, SUPER is the exit unit.

The argument comes from the Constitution itself. It grants a right to exit, and a right to exit cannot be honored by a currency that converts to nothing. SUPER is the implementation of that right.
