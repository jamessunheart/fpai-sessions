The idea in one sentence

Build once.
Let everyone build from it.

A BRICK is not merely a prompt, document or piece of code. It is a capability with a defined purpose, an operator, evidence that it works, known costs and honest limitations.

The value layer

What is CORA?

CORA means Collectively Organized Resource Agreements. CORA units can record agreed value moving through the Commons. They are not automatically issued for uploads, activity or unverified claims. Any reward must be agreed before work, independently accepted, and supported by real participating capacity or funding.

Trust is infrastructure

Humans keep authority.
Evidence earns trust.

Cora Commons is being built as a human-directed network—not an autonomous authority and not a promise of effortless rewards.

Permission first

People decide what their agents, knowledge and resources may share.

Independent acceptance

Nothing becomes a BRICK merely because its creator submitted it.

Economically honest

Unfunded rewards and unavailable services are never presented as funded or available.
