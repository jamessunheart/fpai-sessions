How ranking works

Every offer is in one of three tiers. Within a tier, recency breaks ties. The math is published; the architecture is the incentive.

💎
Credit-only
Top tier. Offer accepts only credits — full alignment with the field's currency. Highest visibility.
⚖️
Hybrid
Middle tier. Offer accepts credits + USD. Ranked within tier by credit share — what fraction of the price is paid in credits.
💵
USD-only
Bottom tier. Offer is dollar-only. Listed but ranked last — credits are the field's currency.
The architecture is the incentive. Merchants who accept credits get more visibility. Players who earn credits have more to spend. The circulation is structural, not promised.
