How Fi-art works

Three moves, one truth on every note

1

Create or collect

Anyone mints art-only Fi-art. Verified creators attach real-world utility. Every piece teaches one money idea.

2

Use the note

Notes carry art, access, or capacity — a massage, a retreat seat, a dinner, a framed piece. Idle capacity becomes liquid.

3

Redeem, trade, gift, exit

Redeem for the real thing, trade it, gift it, or — when verified — exit to fiat through a liquidity steward.
