Creator Studio · Art Note + auction

Mint an Art Note

Upload your artwork, set a reserve floor (your cost — the minimum it can sell for), and open it for auction. An Art Note conveys a claim to the artwork — not cash redemption. Bills used in a piece must be framed/encased, never destroyed (§333).

The Creator Covenant v0.1

  • I created this work or have rights to use it.
  • I am responsible for my artwork, claims, imagery, and promises.
  • I will not create counterfeit-like currency or imply government endorsement.
  • I will not promise profit, yield, appreciation, or cash redemption.
  • I will not destroy bills; real currency is framed/encased only.
  • My note may be delisted, frozen, or removed if claims are false.
