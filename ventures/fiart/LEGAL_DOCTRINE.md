Canonical compliance doctrine, distilled from the U.S.-focused mock trial (James 2026-06-25). Governs `SITE_SPEC.md` and `COMPLIANCE.md`. **The sacred rule: art can be playful; redemption must be exact.**

> ⚠️ Mock trial / design doctrine — NOT legal advice. Counsel sign-off gates any fiat-redemption (Mirror) or reserve product before launch. Cites: FinCEN CVC + prepaid-access FAQ · OCC/Treasury GENIUS Act stablecoin · SEC Howey/crypto interp · FTC truth-in-advertising · BEP currency imagery + 18 U.S.C. §333 · NAIC financial-guaranty · FDIC deposit-insurance · OFAC sanctions · IRS digital-asset-as-property · Copyright Office DMCA §512 · CFTC spot/derivatives.

## The winning frame
> Fi-art is a transparent **rights marketplace** where money-inspired art can remain art, become access, unlock capacity, claim physical work, or — in restricted cases — redeem through regulated liquidity rails. **None are legal tender. Only notes whose Truth Panel says so are redeemable.**

**The danger zone is not the art. It is fixed-value, transferable, fiat-redeemable notes.** Poetic branding cannot protect that layer. If it behaves like money, regulators may treat it like money.

## Platform-wide language law
- ❌ Never say globally: "Not money." (too absolute)
- ✅ Always: **"Not legal tender. Rights vary by note. Read the Truth Panel."**

## The 12 counts → operative fixes

| # | Charge | Weak spot | Operative fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Issuing money** (FinCEN CVC) | "Fiart Credit" sounds like currency; transferable + fiat-redeemable = exhibit A | **Rename → "Fiart Marketplace Credit."** Define: *closed-loop platform credit usable only for eligible Fi-art listings/capacity. Not legal tender, not cash, not redeemable for fiat unless via a stated regulated path.* |
| 2 | **Mirror Notes = payment stablecoins** (OCC/GENIUS) | Public "$1 Fiart = $1" indefensible without permitted issuer/MT license/bank | **No general-purpose Mirror Notes in MVP.** Fiat redemption only via licensed partners; Mirror only as regulated redemption receipts / permitted-stablecoin pathway. |
| 3 | **NFTs = securities** (Howey) | Profit/appreciation/yield/floor-price/fractional-revenue language | **Utility-first verbs only:** collect · redeem · use · gift · claim · attend · book · display · learn. Ban investment language in listings. |
| 4 | **Deceiving consumers** (FTC) | The word "backed" unless precise | **Truth Panel before purchase, not after.** "Backed by" must be exact: *"Supported by 23 social vouches — no collateral backing"* / *"Backed by $10,000 verified reserve held by [entity], verified [date], subject to terms."* |
| 5 | **Capacity Notes = gift cards/prepaid** (FinCEN prepaid; gift-card rules) | "Spend anywhere in network" credit looks like stored value | **Keep Capacity Notes service-specific + merchant-defined:** named provider, service, location, booking window, expiration, blackout, transfer rule, replacement policy. |
| 6 | **Counterfeiting / currency imagery** (BEP; §333) | "Mint scans of banknotes"; high-res exact scans | **Currency-inspired art, not minted scans.** Enforce wizard: real-currency? one-sided? <75% or >150% size? stylized? "NOT LEGAL TENDER"? source files deleted? no seals/counterfeit layout/ad use? |
| 7 | **Vouches = unlicensed insurance** (NAIC) | "Your redemption is guaranteed by the community" | **Three pure labels:** *Social Vouch* (reputation only) · *Collateral Pledge* (locked value, limited amount/term) · *Reserve Support* (verified steward). Ban: insured/guaranteed/protected/risk-free/default-coverage/community-insurance. |
| 8 | **Acting like a bank / implying FDIC** (FDIC) | deposit · bank · FDIC-insured · savings · guaranteed balance | **Use:** marketplace balance · eligible redemption credit · custodied reserve · liquidity steward · redemption receipt. Never wear a bank costume. |
| 9 | **Money laundering / sanctions** (OFAC) | Anonymous cash redemption | **Risk-tiered KYC** (see tiers below). Cash exits = sanctions screening + monitoring + limits + audit logs. |
| 10 | **Tax confusion** (IRS digital-asset-as-property) | Implying tax-free barter / invisible income | **Tax dashboard from day one:** tx history, cost-basis export, redemption receipts, provider revenue reports, "you are responsible for reporting" notices. |
| 11 | **UGC IP infringement** (DMCA §512) | "Creators are responsible" alone is insufficient | **Real broom:** rights attestation · registered DMCA agent (public) · repeat-infringer policy · admin takedown queue · prohibited-content scanner · currency-imagery review. |
| 12 | **Commodity/fraud/manipulation** (CFTC) | Leverage, futures, options, prediction notes, yield pools | **Use-first, not speculation-first.** No leverage/margin/derivatives/event-bets/pooled-return programs. |

## The 6-layer architecture (the defense IS the separation)
The prosecution wins when these blur; the defense wins when each note answers all nine questions below.

1. **Open Art** — no backing, no redemption, pure expression
2. **Capacity** — redeemable for specific real-world services
3. **Access** — membership, events, credentials, community
4. **Physical Art** — claim to a physical/digital artwork
5. **Marketplace Credit** — closed-loop, usable inside Fi-art only
6. **Mirror Notes** — restricted, regulated fiat-redemption receipts

**Every note must answer:** What is this? What backs it? Can it be redeemed? For what? By whom? Can it be sold? Can it expire? Can it become cash? What can fail?

## Risk-tiered access (Count 9)
`Tier 0` browse/learn · `Tier 1` buy art-only · `Tier 2` trade within marketplace limits · `Tier 3` redeem capacity · `Tier 4` issue redeemable notes · `Tier 5` cash exit / liquidity steward. **KYC/KYB weight rises with risk; art-only stays light, cash exits go heavy.**

## Per-note label law
- **Open Fi-art:** Art-only. No backing. No redemption. Resale only if a buyer chooses to buy.
- **Capacity:** Redeemable for listed goods/services/experiences. Not cash redeemable unless stated.
- **Art Note:** Represents digital/physical artwork. No cash redemption unless stated.
- **Access:** Redeemable for listed access/event/membership/credential.
- **Mirror:** Restricted fiat-redemption claim — subject to reserve terms, eligibility, limits, fees, approved partner. Not a deposit, not general-purpose money.

## Revised site copy (apply when synthesis lands)
- **Global:** "Fi-art is not legal tender. Fi-art notes are art, access, capacity claims, physical-art claims, or limited redemption receipts. Each note's rights are defined by its Truth Panel."

## Safe launch order (matches ROADMAP, sharpened)
- **MVP:** Open + Capacity + Art + Access · Truth Panels · creator guidelines · DMCA · currency-imagery compliance · **no cash redemption · no funded vouches · no transferable $1 credit.**
- **P2:** closed-loop Fiart Marketplace Credit · provider dashboards · capacity redemption · booking · redemption receipts · fulfillment scores.
- **P3:** social vouches · provider reputation · collateral pledges (carefully framed) · **no yield, no insurance language.**
- **P4 (licensed rails only):** Mirror Notes · cash exits · liquidity stewards · KYC/KYB · reserve attestations · sanctions screening · licensed-partner structure.
