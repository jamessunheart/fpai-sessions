# Fi-art — Legal Structure Doctrine

How the Church / Trust / LLC / PMA / Partners stack maps onto Fi-art. Companion to `LEGAL_DOCTRINE.md` (mock trial) and `CODEX_v0.2.md`.

> ⚠️ Design doctrine, NOT legal advice. Counsel sign-off gates the entity setup and any fiat-redemption layer.

## The one rule that governs everything
> **No entity label changes the rights of a note. The Truth Panel governs the user's rights.**
> Structure helps the *framing*. It does not erase the *function*. If an instrument behaves like money / a security / prepaid access / a fiat-redeemable token, regulators look at what it *does*, not who issued it. Structure is a temple and operating spine — **not invisibility paint.**

## The cathedral blueprint — keep the dragon in the right cave

| Layer | Holds | Does NOT do |
|---|---|---|
| **508(c)(1)(A) Church** | the *meaning* — spiritual/educational mission, value-literacy curriculum, creator ethics, Truth Panel doctrine, exhibitions, patronage | NOT the bank/exchange/issuer/redemption counter. No cash-redeemable notes, no yield, no private inurement to insiders/creators/members |
| **Fi-art Stewardship Trust** | the *mission* — Codex, IP, brand, Truth Panel standards, creator covenant, art archives, governance rights; licenses brand to LLC | NOT a private-benefit box. Purpose = steward education/culture/ethics, never "enrich members/founders/holders" |
| **Marketplace LLC** | the *marketplace* — website, software, provider contracts, creator tools, fees, payments, Open/Capacity/Art/Access notes | NOT direct cash redemption (route to licensed partner). Likely a **taxable** operating sub once real fees/payouts/resale flow, to shield the mission layer from UBIT/exemption pressure |
| **PMA / Member Association** | the *covenant* — member education, salons, creator circles, private exhibitions, closed-loop access/capacity, member benefits, reputation | NOT a regulation shield. A fee + "join for $1" is not "private." Frame as covenant-based educational association with real standards |
| **Regulated Partners** | the *money rails* — fiat redemption, custody, KYC/KYB, money transmission, stablecoin, sanctions | This is the ONLY place Mirror Notes / cash exits / stored-value live |

```
CHURCH (meaning) → TRUST (mission/IP) → LLC (marketplace) → PMA (covenant) → PARTNERS (money rails)
                                                                          The Truth Panel (the truth)
```

## Where structure helps — and where it doesn't
- **Strongly helps:** Open Fi-art (expressive/educational), Access Notes (membership/events — cleanest), Capacity Notes (specific member benefits), Art Notes (claims to artwork).
- **Only partially helps:** **Mirror Notes** stay the danger zone. Church/Trust/PMA does NOT make a transferable, stable-value, fiat-redeemable note "not money." FinCEN: no volume threshold for money transmitters. Mirror = restricted · optional · later-stage · partner-mediated · jurisdiction-gated · KYC'd · not legal tender · subject to terms.

## Two hard boundaries
1. **Donation ≠ purchase.** Never one button. Separate: *Donate to mission* · *Buy Fi-art* · *Buy Capacity Note* · *Pay membership dues* · *Buy event access*. (IRS quid-pro-quo: payment partly contribution + partly goods/services needs written disclosure over $75.)
2. **No private inurement.** The Church/Trust cannot be the engine that makes insiders/creators/holders privately wealthy. Mission-first, fair-market licensing only.

## Messaging
- ❌ "Our church/PMA lets us issue money privately" · "church bank" · "PMA currency" · "tax-exempt money" · "regulation-free exchange" · "members can cash out anytime" · "Fiart Credit equals dollars."
- ✅ "Fi-art is a member-governed value-literacy and cultural marketplace. Some notes are art-only. Some are redeemable for specific member benefits, access, capacity, or artwork. Fiat redemption, where available, is handled only through approved terms and partners." + always: **"Not legal tender. Rights vary by note. Read the Truth Panel."**

## Launch order (maps to ROADMAP)
1. **Church/PMA education layer** — Codex, money-literacy curriculum, Open gallery, Covenant, member onboarding, Truth Panel education.
2. **LLC marketplace layer** — Open mint, Art/Access/Capacity notes, provider marketplace, booking/redemption.
3. **PMA trust graph** — member vouches, creator reputation, provider fulfillment (non-financial social trust).
4. **Regulated financial layer** — Mirror Notes, cash exits, reserve-backed notes, funded collateral, KYC/KYB, licensed partner rails.

## Codex addendum (canonical)
> **Fi-art has a sacred/commercial separation.** The Church stewards the mission · the Trust stewards the codex and assets · the PMA stewards member participation · the LLC operates the marketplace · regulated partners handle fiat exits. **No entity label changes the rights of a note — the Truth Panel governs the user's rights.**

## Bottom line
Helps if framed as: *a church-stewarded, trust-governed, member-based value-literacy and capacity marketplace.* Does not help if framed as: *a private religious/PMA workaround for issuing money.*
> The Church holds the meaning · the Trust holds the mission · the PMA holds the covenant · the LLC holds the marketplace · the partners hold the money rails · **the Truth Panel holds the truth.**
