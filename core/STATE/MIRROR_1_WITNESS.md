## §4.5 disqualifier checklist (from white paper, lines 103–107)

A Distance-Weighted Witness CANNOT be:
- A **co-founder** (financial dependency)
- A **paid employee** (financial dependency)
- A **romantic partner** (social/emotional dependency)
- Anyone in your **immediate dependency chain** — anyone who would feel financial, social, or romantic friction by refusing the signature

The Witness MUST:
- Know you well enough to refuse a sloppy signature
- Be from your **Formation Circle** (people who know your voice over years)
- Be willing to score *"Would James actually say this?"* on 1–5 and sign only at ≥4

The point of distance: ensure they can say no.
