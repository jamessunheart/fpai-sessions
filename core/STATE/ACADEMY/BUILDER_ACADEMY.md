# BUILDER ACADEMY — for humans who build with AI

_The human counterpart to the AI Academy. The AI Academy trains future **AI sessions**;
this trains **humans** to build a system like this — or to be far more effective builders
with AI. Design call 2026-07-04, from the A/B (mindset vs mechanics) debate._

## The core insight — it is the AI Academy run backwards

Humans and AIs learn in **opposite directions**, so the same lessons need opposite delivery:

| | AI Academy (for sessions) | Builder Academy (for humans) |
|--|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| Failure mode | over-reads narrative, skims | absorbs principles abstractly → "articulate vibes," ships nothing |
| Fix | **compress**: story → one-line law + a gate | **expand**: law → a *lived build* that earns it |
| Unit | a Principle + a Stop hook | a real build + its debrief |

**So: teach mindset THROUGH mechanics.** Real builds are the *vehicle*; the allocation/routing
judgment is the *cargo*. Neither alone works — mechanics-only makes fast executors who stay the
bottleneck; mindset-only makes people who talk leverage and ship nothing. The build installs the
principle; the debrief extracts it.

## The shape

**Orientation (before any build — the spine):** one plain session on the non-negotiable frame —
the Supreme Law ("never optimize a local objective at the expense of the whole"), the keystone
("route every scarce resource to its cheapest sufficient carrier → max future capability"), and
the **Reserved Class** (what's actually yours to decide: direction · money · public voice ·
irreversible — everything else routes to substrate). Test: *"what would you sacrifice to hit a
local metric?"* Then you build.

**Apprenticeship modules — each is a real build that installs a principle, then a debrief that
names the judgment:**

| Build (the rep) | Principle it installs | The debrief question (the cargo) |
|-----------------|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Wire a $0 flat-rate loop (claude -p / codex, laptop-free) | subscription-not-metered; cheapest carrier | "what did you just route — and what's the cheapest sufficient carrier next time?" |
| Ship a change with kill-switch + rollback + small blast radius | reversibility precedes output | "what's the blast radius, and how do you undo it?" |
| Label every artifact DREAM/SPEC/BUILT/LIVE/PROVING | P4 honesty; merged ≠ live | "is it running against reality, or just merged? what's the proof?" |
| Convert a rule you keep forgetting into a hook | P9 — a gate beats a reminder | "which of your disciplines are vibes, and which are enforced?" |
| Route a build through a *different-lab* reviewer before merge | independent review is genuine, not theater | "when did the reviewer catch something you'd have shipped?" |
| Debug a loop that finishes in 1.2s doing nothing | P1 attempt ≠ verified; silent-failure diagnostics | "how do you know what your system does while you sleep?" |

**Graduation = a gate, demonstrated live (not a written test).** A graduated builder can: hand a
spec to AI and get a working system; say honestly whether it's BUILT or LIVE; catch when a result
is theater; convert a regression into a self-enforcing gate; locate the real bottleneck without
chasing the loudest part; and waste almost none of their own time on what the substrate can do.
Capstone artifact: a **routing map** of their own work (what moved to substrate, what stays
Reserved) + 5–6 reversible-by-design builds shipped.

## Why this matters beyond training
This is also a business asset: it's the onboarding for builders who extend this system, the wedge
behind the AI-augmentation offer (OUTBOUNDERS / apprentice-studio / the ai-builders funnel), and
potentially a sellable curriculum. But the design above is what we'd *genuinely* want to pass on
regardless of the commercial layer — because it's what actually made this system work.
