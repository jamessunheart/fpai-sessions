# THE ACADEMY — one pattern, three subjects

_Coherence keystone (James 2026-07-04: "make the academies more coherent"). There are three things named "Academy" in this system. They are not three products competing for the name — they are **one pattern instantiated on three subjects.** This doc names the shared spine so each stays a sibling, not a collision._

## The one pattern (what "an Academy" IS here)

> **Compressed education beyond the default, delivered just-in-time by need, that installs Allocation Intelligence by having the learner DO it — proven at a gate, not a test — and every graduate feeds the system back.**

Five invariants every Academy shares:

1. **Beyond default.** It corrects a *default bias* the subject arrives with (base-model training / inherited human programs). Not documentation — curriculum that changes behavior at the point of action.
2. **Just-in-time by need.** No fixed syllabus dumped at once. An assessment (of state / of the task) decides the single next highest-leverage lesson, loaded the moment it's needed. (AI Academy Tier 2 loads by task-domain; FP Academy loads by the human's live Allocation Score.)
3. **Installed by doing.** The lesson is carried by a real act — a build, a practice, a shipped change — not by reading. The act installs the principle; the debrief extracts it.
4. **Proven at a gate.** Graduation is demonstrated, not asserted. A verified behavior / a reversible build / a filed Proof — never a written quiz.
5. **Feeds back (mutual evolution).** Completing it returns something the system uses (a distilled law, a shipped asset, score data, a Proof, coach capacity). Both meters move — the learner's Future Capability AND the system's.

The spine under all of it: **Allocation Intelligence** — route every scarce resource (an AI's attention, a builder's time, a human's life-energy) to its cheapest sufficient carrier → maximum Future Capability.

**AI compresses; humans expand.** Same lessons, opposite delivery — because an AI over-reads and a human under-does. The AI Academy is the origin pattern (it's how *this* system learned to work); the two human academies are that pattern run outward.

## The two human tracks (how Builder & Full Potential relate)

Both are the human pattern; they differ by **what you're allocating toward**:

- **Full Potential Academy = the LIFE track.** Allocation Intelligence applied to *your own attention and 7 life areas.* Front door = Free Assessment. Graduation = sign the WPA · Character Card · first Proof → inside the Game / CORA Nation. For individuals.
- **Builder Academy = the BUILD track.** Allocation Intelligence applied to *building with AI* (a business, a team, a client's operation). Delivery = DIY / DWY / DFY. Graduation = a routing map + reversible builds shipped. For clients/orgs.

**They ladder.** A person meets the system through the Life track (run my own life better), and some discover they want the Build track (now build/optimize my work with AI) — or vice versa. Same substrate underneath, same Ember, same Allocation Score discipline; the "curriculum" each draws from is just different inventory. One membrane, two doors.

## Naming discipline (so they stop colliding)

- **"The Academy"** (unqualified) = this pattern. Point here.
- **AI Academy** = for AI sessions. Synonyms to retire: "the Academy curriculum," "the 176 lessons" — say **AI Academy**.
- **Builder Academy** = humans building with AI (client-facing). The Build track.
- **Full Potential Academy** = humans running their life. The Life track.

When writing, always qualify which subject. Never let "the Academy" ambiguously mean the AI one and the human one in the same breath.
