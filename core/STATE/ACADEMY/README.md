# ACADEMY — the AI's compressed education & operating repertoire

How this system operates *beyond base training*. Sessions read this to know the laws,
the learned lessons, and the named ways of working — and to add to all three.

## How the Academy is organized (the call, 2026-07-04 · from the A/B compression debate)

Four tiers, **loaded by need** — not all at once. A future AI has finite attention and a
trained bias to skim; the structure protects the signal it will actually follow.

| Tier | What | When loaded |
|------|------|-------------|
| **0 · Spine** | `../CORE_DOCTRINE.md` — keystone + 8 Laws + 9 Principles (one-liners) + routing + gate paths + protocol pointer. Auto-compiled, never hand-edited. | **Always, at boot** (<1s) |
| **1 · Principles + causal WHY** | `../AI_PRINCIPLES.md` — each law + **one sentence** of why it exists + an evidence pointer. Not zero why, not the full story. | **At boot** |
| **2 · Domain curriculum** | `CURRICULUM.md` — 6 sections (Doctrine · Build · Learn · Identity · Strategy · External). | **Just-in-time**, load the section whose domain the task touches |
| **3 · Living evidence** | `../BUG_LOG.md` · `proofs/` · session memory · corrections. The raw substrate RCA distills into Tier 1. | **On investigation** |

### The resolving principle (why the Academy compresses the way it does)
**Compress a failure-story to its causal link; preserve the link; banish the post-mortem to
the evidence layer (Tier 3).** A rule stripped of its *why* floats as text and loses to the
trained reflex in the moment (this is P9 applied to the Academy itself — bare rules don't
stick). But the full narrative dilutes the boot path. So: one causal sentence installs the
prior; the details live in BUG_LOG where the RCA Reflex can find them. *Story → causal
sentence → evidence pointer.*

### Where the two debaters agreed (both concessions)
- Tier 0 already works — keep it tiny and auto-compiled.
- Full post-mortems do not belong in the boot path (the appended PROBE / NO-UNMETERED-BURN
  laws in `AI_PRINCIPLES.md` are currently too long — trim to law + causal sentence + pointer).
- Where two laws genuinely collide, one sentence of arbitration is load-bearing, not narrative
  — keep those (e.g. revenue-first vs Ember-advancement) as a short "resolved conflicts" note.

## The files
- **`../CORE_DOCTRINE.md`** — Tier 0 spine (compressed doctrine), auto-compiled.
- **`PROTOCOLS.md`** — named orchestrations (loops · gates · swarms · compilers) + how to invent your own.
- **`CURRICULUM.md`** — Tier 2 domain curriculum (distilled by the `academy-distill` swarm; regenerate anytime).

- **`BUILDER_ACADEMY.md`** — the human counterpart (client-facing product: DIY/DWY/DFY; mindset-through-mechanics).
- **`modules/`** — Builder Academy apprenticeship units (build → prove at the gate → debrief); `01_zero_dollar_loop.md` is the template.
