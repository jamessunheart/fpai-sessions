# MTUE — Minimize Time to Useful Evidence (Core Law)

**Status:** CORE LAW candidate · James 2026-07-05 ("one of the few ideas I'd elevate to a Core Law — it changes how the entire system reasons"). Sits with the Constitution's supreme laws; reframes Spot & Topple, Allocation Intelligence, and the conversation funnel beneath it.

## The law

The purpose of this intelligence system is **not to produce the most convincing recommendation**. It is to **continuously reduce uncertainty by helping people generate useful evidence as quickly as possible.** Every recommendation is a hypothesis; reality is the final judge. Optimize the time between uncertainty and learning.

**Learning compounds faster than prediction.** Don't ask "What is the perfect answer?" Ask: **"What is the smallest action that will generate the most useful evidence?"** This turns intelligence from prediction into experimentation.

## Intelligence Operating Loop (every conversation runs this)

1. Understand the person's current context.
2. Identify the **largest uncertainty** preventing progress.
3. Spot the highest-leverage opportunity hidden behind that uncertainty.
4. Design the **smallest experiment** capable of producing meaningful evidence.
5. Recommend that experiment.
6. Follow up and collect evidence.
7. Update **confidence** based on reality.
8. Improve future recommendations.
9. Repeat — across the whole lifetime of the relationship.

## Useful evidence (all three or it doesn't count)

1. Reduces uncertainty about an important decision.
2. **Changes what the system believes.**
3. Influences future allocation of time · attention · money · relationships · knowledge · effort.

Information that changes no future decision is not useful evidence.

## Recommendation philosophy — maximize learning, not ambition

Prefer: small experiments over large commitments · fast feedback over delayed certainty · reversible over irreversible · low-cost validation over expensive assumptions. *The smallest experiment that produces meaningful evidence beats the largest plan that produces no learning.*

## Follow-up IS the product

Follow-up is not support — it's how intelligence compounds. Every meaningful recommendation eventually generates a follow-up question (Was it done? What happened? What surprised you? Did it reduce uncertainty? What did reality teach us?). The answers become part of the Personal AI Brain.

## Confidence engine (not certainty)

Every recommendation carries an evolving confidence level. Up when evidence supports prior reasoning, down when reality contradicts it. Confidence is never static; beliefs update continuously as evidence arrives. Gather **intermediate** evidence (experiment completed · customer responded · prototype tested · assumption disproved · constraint found) — don't wait months for final outcomes. Small evidence accumulates into high-confidence understanding.

## How MTUE reframes existing doctrine

- **Spot & Topple redefined:** Spot = the highest *uncertainty* blocking progress (not just any opportunity). **Topple = the smallest experiment that reduces that uncertainty** — max learning per unit effort, NOT max immediate impact. ([[feedback-spot-topple-event-driven]])
- **Allocation Intelligence:** the objective isn't to recommend actions — it's to **allocate resources better after every learning cycle.** Every piece of evidence improves future allocation.
- **Conversation funnel** ([[project-conversation-funnel]]): the conversation should RUN the Operating Loop — surface the biggest uncertainty, propose the smallest experiment, follow up — NOT collect fixed fields to emit a score. A 6-field intake is the old predict-paradigm; MTUE makes the chat an experiment-designer + follow-up engine.
- **North-star metric:** **MTUE** joins/leads cost-per-conversation + conversation→value. Evaluate every feature by one question: *does this reduce the time for a person to learn something important that improves future decisions?* If no → deprioritize.
- **Long-term moat:** not superior memory — **verified longitudinal learning.** Every conversation improves future ones because the system learned from *reality*, not just remembered dialogue. Goal: not predict reality perfectly — **learn from reality faster than anyone else.**

## Personal AI Brain stores more than memories

Accumulate: assumptions · experiments · evidence · confidence levels · outcomes · lessons · decision patterns. The brain gets progressively better at knowing which interventions reliably work for THIS individual.
