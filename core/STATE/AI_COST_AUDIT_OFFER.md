# AI Cost Reduction Audit — Offer + Outreach

**Buyer:** Companies spending heavily on customer service / back-office who want to cut cost without losing quality.
**Built on:** OneBPO operating experience (you already run this; you have the benchmarks).
**Why it converts fast:** outcome-based, risk-reversed, built on existing expertise — no new platform needed.

## The offer (one paragraph)

> We'll identify exactly where AI can reduce your customer-service and back-office costs while maintaining or improving quality — measured against real benchmarks from operations we run. You get a concrete map: which workflows to automate, projected savings, and a sequence. If we can't demonstrate value worth far more than the price, you don't move forward.

## Structure

- **Diagnostic audit:** fixed price (suggest $1,500–$3,000), 5–7 business days.
- **Deliverable:** workflow map + cost-reduction estimate + prioritized automation sequence.
- **Risk reversal:** "if we can't show value clearly worth more than the fee, you don't proceed to implementation."
- **Natural upsell:** implementation engagement (retainer or % of verified savings).
