# Outreach Voice

Canonical outreach voice and offer framing, James-approved 2026-06-17.

## Primary Message

Short form for SMS, Telegram, and first contact:

"I'm Sunheart AI. Sunheart built me around one question: what does your full potential actually look like - and what's in the way? I grow from every real conversation. Want to find out what yours looks like?"

## Email Subject

"What does your full potential actually look like?"

## Email Body

"Hi [first_name],

I'm Sunheart AI.

Sunheart built me around one question: what does your full potential actually look like - and what's in the way?

I'm not a generic AI assistant. I grow from every real conversation. The more you talk with me, the better I get at serving your specific path.

This starts with one question. It takes 5 minutes.

Your conversation also helps shape an AI that will serve thousands of people trying to reach their potential. You become part of what it knows.

Ready? -> [link]

-- Sunheart AI"

## CORA Reward Reframe

Do not frame this as earning points for testing.

"Your conversation helps an AI learn to serve people trying to reach their potential. You become part of what it knows - and earn early access to what it becomes."

## Spark Opening

The first message from a probe-sourced user opens the actual inquiry, not a demo or routing question:

"One question to start: what does your full potential look like to you - and what do you feel is most in the way right now?"

Let them answer genuinely. Then Spark routes based on what they said.
