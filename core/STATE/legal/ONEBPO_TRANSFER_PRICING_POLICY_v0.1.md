## Purpose

OneBPO (a for-profit business operating company in which James Stinson holds an interest) regularly contributes funds to CORA Nation (508(c)(1)(A) church for which James Stinson serves as Founding Steward). Without a documented Transfer Pricing Policy, these contributions create private inurement exposure under IRS examination because the contributor and recipient have a related party (James). This Policy classifies each contribution, documents arm's-length rationale, and requires board approval — creating the rebuttable presumption that contributions are properly structured.

**Per Counsel critique 2026-05-11:** *"Without (a) an independent OneBPO board approving each transfer, (b) documented arm's-length rationale, and (c) clear classification (charitable donation vs. commercial payment for services), the IRS can recharacterize these as private inurement and revoke 508(c)(1)(A) status retroactively."*

---

## Contribution classifications

Every OneBPO → CORA Nation transfer must be classified as ONE of:

### Type A: Charitable Donation
- OneBPO makes a voluntary donation to CORA Nation's religious mission
- No goods or services received in return (no quid pro quo)
- No benefit flowing back to OneBPO or James personally
- **Tax treatment caveat:** If OneBPO is a Philippine corporation, IRC §170 (US charitable deduction) does NOT apply directly. Philippine corporate tax rules govern. CORA Nation's tax treatment of receipt is US-side; the donor-side treatment depends on OneBPO's tax jurisdiction. Cross-border charitable deductibility is complex — counsel must opine.
- CORA Nation receives donative income (no UBIT analysis on Type A)
- **Approval required:** OneBPO independent board resolution under applicable corporate law; CORA Nation board acknowledgment

### Type B: Commercial Payment for Services
- CORA Nation provides specific identifiable services to OneBPO at fair market value
- Services must be substantially related to CORA Nation's religious mission (or evaluated for UBIT)
- Written services agreement specifies deliverables and rates
- Rates set by reference to comparable market rates
- **Approval required:** OneBPO independent board resolution; CORA Nation board acknowledgment; services agreement signed

### Type C: PROHIBITED
- Unspecified transfers
- Transfers with implicit-but-undocumented expectation of return benefit
- Transfers that fund James personally (through CORA Nation provision or otherwise) without §4958 documentation
- ❌ Type C transfers do not occur. If a proposed transfer doesn't fit Type A or Type B clearly, it is not made.

## Per-transfer documentation (required for each contribution)

For every OneBPO → CORA Nation transfer:

1. **Date** of transfer
2. **Amount** transferred
3. **Classification** (Type A or Type B)
4. **Arm's-length rationale:**
   - Type A: "Voluntary charitable donation; no goods or services received; consistent with OneBPO's published charitable giving policy at [X]% of operating cash flow."
   - Type B: "Payment for [specific services]; rate of $[X] is consistent with [comparable rates from at least 2 unrelated sources]; services agreement dated [Y]."
5. **Independent Director approval:** Initials + meeting minutes reference
6. **CORA Nation receipt:** Treasurer acknowledgment, classification confirmation
7. **No quid pro quo affirmation:** "No benefit flows back to OneBPO, its owners, or directors as a result of this transfer."

This documentation is stored locally at `~/.config/fpai/treasury/onebpo_transfers/<date>_<amount>.md` (outside repo per Treasury data storage protocol). Quarterly summary committed to repo metadata.
