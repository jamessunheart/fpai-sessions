## Council Lenses

### Pragmatist

The terms need to make the first Patron Engine launch operationally usable: what the member is buying, what is a gift, what gets recorded, and what the steward must fulfill. The strongest practical rule is the split between `membership_access` and `patron_gift` line items. It keeps checkout, receipts, and ledger records from mixing purchase consideration with mission support.

**Tightening applied:** added the "Two Separate Doors" section, a required member-access ledger, and a fulfillment-first patronage flow.

### Legal Skeptic

The main risk is accidentally selling future appreciation, pooled upside, equity-like rights, tax-deductible treatment, or "compounding utility value." A PMA wrapper does not save language that sounds like an investment. The document must say the prohibited thing plainly and must keep Brand LLCs, future offerings, tokens, and gifts separate from membership access.

**Tightening applied:** added explicit no-equity/no-return/no-securities clauses; prohibited positive return language; flagged tax-deductibility claims as counsel-gated; separated Brand LLC relationships.

### Member Advocate

Members need clarity, not only disclaimers. They should know what they receive now, how withdrawal works, what records they can inspect, what privacy protection applies, and how disputes flow. The terms should not use legal posture to hide fulfillment obligations.

**Tightening applied:** added member rights, member duties, records/privacy, refund/cancellation/transfer boundaries, withdrawal effects, and internal dispute access.

### Stewardship Lens

The Patron Engine exists to turn support into real utility and story while protecting James and the community from selling the dream prematurely. The phrase "Sell the standing thing. Gift the growing thing." should become a contract control, not only a strategy note.

**Tightening applied:** embedded the standing utility rule, launch review checklist, and communications rules for checkout, receipts, and copy.

---

## Synthesis

The draft is tight enough for internal merge review and counsel handoff, but it is not live legal language. The key protection is operational separation: every transaction must identify whether it is a present utility access purchase, a patron gift, or separate line items for both. The no-equity/no-return clauses are explicit, member rights are concrete, and launch is gated on counsel, James approval, checkout language, receipts, ledger fields, and a follow-up review of member-facing copy.

## Remaining Counsel Questions

1. Confirm the signing entity, governing law, notice method, and whether this should be an addendum to the existing PMA agreement or a schedule attached to each access item.
2. Confirm whether any gift acknowledgment, invitation, or member thank-you could affect donation/tax treatment.
3. Confirm refund, cancellation, and rescheduling language by offering type: event, stay, session, tool access, membership period, and gift.
4. Confirm whether any tier names imply priority, asset claim, investment rank, or future financial benefit.
5. Confirm whether the member-access ledger should include additional retention, privacy, or audit fields.
