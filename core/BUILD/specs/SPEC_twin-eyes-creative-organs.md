2. Add matched Twin Eyes observations for Music Magic, Sunstream, Sundance,

3. Preserve the existing historical regression corpus and unresolved inert-build

4. Keep every new observation literal, repo-confined, and non-actuating.

- Twin Eyes names all three creative organs and distinguishes their functions.
- Live corpus remains within its 20–40 observation bound.
- All observations have verified literal evidence.
- Existing conflict recall, labels, determinism, and no-actuation tests pass.
- No external process state is changed.

untouched.
