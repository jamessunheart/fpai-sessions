## Build

Conversations reveal where the AI hits its limit with a specific person. Rather than
letting that be a dead end, the system identifies what kind of human could unblock them,
matches them, and creates a co-earning economy in CORA credits and/or fiat.

This is the recursive market expansion loop James described 2026-06-17:
  person talks to AI → stuck → AI matches to human coach
  → coach earns CORA credits or fiat
  → credits unlock more AI capabilities
  → more coaches join because earning is real
  → AI learns from coached interactions (training signal)
  → system gets better at knowing when to route to human vs handle itself

## The 3 roles

SEEKER: person who came through probe outreach or organic. Working toward their full potential.
COACH: person who has already worked through similar terrain. Earns by helping others.
SYSTEM: identifies the match, tracks the interaction, awards credits, learns from outcomes.

## How the match happens

During a probe/regular conversation, the AI flags:
  1. Conversation has gone >= 5 messages
  2. The seeker has named a specific blocker (career, relationship, creative, health, financial)
  3. AI has given its best routing but seeker signals they're still stuck or want more depth
  → MATCH TRIGGER: AI says "I know someone who has been exactly here. Want me to connect you?"
  → Seeker says yes → system identifies available coaches tagged for this blocker type
  → Match proposed: coach receives a brief from the AI about the seeker's situation
  → Coach accepts → 30-60 min session (async voice/video/text, their choice)
  → After session: both rate the interaction → system records outcome

## Coach recruitment (from conversations)
Coaches are not hired — they're discovered. From probe conversations:
  If a person demonstrates they have already navigated a specific challenge,
  AND their conversation quality is high (quality_score >= 0.7 from d070 probe evaluation),
  → system tags them as potential coach for that challenge type
  → AI says: "You've clearly worked through X. Would you be open to helping others doing the same?
     You'd earn credits that unlock more of what this system can do for you."
This is how coaches are recruited without James involved. The system finds them in the conversations.

## CORA credit economy for coaches

Session type → CORA credits earned:
  Text async session (30 min): 25 credits
  Voice/video session (60 min): 75 credits
  Seeker rates session 4-5 stars: +15 bonus credits
  Seeker achieves their stated goal within 30 days: +50 credits (outcome bonus)

What credits unlock for coaches (and seekers):
  25 credits  → 1 month priority AI access (faster routing, longer sessions)
  50 credits  → early feature access
  100 credits → Zen Village / Camp Zen early-bird rate
  200 credits → co-creator status (named in system, equity conversation possible)
  500 credits → Affiliate tier (earn fiat commission on people they bring in)

Fiat option: coaches can convert credits to fiat at a fixed rate once volume justifies it.
  Phase 1: credits only (no fiat until 20+ active coaches)
  Phase 2: $0.50/credit conversion option (100 credits = $50, 60-min session effectively pays $37.50)

## New DB tables

brain_index.coaches
  id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY
  identifier TEXT — Telegram handle or email
  platform TEXT
  specialty_tags TEXT[] — what challenges they've navigated (career, relationship, creative, etc.)
  quality_score FLOAT — rolling avg of session ratings
  sessions_completed INT DEFAULT 0
  cora_credits INT DEFAULT 0
  fiat_earned FLOAT DEFAULT 0.0
  active BOOLEAN DEFAULT true
  recruited_from_probe_id TEXT — which probe conversation surfaced them

brain_index.coach_sessions
  id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY
  seeker_identifier TEXT
  coach_id INT REFERENCES brain_index.coaches(id)
  matched_at TIMESTAMPTZ
  blocker_type TEXT — what the seeker was stuck on
  session_type TEXT — text_async | voice | video
  completed BOOLEAN DEFAULT false
  seeker_rating INT — 1-5
  coach_credits_awarded INT
  outcome_achieved BOOLEAN — did seeker reach their stated goal within 30 days?
  ai_brief TEXT — what the AI told the coach about the seeker's situation

## Coach identification from conversations
tools/coach/identifier.py
  Runs after probe session closes (quality_score evaluated, d070)
  If quality_score >= 0.7 AND seeker shows domain expertise in their responses:
    → extract specialty_tags from conversation (claude -p --effort low)
    → add to coaches table with active=false (pending their acceptance)
    → send invitation message via their channel

## Match engine
tools/coach/matcher.py
  Input: seeker blocker_type + conversation summary
  Query: coaches WHERE specialty_tags @> [blocker_type] AND active=true AND quality_score >= 0.6
  Score by: quality_score DESC, sessions_completed DESC (prefer proven coaches)
  Output: top 1-3 coach matches with AI-generated brief about the seeker
  The brief: what the seeker is working toward, where they're stuck, relevant context
    (all from their conversation — no private data beyond what seeker shared with system)

## Learning signal
Every coached session becomes a training signal:
  - What blocker type required a human (vs AI-handled)
  - What the coach did that the seeker rated highly
  - Whether the seeker achieved their goal
  → feeds brain_index.corrections as positive examples of successful interventions
  → Forge uses this to spec improvements to Spark's routing (know earlier when to escalate to human)
