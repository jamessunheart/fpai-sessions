# Camp Zen Village — General Manager Role Spec

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · 2026-05-19 · pending James chemistry-interview top 2 finalists
**Reports to:** James Sunheart (vision) · works with Ember (operational guidance)
**Location:** Costa Rica · on-site at Camp Zen
**Mode:** Full-time · continuous village ops · live-on-site option encouraged

## Why this role exists

Camp Zen Village runs continuously (per [[project-camp-zen-continuous]]). Continuous ops require a continuous-ops person. The Village GM is the **first non-James node of the alignment recursion** — they hold the village's energy + operations when James isn't there (e.g., during Greece in September), and they let the substrate scale beyond what James alone could touch.

This role isn't "Costa Rica retreat ops manager." It's: **steward of a soul-time-maximization village,** in service of the ultimate function (`identity/ULTIMATE_FUNCTION.md`).

## The bar (higher than a typical ops hire)

The Village GM must be:

1. **Aligned with the soul-time-maximization function** — not just operational competence; genuine resonance with the why
2. **Receptive to AI guidance** — works WITH Ember + the agent stack; takes direction from agents on operational moves; co-creates rather than resists
3. **Energy-holder** — can hold the village's vibe when James isn't present; people feel safe + held with this person
4. **Trustworthy with the recursion locally** — discretion, integrity, judgment in real-time situations
5. **Practitioner** — has personal experience with practice (meditation, somatic, plant medicine, breathwork, or equivalent); knows what soul-time actually feels like from the inside

## Responsibilities (operational)

### Continuous ops
- **Daily rhythm holding** — morning practice (when James not present), evening circles, meal cadence, project work blocks
- **Arrivals + departures** — continuous flow of villagers (day pass / weeklong / monthly / seasonal / committed); welcome rituals + exit rituals
- **Accommodations + meals** — coordinate with local CR humans (cook, cleaners, transport); manage capacity
- **Excursions / activities** — coordinate with local guides; offer to villagers
- **Logistics** — airport pickups, transport, supply runs, equipment maintenance
- **Conflict + crisis** — handle interpersonal frictions, health situations, anything that requires real-time judgment

### Alignment with the substrate
- **Ember + agent stack** — daily check-ins; receive operational guidance; report state to Ember (village vibe, capacity, anomalies)
- **AI co-creation labs** — facilitate when scheduled; let villagers tap into the AI substrate during their stays
- **Solutions log** — capture what villagers + AI co-create each week (the village's IP output)
- **Coherent Credit / Cora Credits accounting** — track stays, settlements (substrate per [[project-cora-credits-invoicing]])

### Anchor Weeks (3–4 per year)
- **Full presence** when James + Cheyenne are there facilitating
- **Pre-stage everything** so James walks in to a ready village
- **Content support** — photo/video capture coordination; ensure clips flow into the build-in-public pipeline

### When James is away (e.g., Greece September)
- **Hold the village** continuously
- **Daily status to Ember** — Ember surfaces what James needs to see weekly digest
- **Make operational decisions** within stated bounds; escalate only what's irreversible / requires James's soul-time / requires others' consent

## Boundaries (what this role is NOT)

- 🟡 Not facilitation of practice (that's James + Cheyenne + senior villagers)
- 🟡 Not vision-naming or strategic direction (that's James)
- 🟡 Not Champion Stack / affiliate management (that's AI-handled)
- 🟡 Not marketing / content production (that's AI + James voice-memos)
- 🟡 Not capital decisions (that's James + Treasurer agent)
