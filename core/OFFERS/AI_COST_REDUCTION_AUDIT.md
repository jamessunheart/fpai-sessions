# AI Cost Reduction Audit — the first cash offer

> **This is Allocation Intelligence made concrete:** we allocate a real business's
> scarce resources (their AI/software/labor spend) toward higher capability and lower
> cost. The engine's first real signal → real dollar.

"I'll find 15–30% in savings across your AI tools, software, and offshore labor in 5
business days — a prioritized plan with the dollar figures. If I don't find more than
the audit costs, it's free."

## How we capture value from savings (the model — this is the whole game)

**The trap:** if you only hand someone savings, you gave the value away. Savings is
the HOOK, not the product. You use the savings to win the right to *run the spend*.
Three escalating capture layers — the audit opens all three:

**Layer 1 — Gainshare (capture a slice of the one-time win).**
Free/cheap audit → we take **25–30% of verified first-year savings**. Save them
$40k/yr → we capture $10–12k. Zero risk for them, aligned incentives, builds the
case study. Best for the first 1–2 proof-clients. *Weakness: one-time, hard to
enforce long-term. This is the wedge, not the business.*

**Layer 2 — Intelligence retainer (capture it continuously).**
The audit proves value once; spend drifts constantly (new tools, new hires, price
hikes). We become their ongoing **Financial/Operational Intelligence layer** — a
monthly retainer ($1–4k/mo) that keeps re-optimizing their allocation. *The savings
fund the fee* — it pays for itself, so it's an easy yes. Recurring revenue, and it's
literally Allocation Intelligence sold as a subscription.

**Layer 3 — Substitution (become the better allocation — biggest capture).**
The audit finds where AI can replace/augment their labor & tools. We don't just
advise it — we *provide* the AI-augmented service (the OneBPO/Outbounders wedge).
Their $10k/mo VA function → our AI-augmented version at $6k/mo. **They save $4k; we
capture $6k of recurring revenue that was going to their old provider.** Not a % of
savings — the whole re-allocated spend at a better price. The audit earns the right
to replace the spend.

**The path:** Layer 1 proves it → Layer 2 owns the relationship → Layer 3 owns the
spend (the real money). Each client also feeds the engine: their spend-patterns
become reusable intelligence (Two Deliverables Rule).

## Price (pick the entry point at approval)

  Proof over margin.
- **Then → Layer 1 + Layer 2:** small audit fee ($1,500, credited) + retainer at delivery.
- **Where it fits → Layer 3:** any labor/tool-substitution finding becomes a
  service-substitution pitch (highest-margin capture).
