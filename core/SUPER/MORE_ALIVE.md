# MORE ALIVE — persists
The final test of every move. Asked of the people a loop touched, letter by letter, in their words. No letter may be traded for another. Then Q is one signed observation — INCREASED / UNCHANGED / DECREASED — by beneficiaries and a witness, never by the doer.

M  Meaning       did anyone understand why this happened?
O  Openness      was anyone freer to say no, or to leave?
R  Relationship  do people who didn't know each other now?
E  Energy        did the loop leave more than it took (cook, farmer, soil)?
A  Agency        can the receiver decide what to do with what she got?
L  Love          was someone's well-being included who wasn't in the room?
I  Intelligence  is the next Build less wrong because of this record?
V  Vitality      were bodies fed with food they wanted?
E  Evolution     is what we do next different because of what this taught?
