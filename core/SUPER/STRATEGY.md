# SUPER — Strategy: the food level
(James direction 2026-09-03: "Super can become the strategy — supermarkets / farmers markets etc. Food level.")

## Why food
Everyone needs it daily → the floor never goes to zero. It is eaten, not hoarded → structurally biased toward circulation. Pérez Zeledón already has a food loop (farm → feria → soda → family → súper) → SUPER enters an existing network instead of building one. And regenerative vs extractive is visible fastest in food, so Q is easy to see.

## The ladder — every rung is a real test with a real place
| Build | Test | Humans in loop | Touches treasury? |
|---|---|---|---|
| #001 Feed 10 | One soda accepts SUPER for 10 plates; treasury redeems at the súper | 1 vendor + 10 eaters | yes (cash-out) |
| #002 Second hop | The soda spends SUPER with a feria vendor instead of cashing out | 2 vendors | maybe |
| #003 Feria loop | A customer pays a feria vendor in SUPER; that vendor spends it with the vendor beside them | 3+ | no |
| #004 Súper at the register | A supermarket accepts SUPER because its suppliers and customers already hold it | many | no |

Rule: no rung is attempted until the one below it has CLOSED with Q assigned. Each rung is its own Build Card.

## The metric that tells the truth
Revolutions before cash-out (R_co): how many hands SUPER passes through before someone redeems it at the treasury.
- R_co = 1 → you are paying for groceries through a longer pipe. Fine for #001. Not a currency yet.
- R_co ≥ 2 → SUPER carried value between two people who are not you.
- R_co rising over Builds → alive. Flat → decorative.
Treasury redemption is training wheels; the goal is for it to be used less each rung, not removed by decree.

## What the witness watches for at the food level
- Was the food wanted (not just accepted)?
- Was anyone depleted — the cook, the farmer, the soil?
- Did the surplus go somewhere that creates the next revolution, or into a drawer?
