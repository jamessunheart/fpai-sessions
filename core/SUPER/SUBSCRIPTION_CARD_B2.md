## What a customer gets (one card, one outcome)
**A persistent Super Agent that owns ONE outcome for you** — e.g. "keep my books current," "find and vet my next provider," "hold my Build to its release conditions" — and works it across sessions until done or transferred.

- **The deliverable is the unedited ledger.** Every action, block, and failure your agent hits is in your log, append-only. If the record needs editing to look good, we refund the month.
- **Rights, not roles:** your agent gets exactly the access the outcome needs, nothing more. You can read its permissions in one place at any time.
- **It never moves your money, signs, or speaks as you.** Every close is handed to a human you name.
- **Transfer is the promise on day one:** the whole system — files, prompts, scripts, ledger — is built to hand to you. If you leave, you leave with it working.
