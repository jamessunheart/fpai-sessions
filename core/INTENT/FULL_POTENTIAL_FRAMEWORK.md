## The Three Currencies

The Full Potential Game runs on three currencies that work together:

### 🟢 Proof
> What was witnessed and recorded.

- Agreements kept
- Transformations delivered
- Beauty created
- Resources circulated

Proof is the substrate. Without proof, everything else is rumor.

### 🔵 Trust
> Confidence earned through repeated proof and positive impact in your orbit.

- People actually move when you signal a priority
- Trust is *earned*, not granted; *kept*, not assumed
- Trust enables coordination without coercion

### 🟡 Cash
> Real money still matters.

- Flows toward Trust over time
- Regulated by the Treasury Principles
- Subordinate to Proof and Trust — never the primary signal

**The relationship:** *Proof generates Trust. Trust attracts Cash. Cash circulates to fund more Proof.* That's the **Virtuous Circulation Loop.**

## Protection Boundaries

The framework includes *what is off-limits even to the framework itself*:

- 🛡 **Score the proof, not the soul.** Levels mark practice; they never define personal worth.
- 🤐 **Private life is private.** Public roll lists what participants choose to make visible. Internal life is theirs alone.
- ✅ **Consent is non-negotiable.** All participation, all data, all acts of inclusion require explicit consent. Pre-checked boxes, deemed acceptance, urgency-driven enrollment — invalid.
- 🔒 **Data is sacred.** Personal data is held with reverence, not aggregated for extraction.
- ⚖️ **Power is accountable.** Stewards, Guardians, founders — held accountable to the same Agreement they ask others to keep.

These boundaries are *load-bearing*. Without them the Game becomes another extraction system in different clothing.
