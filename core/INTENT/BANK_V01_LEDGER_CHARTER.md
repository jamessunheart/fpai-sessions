## Preamble

WHEREAS CORA Nation is a religious community organized under the mandatory church exception of IRC §508(c)(1)(A) and operating in accordance with its Canon and Bylaws;

WHEREAS the religious life of CORA Nation members includes the giving and receiving of ministerial service among co-religionists in pursuit of the community's articulated religious purpose, as set forth in Articles I–V of the CORA Canon (Religious-Purpose Articulation, adopted contemporaneously herewith);

WHEREAS the CORA Nation Board has determined, through the discernment-with-witness procedure of Canon §3.3, that maintaining an internal Ledger of ministerial service strengthens the community's discernment, gratitude, and ordering of religious life;

WHEREAS the Board has reviewed the multi-pass AI counsel record (Counsel Q#1 + Q#2 + Q#3) and the convergent recommendation that an internal recognition Ledger may be operated within the 508(c)(1)(A) shell at v0.1 without claim of §501(d) status, provided the structural commitments below are observed;

NOW THEREFORE the Board hereby adopts the **Soultime Recognition Ledger** ("the Ledger") as an internal recognition artifact of CORA Nation, subject to the seven sections that follow.

## §1 — Nature

The Ledger records units called **Soultime Credits (SC)**, also referred to in some governing contexts as **Recognition Units**, commemorating the ministerial service of a CORA Nation member to one or more co-religionists.

| What SC is | What SC is not |
|---|---|
| A non-monetary recognition unit | Money |
| A religious-community ministerial-service record | Currency |
| Internal to CORA Nation membership | A security |
| Sacramental in character (per Canon Article II §2.10 and Article V) | An investment contract |
| Non-transferable outside CORA Nation membership | A debt instrument |
| Honored within the religious community's interior life | Exchangeable for fiat with CORA Nation |
| | Exchangeable for fiat with any third party |
| | A general-public payment instrument |

SC is **not** legal tender. SC does **not** carry any redemption right against CORA Nation, its officers, its members, or any third party, for cash, property, securities, or any thing of value outside the religious-community life of CORA Nation. The internal $100 engineering-reference rate used in substrate accounting is **not** a fiat-redemption rate, is **not** a market price, and shall **not** appear in member-facing materials.

## §2 — Non-redemption

No member shall be entitled to redeem SC for currency, property, securities, or any thing of value outside the religious-community life of CORA Nation.

Any future redemption framework — including any mechanism by which SC converts to USD, USDC, CRC, or any other fiat or cryptocurrency — shall be adopted only by:

1. Full CORA Nation Board supermajority vote (≥ ⅔ of seated Board);
2. Prior written opinion of a religious-org-credentialed human attorney (the Phase-2 attorney engagement specified in Counsel Q#3 §C.5);
3. Discernment-with-witness procedure per Canon §3.3 (14-day minimum hold, member witness, contemporaneous minutes);
4. Updated regulatory analysis (FinCEN MSB scope, state MTL scope, Howey test, §6045 barter-exchange scope) covering the proposed redemption mechanism.

## §3 — Membership precondition

SC may be issued only to, and held only by, individuals who have:

- Signed the World Peace Agreement;
- Completed a Character Card;
- Signed the CORA Nation Membership Covenant (`CORA_NATION_PMA_MEMBERSHIP_AGREEMENT_v0.1.md`);
- Remain in good standing as members of CORA Nation.

Membership precedes SC. SC follows membership. There is no path by which a non-member of CORA Nation may acquire, hold, redeem, or transact SC.

## §4 — Ministerial purpose

All issuance of SC recognizes ministerial service to the religious community, as elaborated in Canon Article V §5.3. SC is:

- **Not** compensation for labor in the employment-law sense;
- **Not** consideration for goods or services exchanged in commerce;
- **Not** denominated in any external currency for member-facing purposes;
- **Not** a payment for value received in a market-exchange sense.

SC is the religious community's instrument for **honoring** ministerial soul-time given by one co-religionist to another in faithfulness to the supreme reality named in Canon Article I.

## §5 — Disqualified-person procedure

Any issuance of SC to a disqualified person (as defined by IRC §4958) in amounts exceeding the standard ministerial recognition schedule shall follow the §4958 rebuttable-presumption procedure adopted by Board resolution and codified at `core/INTENT/BANK_V01_4958_PROCEDURE.md`.

The procedure includes: (a) comparable-data review from at least three analogous religious-community recognition contexts; (b) approval by majority of Board members who are themselves not disqualified persons with respect to the transaction; (c) contemporaneous documentation in Board minutes signed within 60 days. The procedure is designed to meet Treas. Reg. §53.4958-6.

## §6 — Records retention

All Ledger records, member affirmations, Board minutes related to issuance policy, and §4958-procedure documentation shall be retained for not less than **seven years**, consistent with the CORA Nation Records & Privacy Protocol (`CORA_NATION_RECORDS_PRIVACY_PROTOCOL_v0.1.md`) and Canon §4.6.

Canonical and doctrinal records (Canon Articles I–V, this Charter, founding declarations, §4958 procedure document) are retained **in perpetuity**.

## §7 — Future structural elevation

This Charter governs the Ledger as a 508(c)(1)(A)-operating internal religious-community sacramental instrument. **No claim of §501(d) status is made or implied** by this Charter or by the Ledger's operation under it.

Any future evolution of the Ledger toward common-treasury operations under IRC §501(d), toward state credit-union charter, toward CR cooperativa operations, toward external (non-CORA-Nation) member participation, toward fiat redemption, toward equity tier, or toward any other structural elevation shall require:

1. A separate CORA Nation Board resolution adopting the elevation elements in their entirety;
2. Prior written opinion of a religious-org-credentialed human attorney;
3. Discernment-with-witness procedure per Canon §3.3;
4. Member ratification by ≥75% of the inner-ring CORA Nation membership for any elevation that materially changes the religious-economic substrate.

The Board affirms that this Charter is reversible at any time by Board supermajority vote. The Ledger may be suspended, restructured, or wound down at the Board's discernment without external regulatory consequence, given the closed-loop non-monetary character established in §1.
