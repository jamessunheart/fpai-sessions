**BUTR is:**

- An investment, security, or yield-generating instrument

**All content, all community, all events, all merch, and all charity flow-through are accessible WITHOUT holding BUTR.**

### 5.1 Howey Four-Prong Analysis (new in v0.3)

Per *SEC v. Howey*, 328 U.S. 293 (1946) — controlling test for investment-contract securities. Each prong analyzed:

| Prong | Application to BUTR | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Investment of money** | Buyers acquire BUTR on open market. Acknowledged satisfied — fair-launch structure does not deny this prong; defense rests on remaining three. | Acknowledged |
| **2. Common enterprise** | See §5.2 — affirmatively severed at the structural level. | Severed |
| **3. Expectation of profits** | Hard-no marketing list (§7) prohibits all profit/yield/price-target language. Cultural identity framing explicitly disclaims appreciation expectation (§5 "BUTR is NOT" list). | Severed |
| **4. Efforts of others** | Brand LLC's promotional, content, and charitable activity is structurally severed from BUTR holder outcomes (see §5.2). Brand LLC's success or failure does not flow to BUTR holders through any contractual or structural mechanism. | Severed |

### 5.2 Common Enterprise Severance (new in v0.3 — addresses CRITICAL #2 from v0.2 critique)

Brand LLC structurally severs the Howey "common enterprise" prong as follows:

- **No pooling.** Brand LLC does NOT pool BUTR proceeds for any collective investment, treasury, or growth purpose. Any BUTR received by Brand LLC is either spent operationally on a near-immediate basis OR converted to USD/USDC and donated to Heart-of-Gold per §0.
- **No proceeds-tied promises.** Brand LLC does NOT receive BUTR in exchange for promises of brand-building activity, marketing intensity, content production, or any commitment that would link BUTR market price to Brand LLC efforts.
- **Brand LLC creative work is independent of BUTR's market price.** Brand LLC's Churn House operations, event hosting, charitable donations, and brand-building activity proceed on a fixed cadence regardless of BUTR's market performance. No public marketing references BUTR price, market cap, or trading volume.
- **Holders' market outcomes are not horizontally linked through any Brand LLC mechanism.** BUTR holders trade on the open market; their outcomes depend on the open market, not on any Brand-mediated pooling or shared-fortunes structure.
- **No vertical commonality.** Brand LLC does not share in BUTR market gains or losses. Brand LLC's revenue (if any) comes from merch sales, event tickets, and direct sponsorship — none of which are denominated in or settled in BUTR.

Counsel must confirm the actual operational structure (Brand LLC promotes BUTR content; if BUTR price rises) survives this prong. Mitigation: Brand LLC's public communications about BUTR are limited to cultural identity content, not market commentary. Any reference to BUTR market performance in Brand LLC channels is hard-no per §7.

### 5.3 Reves Note Severance (new in v0.3)

Per *Reves v. Ernst & Young*, 494 U.S. 56 (1990), notes and debt instruments are subject to a separate "family resemblance" test. BUTR is explicitly NOT:

- A note, bond, or debt instrument
- A promise of future delivery of any good, service, or asset
- An instrument that creates a creditor-debtor relationship between Brand LLC and any holder
- A redeemable claim against Brand LLC for any value

Brand LLC will not accept BUTR in exchange for any commitment, contingent obligation, or future deliverable. Any BUTR received is treated per §5.2.

### 10.5 Blue Sky / State Securities Placeholder (new in v0.3)

Federal Howey analysis (§5.1-5.3) addresses federal securities law. State Blue Sky law applies independently and may impose additional registration or exemption requirements even where federal Howey is severed.

**Known state vectors to address in counsel engagement:**
- **California** — Corporate Securities Law of 1968 (Cal. Corp. Code §25000 et seq.); CA DFPI enforcement posture on meme tokens
- **New York** — Martin Act (Gen. Bus. Law §352 et seq.) — NY AG has aggressive enforcement reach
- **Texas** — Texas Securities Act + State Securities Board; TX SSB has filed actions against meme-token promoters
- **Florida** — Florida Securities and Investor Protection Act
- **Massachusetts** — Section 410(a) registration / exemption analysis

**v0.3 commitment:** No public marketing or sales activity in any state until token counsel confirms Blue Sky position for that state. Token discoverability on open market does not constitute solicitation in a state, but Brand LLC's active promotion in a state may.
