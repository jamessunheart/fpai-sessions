# BUTR — Butter Utility & Trust Reserve

### White Paper • Version 1.0 • June 2025

---

## 0  Abstract

BUTR fuses meme-coin virality with tangible, regenerative value creation.
Holders own a dual-reserve treasury:
- **Floor Reserve (Gold):** 40-70 % of all assets kept in LBMA-accredited gold bars, guaranteeing instant global liquidity and a hard price floor.
- **Yield Reserve (Cows • Ghee • Land • Carbon):** cash-flowing, grass-fed dairy micro-farms that supply shelf-stable ghee, generate carbon credits, and uplift rural communities.

A quadratic-voting DAO determines the exact reserve split (never dropping below 40 % gold). Profits stream to holders in BUTR, while 50 % of every realised surplus is locked into a public-charity **Heart-of-Gold Trust** that funds school-meal programs and climate-positive agriculture.

---

## 1  Vision & Rationale
1. **Food security & dignity** – Deliver fortified ghee to mal-nourished regions.
2. **Climate-positive dairy** – Scale grass-fed herds that *sequester* carbon and slash methane by feeding real pasture, not corn.
3. **Wealth loop** – Convert internet laughter into the oldest stores of value (gold, land, cattle).
4. **Sacred silliness** – "To the MOOO-n!" humour lowers the crypto barrier and drives viral reach.
