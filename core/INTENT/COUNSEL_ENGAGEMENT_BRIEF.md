## 7 required deliverables (the engagement hour focuses here)

### 1. §501(d) Apostolic Association Analysis
**Question:** Does the Covenant Layer community-provision model (housing + meals + pastoral support to Stewards) trigger IRC §501(d) apostolic-association classification rather than 508(c)(1)(A) church classification?

**Why it matters:** §501(d) requires pro-rata taxation of members on community income — materially different economics. We need to know if our architecture survives §501(d) classification or requires restructuring.

**Counsel deliverable:** Determination + reasoning + structural implication.

---

### 2. State Charitable Solicitation Registration (50-state survey)
**Question:** CORA Nation's national online membership intake (`fullpotential.com/game`) solicits charitable participation. Which US states require registration before solicitation, and which religious-organization exemptions apply state-by-state?

**Counsel deliverable:** State-by-state registration requirement matrix; recommended registration sequence; estimated cost per state.

---

### 3. State Money Transmission License (MTL) Analysis for Coherent Credit
**Question:** Coherent Credit is closed-loop (CORA-only redemption, no member-to-member transfer, demurrage-based). Does this structure hold under all applicable state MTL regimes, particularly in states with significant CORA Nation membership?

**Counsel deliverable:** Determination of closed-loop exemption applicability; identification of state regulators most likely to have an opinion; recommended risk-mitigation steps.

---

### 4. Arm's-Length Structural Review (Brand LLC ↔ CORA)
**Question:** Does the relationship between Brand LLCs (Sapphire LLC, Camp Zen LLC, etc.) and CORA Nation — Stewards as both LLC owners and CORA members; Brand LLCs making voluntary charitable contributions; binary membership verification (status only, no balance check); no CORA equity in Brand LLCs — definitively sever private benefit and inurement risks under IRS standards?

**Counsel deliverable:** Determination + identification of any structural gaps; recommended language for Brand LLC Operating Agreement (skeletal template in v0.4 Addendum B.1).

---

### 5. §4958 Rebuttable-Presumption Documentation Confirmation
**Question:** The community-provision compensation template in v0.4 Addendum B.5 requires (a) comparable-compensation data, (b) independent board approval, (c) contemporaneous written documentation. Does this framework satisfy the IRS rebuttable-presumption standard for reasonable compensation?

**Counsel deliverable:** Confirmation OR identification of additional elements needed; recommended documentation cadence (per-Steward, per-year).

---

### 6. Costa Rica + US Tax Structure for Camp Zen
**Question:** Camp Zen operates a physical retreat business on 45 acres in Costa Rica. What is the proper CR legal entity structure (S.A., S.R.L., or other)? How does charitable contribution flow legally between the CR entity and US-based CORA Nation? What FBAR/FATCA obligations attach to US persons in the structure?

**Counsel deliverable:** Recommended CR entity structure; recommended contribution-flow mechanism; FBAR/FATCA compliance protocol; coordination needs with separate CR counsel.

---

### 7. Worker Classification for Dual-Compensation Stewards
**Question:** Stewards receive (a) community-provision from CORA Nation AND (b) Brand LLC salary or contractor pay. How does IRS classify the CORA provision component? How does DOL classify? How does CR labor law classify on-site workers? Does the dual structure work, or does it create misclassification risk?

**Counsel deliverable:** Classification determination for each compensation flow; documentation requirements; risk-mitigation steps.
