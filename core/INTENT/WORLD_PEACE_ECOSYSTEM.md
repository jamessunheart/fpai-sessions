## Layer Glossary

### 1. CHRIST — The Philosophy

The practice itself. Six commitments (Coherence, Healing, Regeneration, Intelligence, Service, Truth) that the manifesto establishes as the spine of everything below.

### 2. World Peace Organization — Stewardship + Coordination Layer

The institutional body that holds the mission. Operates the global coordination function: chapters, partnerships, events, AI coordination, education programs, regenerative projects, community infrastructure. *Not* a synonym for any single activation — it's the layer that holds and coordinates them all.

### 3. World Peace Agreement — Shared Values Layer

The covenant any party can form. Six commitments distilled (Non-Harm, Repair, Truth, Regeneration, Human Dignity, Service to Life). Operationalized via the [WORLD_PEACE_AGREEMENT.md](./WORLD_PEACE_AGREEMENT.md) template, [FORMING_AGREEMENTS.md](./FORMING_AGREEMENTS.md) protocol, and [AGREEMENTS/](./AGREEMENTS/) registry.

### 4. The Three Activation Pillars

**Layer 5 (AI for Peace) is elaborated in detail in [`WORLD_PEACE_AGREEMENTS_PROTOCOL.md`](./WORLD_PEACE_AGREEMENTS_PROTOCOL.md)** — six AI modules (Agreement Builder, Coherence Analyzer, Conflict Translator, Repair Guide, Peace Ledger, Cultural Translator) that operationalize "AI as coherence layer for agreements, not as ruler or judge."



Three distinct but parallel ways the WPO meets people:

| Pillar | Layer | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🎶 **World Peace Party** | Activation | Music, dance, joy, emotional release, social healing, human connection — the celebratory body of the work |
| 🌿 **World Peace Weekend** | Immersion | Retreats, fire circles, sauna, river, workshops, ceremonies, integration — the deep-practice body |
| 🧠 **AI for Peace** | Intelligence | Coordination, translation, education, conflict mapping, memory & systems, regenerative routing — the cognitive body |

These are not competing departments; they are complementary modalities. A given participant may engage with one, two, or all three.

### 5. Zen Village Prototype — Living Demonstration

The physical place in Costa Rica where the whole stack is being prototyped. Regenerative living, sacred gatherings, AI+humanity collaboration, nature, beauty, peace infrastructure, nervous-system healing. The proof-of-concept that the rest of the ecosystem points to and is informed by.

### 6. Media + Culture — The Storytelling Layer

How the practice spreads. Films, music, art, podcasts, interviews, transformation stories, viral peace content. Without this layer the ecosystem stays small; with it, the practice scales.

### 7. Regenerative Economy — The Flow Layer

```
Events → Community → Media → Participation → Regeneration
```

Money is fuel, not goal. Resources flow toward healing, beauty, peace, and life. Connects to existing infrastructure: TRUST token services (membership), Credits Gateway (stewardship), FPI (constitutional intelligence economy).

### 8. The Long-Term Vision

A world where intelligence serves life. A civilization organized around coherence instead of chaos. A culture where peace becomes lived reality.

## Naming Reconciliation

This ecosystem map *refines* the founder's original framing of "World Peace Party = World Peace Organization = World Peace Headquarters = Zen Village."

- **At the level of intent:** these all serve the same supreme purpose — paradise on Earth through cooperation. They are one mission.
- **At the level of function:** they play distinct architectural roles within that mission, as shown above. They are not interchangeable.

Both framings are valid. The first speaks to the unity of purpose; the second speaks to the differentiation of practice. The ecosystem holds both.
