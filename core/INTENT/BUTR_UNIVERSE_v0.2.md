## 0. Architecture decision (locked v0.2)

**BUTR operates as a STANDALONE Brand LLC.** Not under CORA Nation. Not on the Brand Stack.

Reasons:
- Token mechanic is structurally incompatible with CORA's closed-loop §501(d) substrate (v0.4 Brand Tok already killed tokens for CORA Brands)
- "Sexy Milkmaid + crypto degen" tonal range creates IRS / reputational contamination if folded under religious-nonprofit umbrella
- Different counsel needed (securities/token + employment + food + content platform — not the religious-nonprofit / securities team CORA engaged)

**Optional charity flow-through to CORA's Heart-of-Gold fund** = arm's-length donation from BUTR LLC, structured per Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum B.2 (charitable contribution agreement). NOT a Brand Stack license. NOT a CORA-controlled relationship. BUTR may publicly state "we donate X% of profits to Heart-of-Gold school-meal programs" — that's a donor relationship, not a structural linkage.

**Tonal consequence:** BUTR brand voice is independent. May reference CORA / Heart-of-Gold charitably but should not collapse the two brands or imply CORA endorses the "Sexy Milkmaid" content.

## 5. What BUTR Does and Doesn't Unlock (new section, addresses CRITICAL #2)

**BUTR is:**
- A tipping signal (one of many ways to support Milkmaids and the brand)
- A cultural identity marker (you're in the Churn Crew if you hold BUTR — but holding is not required for any access)
- A donation pass-through (Brand LLC donates % of any BUTR it receives to Heart-of-Gold)

**BUTR is NOT:**
- An access key (no content, perks, community participation, or events are gated on holding BUTR)
- A ticket to butter gifts (gift program is creator/Brand discretion, can go to ANY supporter)
- A claim on Brand LLC profits, revenue, or assets
- A right to leaderboard placement
- An investment, security, or yield-generating instrument

**All content, all community, all events, all merch, and all charity flow-through are accessible WITHOUT holding BUTR.**

This is the structural Howey defense. BUTR is appreciation, not access.

## 12. Missing risk vectors addressed (new section per Counsel)

| Risk | Mitigation in v0.2 |
|---|---|
| FTC influencer disclosure (16 C.F.R. Part 255) | Milkmaids who promote BUTR after receiving tips/gifts must disclose — codified in Content & Language Policy + Milkmaid agreement |
| State sweepstakes law (Churn Wars prizes) | Reframed Churn Wars as **community events**, not prize competitions. If prizes are awarded, separate sweepstakes-law review + no-purchase-necessary mechanic |
| Milkmaid crypto tip tax (IRS Notice 2014-21) | Milkmaid agreement includes tax notice + 1099-NEC issued for >$600/year |
| FBAR/FATCA | Brand LLC and Milkmaid wallets US-based unless counsel-cleared; offshore custody deferred |
| Costa Rica overlap | BUTR operations explicitly do NOT touch CR / Zen Village. Charity donations to Heart-of-Gold are USD/USDC transfers from BUTR LLC to CORA, no operational CR linkage |
| Section 230 / platform liability | Brand operates Churn House site as a hosting platform, NOT as content co-creator. Milkmaid content remains theirs |
| NFT addition | Explicitly deferred. Any future NFT layer triggers separate counsel review |
