## 1. Purpose

This document specifies the religious-community process by which an individual moves from interest in CORA Nation to formal Membership, and the process by which a Member may withdraw, be disciplined, be removed, or be restored.

These procedures are religious-community discernment practices, not employment screening, consumer onboarding, or investor qualification. The substantive aim is mutual discernment between the inquirer and the Community as to whether the inquirer's call and the Community's life are in alignment.

---

## 2. Membership Tiers and Pathway

```
Inquirer  →  Apprentice  →  Member  →  Steward (optional)  →  Elder (optional)
                                                   ↘
                                                    Former Member (on exit)
```

| Tier | Entry act | Minimum duration before next tier | Key practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inquirer** | Sign World Peace Agreement (WPA) | None | Reading Canon; attending open gatherings |
| **Apprentice** | Sign PMA Membership Agreement; begin Character Card Quest | 90 days minimum | Character Card; first Mirror Witnessing pairing; participation in Community life |
| **Member** | Complete Apprenticeship; receive Pastoral Council recognition | None | Full participation; voting rights |
| **Steward** | Per Pastoral Leadership Structure §4 (separate recognition process) | 1 year as Member minimum (waivable) | Role-specific duties |
| **Elder** | Pastoral Council recognition (Steward emeritus or long-serving Member) | None | Pastoral counsel function |

## 3. Inquirer Stage

### 3.1 Entry
Any person may become an Inquirer by signing the World Peace Agreement at the Community's designated entry channel (currently `https://fullpotential.com/game`). Signing the WPA is the religious covenant act.

### 3.2 Activities Available to Inquirers
An Inquirer may:

a) Attend open Community gatherings (Ecstatic Weekend public sessions, Saturday Casual, public ceremony);
b) Read all publicly published Canon material;
c) Begin reading the Coherent Champions Manifesto;
d) Begin a Character Card draft;
e) Speak with a Pastoral Council member or designated Steward about Membership.

### 3.3 Activities Not Available to Inquirers
An Inquirer does **not**:

a) Have voting rights;
b) Earn Coherent Credit;
c) Earn or hold Allocation Weight;
d) Have access to closed-Community pastoral practice;
e) Reside at the Zen Village substrate beyond visitor terms;
f) Hold any Steward role.

### 3.4 Duration
There is no maximum Inquirer duration. An Inquirer may remain in this stage indefinitely. An Inquirer who has not advanced to Apprenticeship within two (2) years may be asked to re-affirm interest by the Pastoral Council.

---

## 4. Apprentice Stage

### 4.1 Entry
An Inquirer becomes an Apprentice by:

a) Completing an initial conversation with a Pastoral Council member or designated Steward;
b) Signing the **PMA Membership Agreement** (Constitutional Pack Document #3);
c) Affirming initial Character Card draft;
d) Receiving Pastoral Council acknowledgment (recorded in Community records).

### 4.2 Minimum Duration
Apprenticeship lasts not less than ninety (90) days from PMA Membership Agreement signing. The Pastoral Council may extend Apprenticeship in pastoral discretion.

### 4.3 Apprentice Practices
During Apprenticeship, the Apprentice shall:

a) Complete the Character Card per the Character Card Quest discernment practice;
b) Enter a first Mirror Witnessing pairing per the Mirror Constitution v1.0 (within the Apprenticeship period; ideally first 30 days);
c) Read and discuss with a Steward or Pastoral Council member: the Coherent Champions Manifesto; Canon v0.1; the Bylaws; this document; the Conflict of Interest Policy;
d) Participate substantively in at least three (3) Community gatherings during the Apprenticeship period;
e) Make a sustained good-faith effort to live the Coherent Champions Manifesto principles.

### 4.4 Apprentice Rights
An Apprentice may:

a) Vote on local-Circle matters per Bylaws §2.05 if assigned to a Working Circle;
b) Earn Coherent Credit per the Covenant Layer rules;
c) Earn Allocation Weight at a discounted accrual rate per the Allocation Platform spec (when ratified);
d) Reside at the Zen Village substrate subject to Pastoral Council and Stewardship Council approval;
e) Access pastoral care from the Pastoral Council and Pastoral Stewards.

### 4.5 Apprentice Limits
An Apprentice does **not**:

a) Vote in Community-wide Member meetings;
b) Hold a fiduciary Steward role;
c) Sit on the Stewardship Council or the Pastoral Council;
d) Speak for CORA Nation publicly without Pastoral Council authorization.

### 4.6 Withdrawal from Apprenticeship
An Apprentice may withdraw at any time per §8 below. Withdrawal during Apprenticeship is without prejudice; the individual may return to Inquirer status or re-enter Apprenticeship later.

## 5. Member Stage

### 5.1 Recognition as Member
An Apprentice is recognized as a Member upon:

a) Completion of the Apprentice practices in §4.3;
b) Pastoral Council determination, made in pastoral discernment with the Apprentice, that the Apprentice is ready for Membership;
c) Acknowledgment of the Apprentice's affirmed Character Card;
d) Recognition at a Community gathering (Recognition Ceremony — see §5.4);
e) Update of Community records to reflect Member status.

### 5.2 No Veto by Membership
Membership recognition (Apprentice → Member transition) is a pastoral determination, not a Member vote. The Membership may not vote against an Apprentice's recognition as a Member, except by formal motion for cause per Bylaws §5.02 (and only on the basis of conduct contrary to the Coherent Champions Manifesto or Bylaws, not on the basis of personal preference).

**Scope of this section.** This §5.2 applies only to the Apprentice → Member recognition transition. Recognition of Members as Stewards in fiduciary roles remains subject to Member ratification per Bylaws §5.01(c) and Pastoral Leadership Structure §4.3(c). This §5.2 does not restrict, modify, or supersede the Member voice in Steward recognition.

### 5.3 Member Rights and Obligations
A Member has the rights and obligations specified in the PMA Membership Agreement, the Bylaws, and the Canon. In summary:

a) Full voting voice in Community-wide Member decisions per Bylaws Article V;
b) Continued Mirror Witnessing practice;
c) Substantive participation in religious-community life;
d) Compliance with the Conflict of Interest Policy as applicable;
e) Confidentiality of pastoral and Member-sensitive matters per the Records & Privacy Protocol.

### 5.4 Recognition Ceremony
The recognition of a new Member is solemnized in a Community ceremony per Canon practice. The ceremony marks the threshold from Apprenticeship to Membership and is conducted by a Pastoral Council member or designated Steward.

### 5.5 Annual Renewal
At each Annual Meeting, Members re-affirm their Membership through a designated renewal practice (e.g., signing a renewal entry in the Community records or making an in-meeting renewal affirmation). A Member who does not renew at the Annual Meeting or within thirty (30) days thereafter shall be contacted pastorally by the Pastoral Council; sustained non-renewal may result in inactive-Member status (§7).

## 7. Inactive Membership

### 7.1 Designation
A Member who has not participated in Community life for twelve (12) consecutive months and has not renewed at the Annual Meeting may be designated **Inactive** by the Pastoral Council, with notice to the Member.

### 7.2 Effect
An Inactive Member:

a) Retains formal Membership but does not vote in Member decisions;
b) Does not accrue new Coherent Credit or Allocation Weight (decay continues to apply);
c) Remains in the Community records but does not count toward quorum.

### 7.3 Restoration to Active Status
An Inactive Member may be restored to active status by re-engaging with Community life and notifying the Pastoral Council in writing. No formal re-application is required.

### 7.4 Termination After Sustained Inactivity
An Inactive Member who has been inactive for thirty-six (36) consecutive months may be removed from Membership by the Pastoral Council with thirty (30) days' notice. Removal at this stage is without prejudice; the former Member may re-apply through the Inquirer path.

## 8. Voluntary Withdrawal

### 8.1 Notice
A Member or Apprentice may withdraw by written notice to the Secretary, with copy to the Pastoral Council.

### 8.2 Withdrawal Conversation
The Pastoral Council shall offer a withdrawal conversation to the withdrawing Member before withdrawal takes effect. Acceptance of the conversation is optional; the conversation does not delay withdrawal.

### 8.3 Effective Date
Withdrawal takes effect thirty (30) days after notice. During this period the Member remains subject to the obligations of the PMA Membership Agreement and the Bylaws.

### 8.4 Effect on Holdings
Per PMA Membership Agreement §7.3:

a) Coherent Credit balance lapses to the Community pool on the effective date of withdrawal;
b) Allocation Weight (when implemented) lapses to the Community pool;
c) Personal property of the Member at the Zen Village substrate is returnable to the Member;
d) Confidentiality obligations survive indefinitely.

No monetary value is associated with Coherent Credit or Allocation Weight that would be paid out on withdrawal.

### 8.5 Re-admission After Voluntary Withdrawal
A former Member who withdrew voluntarily may re-apply for Membership through the Inquirer path (§3). The Pastoral Council may, in pastoral discretion, waive or shorten the Apprenticeship requirement for former Members in good standing at the time of withdrawal.

## 9. Discipline and Removal for Cause

### 9.1 Grounds
Discipline or removal for cause may be initiated for:

a) Sustained, documented violation of the Coherent Champions Manifesto;
b) Material violation of the Bylaws, this document, or the Conflict of Interest Policy;
c) Conduct demonstrating sustained unwillingness to walk in the Community's covenant;
d) Conduct that harms other Members or the Community;
e) Conduct that exposes the Community to material legal, financial, or reputational risk.

### 9.2 Initiation
Discipline may be initiated by:

a) The Pastoral Council on its own motion;
b) Written request to the Pastoral Council from any Member describing conduct meeting §9.1 grounds;
c) Notification from the Stewardship Council where the conduct involves fiduciary matters.

### 9.3 Process
Per Pastoral Leadership Structure §6.2, the Member subject to discipline shall receive:

a) Written notice of the conduct at issue;
b) Opportunity to respond in writing or in person before the Pastoral Council;
c) Pastoral mediation prior to any formal discipline decision;
d) A decision in writing setting forth the basis and any conditions for restoration;
e) Right of appeal to the Pastoral Council in plenary session.

### 9.4 Stewardship Council Concurrence
For Members holding fiduciary Steward roles, removal requires joint Pastoral Council and Stewardship Council concurrence per Pastoral Leadership Structure §4.6.

### 9.5 Member Supermajority Path
A Member may also be removed by Member supermajority per Bylaws §5.03(c).

### 9.6 Effect of Removal
Removal for cause has the same effect on holdings as voluntary withdrawal per §8.4. The Pastoral Council may specify conditions on re-admission (§10).

### 9.7 Confidentiality of Discipline
The substance of pastoral discipline proceedings is confidential per the Records & Privacy Protocol and clergy-penitent privilege as applicable. The Community records reflect the fact of discipline and outcome but not the substance.

---

## 10. Restoration After Removal

### 10.1 Restoration Path
A removed Member may seek restoration through:

a) The Pastoral Council process specified in the discipline decision;
b) Pastoral accompaniment for not less than ninety (90) days;
c) Demonstrated change of practice consistent with the Coherent Champions Manifesto;
d) Restitution where the conduct involved harm to other Members or Community resources;
e) Re-recognition through the Apprentice or full Member-recognition process.

### 10.2 Pastoral Council Determination
The Pastoral Council determines, in pastoral discernment, whether restoration is granted. Restoration is restorative in intent and shall not be unreasonably withheld where the conditions are met.

## 11. Special Cases

### 11.1 Minors
CORA Nation does not admit minors as Members. Children of Members may participate in family-friendly Community life under parent supervision; formal Membership requires age 18+.

### 11.2 Joint Membership of Romantic Partners
Romantic partners apply for Membership individually. Both members of a partnership may be Members. Pastoral care of partnerships is provided per Canon practice.

### 11.3 Members Resident Outside the United States

Membership is open to persons resident outside the United States subject to (i) ability to participate substantively in religious-community life (whether remotely or by periodic visit), (ii) compliance with applicable national laws governing religious-association membership, and (iii) for Costa Rica residents, compliance with Costa Rica labor and immigration law for any residence at the Zen Village substrate.

**§11.3(a) High-Risk Jurisdiction Bright-Line.** Allocation Weight activation, Coherent Credit activation, and any in-Community economic-instrument participation shall NOT be available to Members resident in the following jurisdictions until the Allocation Platform spec and Coherent Credit operations have been reviewed by counsel licensed in (or competent on the law of) that jurisdiction:

a) European Union member states (subject to Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) and successor regimes);
b) United Kingdom (subject to FCA cryptoasset regime);
c) People's Republic of China;
d) Any jurisdiction on the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) "Call for Action" or "Increased Monitoring" lists from time to time;
e) Any jurisdiction subject to comprehensive U.S. sanctions (currently including Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, and Crimea/DNR/LNR regions of Ukraine);
f) Any other jurisdiction the Counsel Liaison and external counsel identify as creating material foreign-securities, foreign-money-transmission, or foreign-AML exposure.

A Member resident in any §11.3(a) jurisdiction may still hold full religious-community Membership and participate in ceremonial, doctrinal, and pastoral life; the restriction is solely on economic-instrument participation pending jurisdiction-specific counsel review.

**§11.3(b) External Counsel Referral.** The Counsel Liaison (an internal Officer) is responsible for flagging foreign-Member exposure cases but is NOT responsible for making the substantive determination of securities, tax, or money-transmission applicability. Any case where a prospective or existing Member's foreign residency may create material foreign-securities, foreign-tax, foreign-money-transmission, or foreign-AML exposure beyond §11.3(a) shall be referred to external counsel licensed in (or competent on the law of) that jurisdiction before the Membership determination is finalized. The Counsel Liaison's role is to identify and refer, not to opine.

### 11.4 Members Who Are Also Brand LLC Investors
A Brand LLC investor (per Reg D or Reg CF) may also be a CORA Nation Member, subject to:

a) The two relationships being demonstrably independent — Membership not conditioned on, induced by, or marketed in connection with the Brand LLC investment;

b) The Brand LLC investment and Membership relationships being **temporally separated** — neither occurring within the immediate context of the other;

c) Compliance with the Conflict of Interest Policy at all times;

d) Acknowledgment in writing by the Member that the two relationships are separate per PMA Membership Agreement §2.3 and §8.6.

This case is referred to Counsel during the Counsel engagement per the Brand Tokenization v0.4 activation preconditions.

## 12. Records of Admission and Exit

The Secretary maintains the **Membership Roll**, recording for each individual:

a) Tier (Inquirer / Apprentice / Member / Steward / Elder / Former / Inactive);
b) Date of each tier transition;
c) Signed PMA Membership Agreement on file (with version);
d) Character Card on file;
e) Mirror Witness pairing record;
f) Renewal record per Annual Meeting;
g) Discipline and restoration history (substance confidential per §9.7);
h) Date and basis of withdrawal or removal (if applicable).

Member access to one's own record is per the Records & Privacy Protocol.
