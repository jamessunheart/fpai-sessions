> *This Agreement is the legal-association act by which an individual becomes a Member of CORA Nation. It is signed in addition to (not in lieu of) the World Peace Agreement (which is the religious covenant act). Both acts together constitute Membership.*

---

## 1. Parties

This Private Membership Association Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into between:

**CORA Nation** — an unincorporated religious membership association self-declared under Internal Revenue Code § 508(c)(1)(A), operating per the Bylaws of CORA Nation v0.1 (the "**Community**"); and

The undersigned individual seeking Membership in the Community (the "**Member**").

---

## 2. Religious Character of This Agreement

### 2.1 Religious-Community Membership
This Agreement is an act of **religious-community membership**. The Member, in signing this Agreement, joins CORA Nation as a religious community organized under the First Amendment freedom of association, federal church-doctrine protections (IRC § 508(c)(1)(A)), and common-law association doctrine.

### 2.2 Not an Investment, Partnership, or Consumer Transaction
This Agreement is **not**:

a) an investment, securities purchase, subscription agreement, or any other transaction within the meaning of the Securities Act of 1933, the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, or any state securities laws;

b) a partnership, joint venture, or business association of any kind;

c) a consumer contract for goods or services subject to consumer protection laws;

d) an employment, independent contractor, or agency agreement;

e) a license, franchise, or similar commercial arrangement.

The Member acknowledges that no person at CORA Nation or representing CORA Nation has represented to the Member that any economic return, monetary profit, appreciation of holdings, or commercial benefit will flow to the Member from this Agreement or from Membership.

### 2.3 Distinction from Brand LLCs
The Member acknowledges that CORA Nation operates separately and at arm's-length from any **Brand LLC** (a for-profit limited liability company operated by Stewards or other persons). Any investment, employment, consumer purchase, or other commercial relationship the Member may have with a Brand LLC is governed by separate agreements with that Brand LLC and is not part of this Agreement or of Membership in CORA Nation.

## 4. Rights Conferred on the Member

Subject to the Bylaws, this Agreement, and the Admission & Exit Procedures, Membership confers the following rights:

### 4.1 Religious-Community Participation
The right to participate in all religious-community gatherings, ceremonies, and sacramental practices of CORA Nation as a Member in good standing.

### 4.2 Pastoral Care
The right to receive pastoral care and Mirror Witnessing from the Pastoral Council and recognized Stewards.

### 4.3 Governance Voice
The right to participate in Member meetings and to vote on matters within Member voice per Article V of the Bylaws, subject to the tier-graduated voting structure in Article II §2.02.

### 4.4 Coherent Credit
The right to earn and hold Coherent Credit per the Covenant Layer of Remarkably Coherent Treasury v0.10, subject to the closed-loop, non-transferable, CORA-only-redemption design of that instrument.

**This right is subject to activation of the Covenant Layer per Treasury v0.10 §Six Key Preconditions, including external religious-nonprofit counsel review and sign-off.** Until counsel sign-off on the Covenant Layer, Coherent Credit accrues to the Member's record in a non-activated ledger and is not distributable or redeemable. Once the Covenant Layer is activated by counsel sign-off and Member supermajority ratification, accrued non-activated Credit converts to active Credit per the Covenant Layer activation procedure.

### 4.5 Allocation Voice
When and if the Allocation Platform spec is **ratified by Member supermajority per Bylaws §5.03(a) following external counsel sign-off**, the right to earn and exercise Allocation Weight per the rules of that platform, subject to the hard-rules of Bylaws Article V §5.05 and §5.06 (non-transferable for value, recusal applies, concentration limit applies).

Until such ratification, this right is reserved but not active; the Member is not granted any Allocation Weight or related instrument before the platform spec is ratified.

### 4.6 Access to Records
The right to inspect Community records pertaining to the Member's own participation, financial transactions involving the Member, and the records described in the Records & Privacy Protocol as Member-accessible.

### 4.7 Access to Zen Village Substrate
The right to visit, reside (subject to Pastoral Council and Stewardship Council approval), and participate in religious-community life at the Zen Village substrate, subject to the operational rules of that location.

## 5. Obligations of the Member

By signing this Agreement, the Member accepts the following obligations:

### 5.1 Bylaws and Canon
The Member shall comply with the Bylaws, the Canon, and the policies adopted by the Community.

### 5.2 Conflict of Interest Disclosure
If the Member becomes a Steward, Council member, or otherwise enters a role subject to the Conflict of Interest Policy, the Member shall make required annual and event-driven disclosures per that Policy.

### 5.3 Recusal
The Member shall recuse from any decision in which the Member has a material economic interest as defined in the Conflict of Interest Policy, regardless of whether such recusal is technically enforced by the Allocation Platform.

### 5.4 Confidentiality
The Member shall maintain confidentiality of pastoral communications, sensitive Member information, and Community deliberations marked confidential, except as required by law or as permitted by the Records & Privacy Protocol.

### 5.5 No Holding Out
The Member shall not represent that the Member holds an investment interest, ownership, or partnership position in CORA Nation or any related entity. The Member shall not solicit investments on behalf of CORA Nation.

The Member may, separately and not in connection with CORA Nation membership, invest in a Brand LLC under that Brand LLC's own separate offering. "Discuss such investment in accordance with applicable securities law" — as used in this section — means **solely** that the Member may describe the existence of a Brand LLC offering in response to unsolicited inquiry from another person. This paragraph does NOT authorize the Member to:

a) Solicit investments in any Brand LLC or any other security;
b) Receive compensation, finder's fees, referral fees, or other remuneration for referring investors to any Brand LLC offering;
c) Act as an agent, broker, or representative for any Brand LLC in connection with any offering;
d) Make any representations regarding the merits, expected returns, or suitability of any Brand LLC investment.

Any activity described in (a)-(d) requires compliance with applicable broker-dealer registration requirements under Sections 15(a) and 29(b) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and applicable state Blue Sky laws, which the Member shall satisfy independently before engaging in such activity. CORA Nation does not register, sponsor, or supervise any Member's securities activity, and the Member is solely responsible for compliance.

### 5.6 Substantive Religious Participation
The Member shall participate substantively in religious-community life and shall not treat Membership as a nominal status.

### 5.7 Acceptance of Discipline
The Member accepts the Pastoral Council's authority over discipline and restoration per the Admission & Exit Procedures.

## 6. Intra-Association Activity and Dispute Resolution

### 6.1 Intra-Association Nature
The Member acknowledges that all activity conducted within CORA Nation as Members — including ceremonies, witnessing, allocation voting, Coherent Credit transactions, and the receipt of pastoral care — is intra-association activity protected under the First Amendment freedom of association, IRC § 508(c)(1)(A), and applicable PMA doctrine.

### 6.2 Religious-Community Dispute Resolution
Any dispute among Members, or between a Member and the Community, arising out of or related to this Agreement, the Bylaws, the Canon, or the Member's religious-community participation, shall be resolved in the first instance through the religious-community dispute resolution process set forth in the Admission & Exit Procedures (Constitutional Pack Document #5), which includes:

a) Direct conversation with the affected Member or Steward;
b) Pastoral mediation by the Pastoral Council;
c) Stewardship Council review for matters with fiduciary implications;
d) Member supermajority review for matters of general application.

### 6.3 Limited External Resort

**6.3(a) Internal Process — Maximum Timeline.** The internal religious-community dispute resolution process described in §6.2 shall be completed within ninety (90) days of the initial written invocation of that process by the Member. If the process is not resolved within ninety (90) days, the Member may seek external resolution without further obligation to exhaust internal process, except where the Member's own conduct caused the delay.

**6.3(b) Conditional Pre-Suit Exhaustion.** The Member agrees not to seek external (civil court) resolution of any intra-association religious-community dispute except after good-faith exhaustion of the religious-community dispute resolution process described in §6.2 (subject to §6.3(a) timeline), and only for matters that (i) involve criminal conduct, (ii) involve civil rights protected by applicable law, (iii) are by law not subject to religious-community resolution, or (iv) involve disputes with third parties not subject to the religious-community process.

**6.3(c) EEOC and Civil-Rights Carve-Out.** Notwithstanding §6.3(b), no provision of this Agreement shall be construed to require pre-dispute waiver, mediation, or arbitration of claims that by law cannot be pre-disputed waived, including but not limited to claims under Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, the Americans with Disabilities Act, the Age Discrimination in Employment Act, state human-rights statutes, sexual-harassment claims, retaliation claims, or any other federal or state civil-rights claim. The Member may file such claims with the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, state human-rights agencies, or directly in court without exhausting internal process or any other condition under this Agreement.

**6.3(d) No Effect on Counsel Engagement.** Nothing in this §6.3 restricts the Member's right to consult with personal counsel at any time, before or during any internal process.

### 6.4 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflicts-of-law principles, subject to the federal First Amendment and IRC protections that govern religious-community associations. To the extent CORA Nation maintains operations in Costa Rica (the Zen Village substrate), Costa Rica law governs solely the employment, residency, and tax matters arising from those operations.

Florida is selected as the governing-law jurisdiction because: (i) CORA Nation is domiciled in Florida (Bylaws §1.02); (ii) Florida has notarized founding declarations of CORA Nation's existence; (iii) Florida's Religious Freedom Restoration Act (F.S. 761.01 et seq.) and Church Property Act provide strong protections for religious associations; and (iv) Florida's religious-organization exemption from charitable-solicitation registration (F.S. 496.403) applies to CORA Nation's intra-Member religious-community activity.

## 7. Term, Withdrawal, and Termination

### 7.1 Term
This Agreement is effective on the date of signing and continues until terminated per this Article.

### 7.2 Voluntary Withdrawal
The Member may withdraw from Membership at any time by written notice to the Secretary. Withdrawal becomes effective thirty (30) days after notice, during which period the Member remains subject to the obligations of this Agreement.

### 7.3 Effect of Withdrawal on Holdings
Upon withdrawal:

a) Coherent Credit balance lapses to the Community pool (no cash redemption, no transfer);
b) Allocation Weight (when implemented) lapses to the Community pool per the Allocation Platform spec;
c) Personal property of the Member at the Zen Village substrate is returnable to the Member;
d) Confidentiality obligations under §5.4 survive withdrawal indefinitely.

The Member acknowledges that no monetary value is associated with Coherent Credit or Allocation Weight that would be paid out on withdrawal.

### 7.4 Pastoral Discipline and Removal
The Pastoral Council may suspend or terminate Membership for cause per the Admission & Exit Procedures, with appeal rights set forth therein.

### 7.5 Re-admission
A former Member may seek re-admission per the Admission & Exit Procedures.
