## 1. WHAT THIS IS

The Full Potential Game is a proof-based operating system for human potential. It makes the work people do for each other — the agreements kept, the offers delivered, the transformations witnessed, the resources circulated — *visible, scored, and recognized* in a way the dominant economy cannot do.

**Who it's for.** Builders, facilitators, founders, artists, operators, coaches, space-holders, and community leaders who are tired of proving themselves through noise and are ready to prove themselves through useful output.

**The player promise.** If you play well, three things happen:

1. Your life gets more coherent.
2. Your work becomes easier to trust.
3. More resources can flow through you without corrupting you.

That is the offer: coherence, trust, and a greater capacity to steward what flows through you.

---

## 2. WHAT THIS IS NOT

**This is not a social credit system.**

The Game does not score your soul, your worth, your private life, your personality, your trauma, your beliefs, or your potential. It scores witnessed proof: agreements kept, useful outputs shipped, beauty created, transformations delivered, resources circulated, and commitments completed.

Inner work is private. Public recognition belongs only to public proof. Nothing about you becomes part of the public record without your consent.

That boundary is the whole protection. Hold it and the system stays clean. Lose it and the temple becomes a panopticon.

## 3. THE PREMISE

The dominant economy on Earth runs on extraction. Whoever takes the most wins. The math doesn't work — extraction collapses. Every empire built on it has fallen. We're watching another round play out now.

The Full Potential Game makes a different economy visible: **circulation**. Resources that flow create more than resources that pile. The math is sustainable. It's also harder to play, because the metric isn't *how much do I have* — it's *how much moves through me*.

The proof already exists. OneBPO, a 200+ person operation in the Philippines, runs on circulation. Alice was elevated from a $7/hour worker to 50% co-owner. The business kept running. The number went up, not down. Circulation works in practice, not just theory.

We're not waiting for permission to switch games. The new game runs underneath the old one. The point of this guide is to make the board visible enough that other players can find their pieces.

## 4. YOUR FIRST MOVE — THE 7-DAY FIRST GAME

Before any of the architecture below means anything, you run **one proof loop**. Day by day:

> **Day 1.** Choose one transformation you can honestly help someone achieve.
> **Day 2.** Write the offer in one sentence.

That's the seed crystal. Every villager runs a version of it. The output of one loop becomes the input for the next. The first complete loop is what entitles a player to play higher tiers.

If you can't run the 7-Day First Game, the higher layers are decoration. If you can run it, the higher layers become available. There's no shortcut. There's also no obstacle besides you.

## 5. THE THREE CURRENCIES

The Game runs on three currencies, all real, all tracked, all convertible at the right altitude.

**Proof.** What was witnessed and recorded. An offer launched, an agreement kept, a transformation delivered, a beauty sealed.

> *Plain English: Proof is your record of completed work. Not promises — proofs.*

**Trust.** The confidence earned through repeated proof over time, weighted by how the people in your orbit actually respond when you create direction.

> *Plain English: Trust measures whether people actually move when you signal a priority. Not how many people watch you. How many people trust your signal enough to act.*

**Cash.** Real money still matters. The Game doesn't pretend money isn't money. It just refuses to make money the *only* score. Cash flows toward Trust over time, because that's where the real returns are. Short-term, it doesn't always look that way. Players who chase only cash end up with cash and not much else.

In the technical layer, Proof shows up as **Field Score** (per-event scoring) and Trust shows up as **CPI — Compounded Positive Impact** (the rolled-up sum of Proof over time, weighted by network responsiveness). Tokens may later become the portable technical form of Proof, but the Game does not require tokens to begin.

## 6. THE SCORING BOUNDARY

The first rule, the rule that protects the whole system from becoming social credit in temple clothes:

> **Score the proof, not the soul.**

You score what was witnessed and shipped. You don't score what someone *is*. You don't score their inner life. You don't score their potential. You score what they did, who saw it, and what it produced.

This is enforced through three layers.

**The public / private boundary.**

*Private* — visible only to the player and their direct stewards: reliability patterns, response time, agreement-keeping rate, growth edges, the texture of the work behind the work.

*Public* — visible to the field: completed quests, offers launched, transformations witnessed, contributions named, ads run, sales delivered.

**Consent governs witnessing.**

Nothing becomes public proof without consent from the people involved. A transformation can be witnessed privately, anonymized, or sealed as public proof depending on what the person agrees to. This is non-negotiable, especially in Zen Village containers, healing spaces, retreats, and any moment of real transformation.

**The Pause has a quality check.**

Most systems punish stopping. The Full Potential Game rewards a *clean pause* — the ability to set down work and redirect without resentment. But the pause earns score only when witnessed, articulated, and clean. Pauses that look like avoidance don't score. Pauses that look like wisdom do. The witness decides.

### The Five Agreement Types

Every commitment falls into one of five types. They score differently because they cost differently.

1. **Date-Specific Showup** — *"I'll be there Tuesday at 3PM."* You show, you score. Reliability metric.
2. **Time Block Commitment** — *"I'll work on this 4 hours."* Output + duration. Consistency metric.
3. **Deliverable by Date** — *"Done by Friday."* Scored on completion AND timing. Execution metric.
4. **Priority Interrupt** — *"Drop everything. This is now #1."* Rare. Most valuable when invoked correctly. Anti-attachment metric.
5. **Paradigm Shift** — *"Better way found. Stop the old way."* Rarest. Highest score. Anti-ego metric.

### The Proof Log

Every completed loop produces a Proof Log entry. The structure:

- **Agreement** — what was promised
- **Output** — what was completed
- **Witness** — who saw it
- **Result** — what changed
- **Consent** — private / anonymized / public
- **Next Quest** — the next clean move

Logs are stored in the player's record. Public-consent entries flow up to the field. Private entries stay in the player's own ledger. The fillable template lives on the Player Card.

### Minimum Viable Scoreboard

While the full CPI engine is being built, every player tracks five simple metrics:

1. Agreements kept
2. Useful outputs shipped
3. Transformations witnessed
4. Resources circulated
5. Clean pauses completed

That is enough to play. The full scoring engine arrives later. The five metrics are the whole game until it does.

### Misuse Protections

Scores cannot be used to shame, rank human worth, pressure disclosure, bypass consent, or override lived context. A score is a map of witnessed proof, not a verdict on a person.

Anyone — player, steward, or witness — can flag a misuse and pause the use of a score until the misuse is resolved. The constitution is short because it has to be remembered: *score the proof, never weaponize it.*

## 7. THE WORLD ARCHITECTURE

The Game runs on a **Sacred Trinity**:

- **Community** — who you play with.
- **Communication** — the nervous system: messages, voice, AI memory, agreements, public updates, proof logs, and the way priorities move through the field.
- **Currency** — how value moves: Proof, Trust, Cash.

All three have to be coherent or the Game breaks. Most modern systems get one or two right and lose on the third. The Full Potential Game is engineered to keep all three in alignment.

The Trinity is held by **four containers**.

**Zen Village** — *The Proof Incubator.* A 45-acre node in Chirripó, Costa Rica. Not a retreat center. The forge where villagers build the brand, run the offer, deliver the transformation, and graduate. Atlas is villager #1. Each villager that follows runs a similar arc, with the agreement they make becoming the template for villager n+1.

**Coherence** — *The curriculum.* Teaches the awareness layer underneath everything else: Wide → Deep → Compress → Conscious. The path from noise to stillness, from reaction to response. Without coherence, the rest is just productivity.

**Full Potential AI** — *The intelligence layer.* Diagnostic session → tailored AI system per client → human team layer → circulation ecosystem. Where the leverage compounds. The engine room.

**CORA Nation** — *The stewardship container.* A faith-rooted structure for holding resources, agreements, and circulation in service of the broader field. Mutual credit, not extraction. The container that keeps Trust and Cash legible to each other.

These four are not pillars. They're a stack. Coherence at the foundation. Zen Village as the embodied node. Full Potential AI as the leverage. CORA as the container that holds them all in coherence.

Every player has the same instrument and a different config.

> Noise → Distraction → Attention → Zense → Presence → Coherence → Flow → Stillness

> Coherence · Celebration · Care/Communication · Connections · Cash Flow

Above all of them sits a meta-ratio: **Creation / Consumption.** When that ratio inverts (you're consuming more than you're creating), every C below it gets noisy. When it's right-side up, the C's tune themselves.

**Your operating rule** — adopted by every serious player — is the **Sunheart Rule**:

> Do only what you do better than AI. Everything else, the system handles.

Your time is rare. Spend it on the ceremony, the diagnostic, the relationship initiation, the vision. Players who skip this rule burn out faster and contribute less.

## 9. QUEST TIERS

Quests escalate by stakes and time horizon.

- **Daily Quests** — Habits, check-ins, reps. Anyone can play. The basement of the Game. Most days you just keep showing up.
- **Weekly Quests** — Project milestones, team commitments, deliverables. Where the work actually moves.
- **Life Quests** — Brand launches, program completions, identity shifts. Heavy. You don't run more than a few of these per year.
- **Civilization Quests** — Run for office. Lead a movement. Build infrastructure that outlasts you. When one of these completes, the whole Game upgrades for every player.

You don't pick your quest tier. The tier picks you. The Game tracks readiness — you're invited up when the lower tier is consistently solid.

- The **tamper-proof Useful Output oracle** — the open problem underneath everything. Without it, advanced scoring stays approximate.

### The oracle problem

CPI is calculated *through* a player's network — measuring how the field actually responds when a priority shifts. That's the design. The implementation is genuinely hard. Self-reported activation is gameable. Surveillance is the wrong answer. Proof-of-action witnessing scales poorly without infrastructure.

Until the oracle is solved, CPI remains aspirational and the **Minimum Viable Scoreboard** (Section 6) is what actually runs. Stewards should know this. Players don't need to.

### The witness layer

The whole proof system depends on the integrity of witnesses. A score is only as honest as the people who sign it. CORA stewardship and Zen Village culture exist in part to keep that integrity high — to make sure witnesses are discerning, that they don't sign sloppily, and that misuse of the witness role is itself surfaced and addressed. The Game's worst failure mode is a corrupt witness layer; the most important steward investment is in keeping that layer clean.

A practical implication: stewards should refuse to sign proofs they cannot stand behind, even when refusal is socially expensive. The cost of a sloppy signature is paid by every future player who reads the score.

### Legal and compliance realities

The architecture requires specialized counsel — generalist faith-org lawyers will miss the specifics of cross-border community-provision structures. Cross-border financial holdings trigger disclosure obligations for citizens of jurisdictions that require them; US stewards in particular have aggressive reporting requirements on foreign accounts. The Circulation Equity Formula will be open-sourced for auditability before any meaningful scaling. A written conflict-resolution protocol governs disputes between stewards. OneBPO operates with independent directors and documented dissent. Externals are paid through community-provision rather than direct stipend in most cases, for both legal and circulation reasons.

CORA's compliance posture is **v0.9** — solid principles, real gaps. The gaps are tracked in the legal working doc, not here. A steward's responsibility is to know they exist and to operate accordingly until they close.

### The frame-inflation trap

The largest internal risk is the founder pattern of joining the next big thing before completing the current one. The Game itself can become this trap.

Civilization Quests are the long horizon, not the current quarter. World Peace Game language is the vision frame, not the next product launch. Stewards hold the line on this. When the founder reaches for new architecture before the current architecture has shipped its proof, stewards remind. That's part of stewardship — and part of the reason the Stewards' Cut exists at all.

The Tide Turner archetype is generative *and* expansionary. Stewards are the counterweight: the ones holding the work that exists until it ships, even when the field around it is calling for the next leap.
