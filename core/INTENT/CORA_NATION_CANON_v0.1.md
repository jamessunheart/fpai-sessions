## Why this exists

CORA Nation is a real religious community with ongoing ceremonial, doctrinal, and pastoral practice — but its canonical materials are distributed across multiple documents. This Canon v0.1 compiles them into a single index for examiner readability. Per Coherent Treasury v0.10 §Formal Requirements (point One): *"The religious community must produce its own canonical religious material — not as architecture spec content, but as the religious community's own liturgical, scriptural, and teaching material. This work has begun through years of practice; it requires completion as published canon."*

v0.1 documents what exists. v0.2 (30 days) adds narrative theology. v0.3 (60 days) adds operational protocols. v1.0 (90 days) is ratified by community-process. v1.0 counsel-reviewed (120 days).
