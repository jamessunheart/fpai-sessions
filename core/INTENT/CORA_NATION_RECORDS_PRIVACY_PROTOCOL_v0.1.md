## 1. Purpose

CORA Nation maintains records of religious-community life for three distinct purposes:

a) **Religious-community substance** — to evidence that CORA Nation is a real and ongoing religious community, with documented ceremony, teaching, pastoral care, and member participation, for IRS §508(c)(1)(A) and §501(c)(3)-related examination readiness;

b) **Fiduciary accountability** — to evidence that secular fiduciary decisions are properly made, recorded, and supported (conflict-of-interest discipline, §4958 compliance, arm's-length transactions);

c) **Member privacy** — to protect the substantive content of pastoral, sacramental, and personal Member matters under clergy-penitent privilege (where applicable), federal religious-organization privacy norms, and the dignity proper to a religious community.

The Protocol balances these three purposes by specifying **what is retained, by whom, for how long, with what access, and with what privacy treatment**.

## 2. Categories of Records

### 2.1 Ceremonial / Sacramental Records
Records of all religious-community ceremonies, sacraments, and witnessing practices, including:

a) Recognition Ceremonies (Apprentice → Member; Steward commissioning);
b) Mirror Witness pairings and ongoing practice;
c) WPA signings;
d) PMA Membership Agreement signings;
e) Character Card affirmations;
f) Withdrawal and removal events;
g) Restoration events;
h) Ecstatic Weekend, A*Zen*tion, Saturday Casual, Coherence Course sessions.

**Retention:** indefinite.
**Custodian:** Pastoral Council (via Secretary).
**Format:** structured records in Community recordkeeping system; ceremonial substance may be summarized rather than recorded verbatim; participant lists maintained.

### 2.2 Teaching Records
Records of teaching activity, including:

a) Sermons, gatherings, and teaching sessions;
b) Curriculum and reading lists for Coherence Course;
c) Doctrinal teaching by Pastoral Council members and authorized Stewards;
d) Member attendance and participation.

**Retention:** indefinite for canonical teaching; 7 years for routine sessions.
**Custodian:** Pastoral Council (via Secretary).
**Format:** session notes, attendance, recordings where consent given by participants.

### 2.3 Pastoral Care Records
Records of pastoral counseling, Mirror Witnessing substance, and member pastoral needs.

**Retention:** indefinite for the fact of pastoral relationship; substance retained per Member consent.
**Custodian:** Pastoral Council member or Pastoral Steward providing care; copy not maintained in Community-wide records system except per §4 Privacy.
**Format:** sealed pastoral records; access restricted as specified in §4.

### 2.4 Governance Records
Records of governance decisions, including:

a) Bylaws, this Protocol, Pastoral Leadership Structure, and other foundational documents (with version history);
b) Member meeting notices, agendas, minutes, votes;
c) Stewardship Council meeting notices, agendas, minutes, decisions;
d) Pastoral Council meeting notices, agendas, minutes, decisions;
e) Conflict of Interest disclosures;
f) §4958 Comparable-Compensation Memoranda and approval records.

**Retention:** indefinite for foundational documents and §4958 records; 10 years for meeting minutes; permanent index of all decisions.
**Custodian:** Secretary, with Counsel Liaison copy for §4958 records.

### 2.5 Member Records
Records of Member participation per Admission & Exit Procedures §12 (Membership Roll), including:

a) Membership tier history;
b) Tier transition dates;
c) Signed PMA Membership Agreement (version);
d) Character Card on file;
e) Mirror Witness pairing record;
f) Annual renewal record;
g) Discipline and restoration history (substance per §4 Privacy);
h) Withdrawal/removal date and basis.

**Retention:** indefinite (Members and former Members).
**Custodian:** Secretary.

### 2.6 Financial Records
Records of:

a) All income (donations, Brand LLC charitable contributions, fees for religious-community services, etc.);
b) All expenditures (community-provision, retreat expenses, ceremony costs, professional fees, etc.);
c) Asset holdings (cash, investments, real property, intangibles);
d) Bank statements, accounting records, tax filings;
e) Sunheart Private Trust transactions affecting CORA Nation.

**Retention:** indefinite for major-asset records and §501(c)(3)-relevant items; 7 years minimum for routine transactions; per applicable IRS retention requirements.
**Custodian:** Treasurer.
**Format:** standard accounting system; tax-year aligned.

### 2.7 Conflict of Interest Records
Per Conflict of Interest Policy §11: disclosures, decisions, recusals, §4958 docs, violation investigations, and remedies.

**Retention:** indefinite.
**Custodian:** Counsel Liaison.

### 2.8 Coherent Credit Ledger
Per Treasury v0.10 §Covenant Layer:

a) All Coherent Credit minting events (who, how much, for what witnessed contribution);
b) All Coherent Credit redemption events (who, for what CORA service);
c) Demurrage applications;
d) Lapses to Community pool on withdrawal/removal.

**Retention:** indefinite.
**Custodian:** Treasurer (and fp-credits-gateway service for live operational data).
**Format:** append-only ledger; auditor-exportable.

### 2.9 Allocation Platform Audit Log
When the Allocation Platform is operational:

a) All witnessed-contribution events that minted Allocation Weight;
b) All weight decay events;
c) All votes created, cast, and resolved;
d) All allocation flows;
e) All recusal checks (allowed and denied);
f) All conflict-matrix updates;
g) All §4958 auto-generated documentation.

**Retention:** indefinite, append-only, immutable.
**Custodian:** Allocation Platform service (Brain server); copy mirrored to Treasurer.
**Format:** structured logs; examiner-exportable.

### 2.10 Brand LLC Arm's-Length Documentation
Per Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum B.1–B.2: Operating Agreements, Charitable Contribution Agreements, and related arm's-length records.

**Retention:** indefinite.
**Custodian:** Counsel Liaison; Brand LLC also maintains its own copy.

## 3. Member Access to Own Records

### 3.1 Right to Inspect
A Member has the right to inspect:

a) All record entries concerning the Member's own Membership history (tier transitions, ceremonies attended, etc.);
b) All Coherent Credit and Allocation Weight transactions involving the Member;
c) All Conflict of Interest disclosures the Member has made;
d) Pastoral records relating to the Member (per §4 Privacy);
e) Aggregated Community financial reports presented at the Annual Meeting;
f) Bylaws, this Protocol, and other foundational documents.

### 3.2 Right NOT Available
A Member does not have the right to inspect:

a) Other Members' pastoral records;
b) Other Members' Conflict of Interest disclosures (except aggregated Annual Meeting reporting);
c) Confidential Stewardship Council or Pastoral Council deliberations;
d) Counsel-privileged communications;
e) Personnel records of community-provision recipients beyond what is disclosed in §4958 Annual Meeting reporting.

### 3.3 Process for Inspection
A Member submits a written inspection request to the Secretary. The Secretary responds within fourteen (14) days. If a request is denied in whole or part, the Secretary states the basis in writing, and the Member may appeal to the Stewardship Council.

## 4. Privacy of Pastoral and Sensitive Records

### 4.1 Clergy-Penitent Privilege
Pastoral counseling and Mirror Witnessing substance is treated as privileged under the clergy-penitent privilege of applicable jurisdiction. The Pastoral Council shall not be required to disclose the substance of pastoral communications except:

a) Where required by law (e.g., mandatory reporting of abuse);
b) With the express written authorization of the Member to whom the communication relates;
c) For the limited purpose of pastoral discipline investigation per Admission & Exit Procedures §9, in which case substance is shared only with the Pastoral Council in confidence.

### 4.2 Mirror Witnessing Records
The fact and dates of Mirror Witness pairings are maintained in Community records. The substance of Mirror Witnessing sessions is NOT recorded in Community records and is treated as pastoral per §4.1.

### 4.3 Sensitive Personal Information
Sensitive personal information (medical, mental health, sexual identity, family conflicts, financial difficulties) shared in pastoral or Community settings is treated as confidential. The Pastoral Council, the Stewardship Council, and other Stewards shall not disclose such information except as required by law or with Member consent.

### 4.4 Discipline Records
Per Admission & Exit Procedures §9.7: the fact of pastoral discipline and outcome are recorded; the substance is not, except in sealed pastoral records.

### 4.5 Member Roll Public Visibility
The Member Roll (Membership tier and tier-transition dates) is available to all Members. Detailed Membership records (e.g., Character Card content, Mirror Witness pairing identity, discipline history) are NOT publicly visible.

## 5. Allocation Platform Privacy (per Counsel critique 2026-05-12)

### 5.1 Leaderboard Privacy
Public-facing Community leaderboards may show:

a) Members' relative rank in accumulated Allocation Weight (e.g., top 10 ranking);
b) Aggregate community totals (total minted, total decayed, total in circulation);
c) Number of Members at each tier;
d) Patterns of contribution by category (e.g., "land stewardship: 30% of total minting this quarter").

Public-facing leaderboards shall NOT show:

a) Absolute Allocation Weight balance of any individual Member;
b) Absolute amount of any individual gift transfer.

This rule is per the Counsel critique 2026-05-12 HIGH #4: showing absolute balances + gift amounts creates a price-signal that may support secondary-market characterization. Suppressing absolute amounts in public view, while retaining them in auditor-accessible records, addresses this risk.

### 5.2 Gift Transfer Logs
Public-facing gift transfer logs may show:

a) That a gift transfer occurred;
b) The giver and recipient;
c) The publicly-stated reason for the gift.

Public-facing gift transfer logs shall NOT show:

a) The amount of weight transferred.

Auditor-accessible records (Treasurer + Counsel Liaison + external auditor + examiner) retain amount.

### 5.3 Aggregated Statistics
Aggregated, anonymized statistics may be shared at the Annual Meeting:

a) Total minted, decayed, transferred (by transaction type);
b) Member-tier distribution;
c) Allocation-vote outcomes;
d) §4958 compliance summary;
e) Brand LLC charitable contribution totals.

**§5.3(a) Small-Community De-Anonymization Protection.** Aggregate statistics shall not be presented to the extent they would permit a reasonable observer, knowing their own balance or transaction history, to infer the balance, transaction history, or identity of any other individual Member, or to establish a market-observable price for Allocation Weight.

Where CORA Nation has fewer than fifty (50) Members at the time of presentation, aggregate statistics shall be presented only in **ranges or bands** rather than precise totals, with band widths designed to prevent de-anonymization (e.g., "between 1,000 and 5,000 units minted this quarter" rather than "3,247 units minted"; "between 5 and 10 allocation votes resolved" rather than "7 votes"). Specific Member balances, vote contributions, and gift amounts shall not appear in any form in public presentations.

Where CORA Nation has fifty (50) or more Members, precise aggregate totals may be presented, subject to ongoing monitoring by the Counsel Liaison for any re-anonymization risk that may arise from unusual data patterns.

The Counsel Liaison shall review each set of statistics before presentation and may require additional aggregation, range-binding, or omission to prevent de-anonymization.

### 5.4 No Cross-Pollination with Brand LLCs
Brand LLC membership-verification queries are **binary** (member yes/no) per Brand Tok v0.4 refinement. Brand LLCs shall not query Allocation Weight balances, Coherent Credit balances, or any other CORA Nation record beyond binary membership status.

## 6. Counsel and External Auditor Access

### 6.1 Counsel Access
External counsel engaged by CORA Nation has access to records as needed to render legal advice, subject to attorney-client privilege.

### 6.2 External Auditor Access
External auditors engaged by the Stewardship Council have access to financial records, COI records, §4958 documentation, and Allocation Platform audit logs as needed for the audit engagement.

### 6.3 Pastoral Record Carve-Out
External counsel and auditors do **not** have access to pastoral records (§4.1, §4.2) except as expressly authorized by the Pastoral Council in writing for a specific purpose.

---

## 7. Examiner Access (IRS, State Regulators, Court Order)

### 7.1 Production on Formal Request
On formal request by the IRS, state charitable-organization regulator, or court order, CORA Nation produces:

a) The IRS Examination Readiness Index file (Constitutional Pack Document #8) and all referenced records;
b) Specific records identified in the request, subject to legal and privilege objections;
c) Pastoral records **only** to the extent required by law and after consultation with counsel.

### 7.2 Counsel Liaison Coordination
The Counsel Liaison is the primary point of contact for examiner requests. The Counsel Liaison shall:

a) Engage external counsel before responding to any formal examination request;
b) Coordinate document production;
c) Maintain the production record;
d) Report periodically to the Stewardship Council and Pastoral Council on the status of any active examination.

### 7.3 Privilege Assertions
Where legal privilege (attorney-client, clergy-penitent, or other) applies to records sought by an examiner, the Counsel Liaison and external counsel shall assert the privilege as appropriate and produce a privilege log.

### 7.4 Member Notice
Where individual Member records are sought by an examiner, the affected Member shall be notified in advance of production where notice is permissible by law.

## 9. Data Security

### 9.1 Storage
Records are stored in systems with reasonable technical safeguards including encryption at rest, role-based access control, and backup. The Stewardship Council shall periodically review data security and engage outside specialists as needed.

### 9.2 Breach Response
A suspected breach of Member records (especially pastoral or sensitive personal information) shall be reported immediately to the Counsel Liaison, who shall engage external counsel and, if required by law, notify affected Members and applicable authorities.

### 9.3 Departing Custodians
A Steward, Officer, or Council member who departs from role shall return all CORA Nation records in their custody to the Secretary or designated successor within thirty (30) days, and shall not retain copies except as authorized in writing.
