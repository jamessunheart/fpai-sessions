## Basis

This Agreement is founded on the seven CHRIST principles of the Coherent Champions of CHRIST Manifesto:

- **Coherence** — alignment between thought, word, action, and consequence
- **Healing** — work that heals rather than harms
- **Regeneration** — building systems that restore more than they consume
- **Intelligence** — guided by wisdom, humility, discernment, and care
- **Service** — gifts in service to the flourishing of life
- **Truth** — sought courageously, held compassionately

## Commitments

### James Sunheart

The seven clauses of the canonical World Peace Agreement template:

- I agree to practice peace in thought, word, and action.
- I agree to reduce unnecessary suffering.
- I agree to seek understanding before hatred.
- I agree to repair where I have caused harm.
- I agree to protect life, truth, beauty, and future generations.
- I agree to become trustworthy with intelligence, influence, and resources.
- I agree that peace must become visible through action.

### World Peace Organization (Zen Village)

The WPO commits to receiving and honoring this signing:

- To list the signer (James Sunheart) on the public Champions Roll
- To not retract or modify the signer's commitment without their consent
- To repair its own conduct if it falls short of the principles the signer is being asked to uphold
- To treat the founder's personal signing not as ceremonial but as binding on the founder in the same way it binds any other signer

## Repair Process

When this Agreement is broken (by James or by the WPO):

1. **Notice** — anyone may name the break
2. **Acknowledge** — the breaching party acknowledges without rationalization
3. **Understand** — both parties name the conditions
4. **Restore** — repair what can be repaired
5. **Update** — amend if the Agreement was insufficient

The founder is held to the same Agreement as anyone else who signs. *Power is accountable* — including the framework's own.

---

## Renewal

This Agreement is renewed by being lived. It is not re-signed. A breach without repair effectively withdraws James from being in active practice — even if his name remains on the Roll.
