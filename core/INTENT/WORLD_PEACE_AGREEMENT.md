# THE WORLD PEACE AGREEMENT

**A signable covenant of the World Peace Organization (Zen Village)**
**Founder:** James Sunheart
**Source:** Coherent Champions of CHRIST — Manifesto v1.0
**Adopted:** 2026-05-07

---

## The Agreement

I agree to practice peace in thought, word, and action.

I agree to reduce unnecessary suffering.

I agree to seek understanding before hatred.

I agree to repair where I have caused harm.

I agree to protect life, truth, beauty, and future generations.

I agree to become trustworthy with intelligence, influence, and resources.

I agree that peace must become visible through action.

---

*Signed not in perfection, but in sincere participation.*

---

## What Signing Means

Signing the World Peace Agreement is not a declaration of perfection. It is an entry into practice.

A signer commits to:

- **Daily**: one act of service, one act of truth, one act of repair, one act of regeneration, one moment of silence.
- **Weekly**: shared meals, dialogue circles, music, movement, nature, reflection, community contribution.
- **When harm occurs**: accountability, understanding, and restoration wherever possible.

The agreement is renewed by living it, not by re-signing it.

---

## Membership Primitive

Within the World Peace Organization, the Agreement is the entry point. A signer becomes a participant in the Coherent Champions of CHRIST — not as a title, but as a practice.

Operationally, the Agreement maps to existing infrastructure:

| Agreement Clause | Operational Reflection |
|---|---|
| "Practice peace in thought, word, and action" | Personal discipline — no infrastructure required |
| "Reduce unnecessary suffering" | Service, contribution, repair |
| "Seek understanding before hatred" | Dialogue circles, gatherings |
| "Repair where I have caused harm" | The Practice of Repair |
| "Protect life, truth, beauty, future generations" | Regenerative projects, stewardship |
| "Become trustworthy with intelligence, influence, and resources" | TRUST token (earned, needs-based — `SERVICES/trust-index/`, `contribution-tracker/`) |
| "Peace must become visible through action" | Public proof — gatherings, contributions, signal pages |

The TRUST token is the membership ledger. It is earned through verified contribution, not purchased. It tracks who is in active practice, not who has signed a piece of paper.
