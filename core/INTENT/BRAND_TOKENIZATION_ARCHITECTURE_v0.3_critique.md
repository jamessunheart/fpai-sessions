## Issues (ranked by severity)

### 🟠 HIGH — Religious Doctrine Codification Is a Future Timeline, Not a Present Fact

**Risk:** The entire 508(c)(1)(A) defense — and by extension every other legal structure in this architecture (Credit legitimacy, Covenant Layer, community-provision compensation, arm's-length charitable contributions) — rests on CORA Nation being a *substantive* religious community. `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` (§Formal Requirements, point One) is explicit: "The religious community must produce its own canonical religious material — not as architecture spec content, but as the religious community's own liturgical, scriptural, and teaching material. This work has begun through years of practice; it requires completion as published canon." Point Six reiterates: "Counsel credentialed in 508(c)(1)(A) religious organizations... engagement is required before any Coherent Credit is issued as Covenant Layer distribution." v0.3's 30-day-to-120-day timeline is directionally correct but the canon does not yet exist. If the architecture goes to human counsel sign-off before the canon is compiled, counsel is being asked to sign off on a structure whose legal foundation is still under construction. An IRS examiner who receives this architecture without the underlying canonical materials has nothing to examine.

**Corpus citation:** `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` §Formal Requirements, points One, Two, Six; §Open Questions, item 2 ("CORA Nation's religious doctrine is present in the body of work developed over years... It has not yet been formally codified as canonical religious texts")

**Suggested fix:** Two options. (A) Complete at minimum the 30-day step (compile existing canonical sources into CORA Nation Canon v0.1) *before* human counsel sign-off — counsel can then review the canon draft simultaneously with the architecture. (B) If human counsel sign-off proceeds before canon completion, explicitly scope the counsel engagement to include: "Note: canonical materials are in compilation; this sign-off is conditional on canon meeting the substantive religious community standard." Don't let counsel sign off on completeness if the underlying canon doesn't exist yet. Option A is cleaner.

### 🟠 HIGH — Arm's-Length Documentation Templates Deferred to v0.4

**Risk:** v0.3 correctly identifies five template documents needed (Operating Agreement, Contribution Agreement, COI Policy, Recordkeeping Protocol, §4958 Template). It then defers drafting them to v0.4. The problem: the human counsel sign-off is intended to validate the *architecture*, but the architecture's arm's-length claim depends entirely on those documents. Without the templates, counsel is being asked to approve "we will document this arm's-length relationship in a document we haven't written yet." That is not a sign-off on arm's-length — it is a sign-off on the intention to achieve arm's-length. `legal_framework_synthesis_v2.md` (§Documentation Required) lists operating agreements and substantial relationship documentation as required items, not aspirational ones. `18pgChurch_Legal_Summary-Resource.md.pdf` (compliance monitoring, annually: "Legal counsel comprehensive review — update all documentation") presupposes the documentation exists.

**Corpus citation:** `legal_framework_synthesis_v2.md` §Documentation Required checklist; `18pgChurch_Legal_Summary-Resource.md.pdf` §Compliance Monitoring (ANNUALLY)

**Suggested fix:** Either (A) draft at least skeletal templates (term sheets, not final documents) as v0.3 addenda so counsel can review the substance of the arm's-length claim, not just its description; or (B) restructure the counsel engagement as a two-phase sign-off: Phase 1 = architecture review (v0.3), Phase 2 = template review (v0.4), with Phase 1 explicitly limited in scope. Don't represent a complete architecture review if the documents that would make it complete don't exist yet.

### 🟠 HIGH — IRC §501(d) Apostolic Association Question Unresolved, With Active Tax Consequences

**Risk:** v0.3 correctly flags IRC §501(d) as an open question for counsel. But the risk is not merely theoretical. `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` (§Formal Requirements, point Six) specifically flags "IRC 501(d) apostolic associations (noting the pro-rata taxation consideration flagged by Gemini's fifth-pass review)" as requiring counsel credentialed in that area *before* Covenant Layer activation. The risk: if CORA Nation's community-provision model (housing, meals, pastoral support as compensation) functionally resembles an apostolic association, the IRS may classify it as §501(d) rather than 508(c)(1)(A). The tax consequence is meaningful — §501(d) organizations pay tax pro rata on their members' behalf, which changes the economics of the entire Covenant Layer. The architecture is currently written entirely on a 508(c)(1)(A) assumption. If §501(d) classification applies, the community-provision model, the contribution flows, and the Steward compensation structure all need to be re-examined. This is not a question that can be safely deferred past human counsel sign-off.

**Corpus citation:** `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` §Formal Requirements, point Six (explicit §501(d) flagging); §Open Questions, item 4 (implicitly referenced through Covenant Protocol design)

**Suggested fix:** Make the §501(d) analysis a *required deliverable* of the human counsel sign-off engagement, not an open question after it. The 1-2 hour counsel scope should explicitly include: "Assess whether the Covenant Layer community-provision model triggers §501(d) classification and, if so, what the pro-rata tax consequences are and whether the architecture survives them." Without this, the sign-off is incomplete for any Covenant Layer activation.

### 🟡 MEDIUM — Coherent Credit Demurrage Rate Unspecified

**Risk:** v0.3 carries forward "demurrage: per Coherent Treasury v0.10 (TBD: rate)" as a non-blocking open item. This is acceptable for architecture purposes but creates a residual issue: the demurrage mechanism is one of the key factual elements supporting the "not a security, not a store of value, not a currency" characterization. `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` (§Cosmos SDK sovereignty; spectral radius 0.7 safety bound) treats demurrage as an architectural element, not an optional parameter. An unspecified demurrage rate means the Credit's economic behavior is undefined. If the rate is zero, the Credit behaves more like a stored-value instrument. If the rate is material, it reinforces the "designed to circulate, not accumulate" characterization supporting the non-security analysis.

**Corpus citation:** `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` (Changelog from v0.5: "Mutual credit clearing; Two-Economy Model"); general law (demurrage as anti-accumulation mechanism supporting non-security characterization — not specifically in corpus, flagging as reasoning beyond corpus)

**Suggested fix:** Specify at minimum a rate range (e.g., "X% per month on balances idle more than Y days") before the first Credit issuance. The exact rate doesn't need human counsel sign-off, but the existence of a non-zero demurrage rate should be confirmed before sign-off because it affects the securities analysis.

### 🟡 MEDIUM — "Membership Verification" by Brand LLCs Creates Residual Entanglement Risk

**Risk:** v0.3 specifies that Brand LLCs "verify CORA membership via Coherent Credit balance (membership-verification only, NOT payment)." This is the correct framing — but in practice, it creates a dependency between the Credit system and Brand LLC commercial access. A member who maintains a Coherent Credit balance gets a discount at a Brand LLC. The Credit balance is earned through CORA Nation religious participation. The commercial benefit (discount) flows from the religious organization's credentialing system. This residual linkage — Credit balance as access credential for commercial benefit — is not a securities issue (the Credit itself isn't the payment), but it does create a soft private-benefit vector: religious participation is incentivized by commercial advantage. `18pgChurch_Legal_Summary-Resource.md.pdf` (red flags: "Primarily commercial activity") and `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` (§Formal Requirements, point Three: "Clean separation between Interface Layer employment and Covenant Layer participation") both flag this kind of bleed. The fix in v0.2 removed the settlement mechanic; v0.3 retains the credentialing linkage.

**Corpus citation:** `18pgChurch_Legal_Summary-Resource.md.pdf` §Red Flags; `remarkably-coherent-treasury-v0.10.md` §Formal Requirements, point Three

**Suggested fix:** Add explicit language to the architecture (and to the eventual Brand LLC Operating Agreement template) that: (a) the Brand LLC discount is a purely commercial decision by the LLC funded by the LLC, (b) CORA Nation receives no benefit from the discount, (c) CORA membership verification is a binary credential check (member/not-member) and the Credit *balance* is not checked — only membership status. This severs any remaining economic linkage between Credit accumulation and commercial benefit.

### 🟡 MEDIUM — State Charitable Solicitation Registration Explicitly Unaddressed

**Risk:** v0.3 correctly flags this as unaddressed. CORA Nation's online membership intake is national. Most states with charitable solicitation registration laws (approximately 40 states) require registration before soliciting residents, even for religious organizations. The exemption for religious organizations varies by state — some states exempt 508(c)(1)(A) organizations, others do not. `legal_framework_synthesis_v2.md` and `180pgChurch_Legal_Resource.pdf` do not address state charitable registration specifically (corpus does not cover this). This is a real compliance gap, not a theoretical one.

**Corpus citation:** Corpus does not address state charitable solicitation registration. Flagging as general law not from corpus. (This makes it a MORE significant gap — the corpus's silence means the architecture has never addressed it.)

**Suggested fix:** Add to the human counsel sign-off scope: "Assess state charitable solicitation registration requirements triggered by national online membership intake; identify which states require registration and whether religious-organization exemptions apply." This doesn't need to be resolved before sign-off, but it needs to be on counsel's explicit list.

### 🟡 MEDIUM — UBIT Analysis for CORA Nation Services Not Documented

**Risk:** CORA Nation provides services for which Coherent Credits are redeemable: Mirror witnessing sessions, pastoral coaching, retreat access, ceremony participation. These are characterized as religious-community services. But the question of whether any of these services constitute unrelated business income if provided to non-members or fee-paying participants has not been analyzed. `18pgChurch_Legal_Summary-Resource.md.pdf` (Risk Assessment Table: "Credit System Income — MODERATE — HIGH [defense strength]") indicates this is a known risk with available defenses, but those defenses require documentation. `legal_framework_synthesis_v2.md` (§Documentation Required: "UBIT defense memoranda") lists UBIT defense memos as required documentation.

**Corpus citation:** `18pgChurch_Legal_Summary-Resource.md.pdf` §Risk Assessment Table; `legal_framework_synthesis_v2.md` §Documentation Required

**Suggested fix:** Add "UBIT defense memorandum for CORA Nation service offerings" to the v0.4 documentation deliverables list. Specifically: are retreat access and pastoral coaching substantially related to CORA Nation's religious mission? If yes, documented how. If any service could be characterized as commercial, segregate it.
