## 1. Purpose

CORA Nation maintains a Know-Your-Member ("KYM") and Anti-Money-Laundering ("AML") framework to:

a) Verify the identity of Members at admission and periodically thereafter, consistent with religious-community discernment practice;
b) Document sources of Member contributions above thresholds defined herein;
c) Screen Members and contributions against U.S. sanctions lists (OFAC) and high-risk-jurisdiction restrictions;
d) Flag suspicious patterns in Coherent Credit issuance / redemption and (when ratified) Allocation Platform activity;
e) Establish the framework for FinCEN analysis to determine whether any CORA Nation operations constitute regulated money services business activity.

This Policy is intentionally a v0.1 stub. Final implementation gates on external counsel determination of which specific AML / KYC regimes apply to CORA Nation's religious-community economic operations.

## 2. Legal Framework

The following regulatory bodies and frameworks may apply to CORA Nation operations; counsel determines applicability:

| Framework | Potential applicability | Counsel deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| **FinCEN (Bank Secrecy Act)** | Coherent Credit issuance + redemption may constitute money services business if treated as accepting/transmitting value | Money services business analysis per 31 CFR §1010.100(ff) |
| **OFAC** | All U.S. persons (and their organizations) must screen against Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list | Continuous screening obligation |
| **State money transmitter laws** | Each state where Members reside may have separate money-transmitter regime; CORA's closed-loop Credit design per Treasury v0.10 is designed to avoid this, but counsel confirms | 50-state survey per Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum C |
| **EU MiCA** | If any Member is EU-resident, MiCA may apply to crypto-asset-like instruments | Per-jurisdiction analysis |
| **UK FCA cryptoasset regime** | Same for UK Members | Per-jurisdiction analysis |
| **FATF Travel Rule** | For cross-border value transfers above thresholds | Per-jurisdiction analysis |

The Admission & Exit Procedures §11.3(a) bright-line restriction on Allocation Weight / Coherent Credit activation for Members in EU MiCA, UK FCA, PRC, U.S.-sanctioned, and FATF-listed jurisdictions is the foundational structural defense; this Policy operationalizes additional KYM and AML disciplines on top of that bright-line.

## 3. Know-Your-Member (KYM) Verification

### 3.1 Identity Verification at PMA Agreement Signing

At the time of PMA Membership Agreement signing (Apprentice tier admission per Admission & Exit Procedures §4.1), the prospective Member shall provide:

a) **Verifiable government-issued identity document** — passport, state driver's license, or equivalent. The Counsel Liaison or designated Officer reviews the document and records the type, issuing authority, and document number (not photo copies stored in plaintext);

b) **Current residence address** — confirmed by a recent (within 90 days) utility bill, bank statement, or comparable document;

c) **Member-affirmed statement of religious-community purpose** — affirmation that the Member joins CORA Nation for religious-community membership and not for any commercial, investment, money-transmission, or evasive purpose;

d) **OFAC SDN screening confirmation** — the Counsel Liaison runs the prospective Member's name against the OFAC SDN list at the time of admission. Hits or partial matches halt admission pending review.

### 3.2 Identity Verification — Records and Privacy

Identity verification records are stored per the Records & Privacy Protocol with the following additional protections:

a) Encryption at rest, role-based access (Counsel Liaison + designated officer only);
b) No public-facing or Member-facing display of identity document numbers, dates of birth, or residence addresses beyond the Member's own record;
c) Retention indefinite, consistent with §501(c)(3) church examination-readiness;
d) Disposal upon Member withdrawal or removal limited to redaction; the fact of admission and verification status is retained for examiner-readiness.

### 3.3 Periodic Re-Verification

Members re-confirm identity and residence at each Annual Meeting through a designated procedure (e.g., in-person renewal affirmation, secure electronic re-attestation). Members whose residence has changed shall update records within 30 days of the change.

## 4. Contribution Source Documentation

### 4.1 Threshold Triggers

The following events trigger contribution-source documentation:

a) **Any single contribution above USD $10,000** to CORA Nation (cash, check, electronic transfer, securities, or in-kind value);
b) **Aggregated annual contributions above USD $25,000** from any single Member;
c) **Any contribution in cryptocurrency, regardless of size**, requires source-of-funds analysis given the FATF Travel Rule and chain-of-title considerations;
d) **Any contribution from a Member resident in a jurisdiction flagged under Admission & Exit Procedures §11.3(a)**, regardless of size.

### 4.2 Documentation Required

For each trigger event:

a) Member declaration of source of funds (employment, business income, investment proceeds, gift, inheritance, etc.);
b) Supporting documentation (recent paystubs, business income statements, brokerage statements, gift/inheritance documentation as applicable);
c) Counsel Liaison review and acceptance;
d) Records retained per Records & Privacy Protocol with auditor + examiner accessibility.

### 4.3 Refusal to Document

A Member who declines to provide source-of-funds documentation when triggered may have the contribution returned, and the Pastoral Council may determine whether the failure to document is consistent with religious-community covenant participation.

## 5. Suspicious Activity Flagging

### 5.1 Patterns to Flag

The Counsel Liaison reviews ledger activity periodically and flags the following patterns for further review:

a) **Structuring** — multiple contributions just under threshold amounts, particularly within short time windows;
b) **Rapid through-flow** — large contribution followed shortly by large redemption or transfer;
c) **Unusual minting events** — Coherent Credit or Allocation Weight minted to a Member in volumes substantially disproportionate to that Member's documented religious-community contribution history;
d) **Cross-pollination patterns** — patterns suggesting a Member is acting as a conduit for non-Member value into the Community;
e) **External jurisdiction patterns** — unusual activity associated with Members in flagged jurisdictions per §11.3(a).

### 5.2 Review Process

Flagged patterns are reviewed by:

a) Counsel Liaison initial review;
b) Stewardship Council review for patterns suggesting fiduciary risk;
c) External counsel engagement for patterns suggesting AML, securities, or sanctions exposure;
d) FinCEN suspicious activity report (SAR) filing where required by law.

### 5.3 Confidentiality of SAR Filing

If a SAR is filed under FinCEN regulations, the existence of the filing is confidential per 31 CFR §1010.320. The Counsel Liaison does not disclose the filing to the affected Member or to other Members.

## 6. Sanctions Screening

### 6.1 OFAC SDN Screening

The Counsel Liaison screens all prospective Members at admission and all existing Members annually against the OFAC SDN list and applicable Sectoral Sanctions Identifications List (SSI). Hits or partial matches halt the admission process or trigger review of the existing Membership.

### 6.2 Sanctioned Jurisdictions

Persons resident in jurisdictions subject to comprehensive U.S. sanctions (currently Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, Crimea/DNR/LNR) shall NOT be admitted to CORA Nation as Members, regardless of religious-community discernment. This is a categorical rule that may not be waived.

Persons resident in jurisdictions subject to selective sanctions may be admitted subject to external-counsel review of the specific sanctions regime applicable to that person and to CORA Nation's interaction with that person.

### 6.3 Member Becomes Sanctioned

If an existing Member becomes subject to OFAC sanctions during their Membership:

a) The Counsel Liaison immediately freezes any pending Coherent Credit issuance / redemption / Allocation Weight activity for that Member;
b) The matter is escalated to external counsel for guidance on continuing relationship under sanctions law;
c) The Pastoral Council is consulted on pastoral aspects of the relationship;
d) Action consistent with both law and religious-community covenant is taken.

## 7. FinCEN Analysis (Counsel Deliverable)

The fundamental question — whether CORA Nation's Coherent Credit operations or any Allocation Platform feature constitutes money services business activity under 31 CFR §1010.100(ff) — is a counsel determination, not an AI determination. This Policy reserves the question for counsel.

Treasury v0.10 §Covenant Layer designs Coherent Credit as:
a) Closed-loop (CORA-only redemption);
b) Non-transferable between Members for value;
c) Non-purchasable (earned only by witnessed contribution);
d) Subject to demurrage (non-savings-instrument-like);
e) Religious-community-internal.

These design choices are intended to keep Coherent Credit outside money services business definitions. Counsel reviews and confirms; until then, this Policy assumes the worst case (FinCEN registration may be required) and operates accordingly.

---

## 8. Brand LLC Interaction

CORA Nation does NOT undertake AML obligations on behalf of any Brand LLC. Each Brand LLC is independently responsible for its own AML / KYC obligations under applicable law.

Where a Member is both a Brand LLC investor and a CORA Nation Member per Admission & Exit Procedures §11.4, the two relationships maintain separate AML / KYC files. CORA Nation may not share its KYM file with any Brand LLC, and a Brand LLC's KYC file is not shared with CORA Nation, except as required by law or with the affected Member's written consent.

---

## 9. Records

All records under this Policy are maintained per the Records & Privacy Protocol with:

a) Encryption at rest;
b) Role-based access (Counsel Liaison + designated officers);
c) Indefinite retention;
d) Examiner-exportable on formal request;
e) Annual aggregate reporting (e.g., total Members screened, flags raised, SARs filed) to the Stewardship Council; aggregate-only reporting to the Membership at the Annual Meeting.

## 10. Counsel Engagement Scope (Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum C expansion)

The human counsel engagement scope established in Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum C is expanded to include the following AML / KYC deliverables:

1. **FinCEN money services business analysis** — does Coherent Credit issuance / redemption constitute MSB activity?
2. **State money-transmitter 50-state analysis** — does any state where CORA has Members require MTL?
3. **OFAC compliance program review** — is the screening framework in §6 sufficient?
4. **High-risk jurisdiction analysis** — confirmation of the §11.3(a) bright-line list; identification of any additional jurisdictions to add
5. **Brand LLC / CORA AML interaction** — counsel review of §8 separation
6. **Foreign-Member-specific analysis** — for any Member outside the U.S., what foreign-AML regime applies and how does CORA's framework satisfy it
7. **SAR-filing decision framework** — when must CORA file FinCEN suspicious activity reports
