## The shape

Every Villager wears **two hats simultaneously**:

1. **Mode** — *how you feed the communal treasury* (Hunter or Gatherer)
2. **Craft** — *what you do day-to-day in the Village* (Builder · Farmer · Chef · Cleaner)

You pick one of each. You can switch with a Proof and Council nod. Anyone can pick up another hat for a day — but your declared Mode + Craft is what earns base share.

The mockumentary documents both layers. Every Proof = a scene.

---

## Modes — how the treasury fills

### 🏹 Hunter — *outbound*
Goes outside the Village walls. Brings dollars/guests/sponsors back.
- DM, pitch, close
- Book retreat guests
- Sell content / land sponsors
- Negotiate barter with neighbors

**Hunter Proof:** any dollar (or in-kind value) confirmed into the communal fund. Treasury bonus = % of inflow.

### 🌿 Gatherer — *inbound*
Stays present. Attracts attention back to the Village. Builds the field.
- Mockumentary scenes (the daily show)
- Post / shoot / edit
- On-camera presence + storytelling
- Audience growth, conversion to visitor

**Gatherer Proof:** content shipped that demonstrably grew followers, leads, or conversions. Treasury bonus = % of attributable inflow (lagged).

> *Hunter = spear. Gatherer = lens. Both feed the same pot.*

## Crafts — what you actually do

| Craft | Daily work | Hat Proof | Satire archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔨 **Builder** | Construction, AI tools, brand assets, infra | Build shipped (room, tool, site, system) | "Brutus" — earnest hammer-wielder who Vibe-Codes shrines |
| 🌱 **Farmer** | Food production, compost, land tending | Harvest delivered (lbs to kitchen, seedling counts) | "Gaia" — overly mystical about soil pH |
| 🍳 **Chef** | Meals for Village + guests | Meals served (count, NPS) | "Mama" — every plate is "made with love and bone broth" |
| 🧹 **Cleaner** | Village upkeep, waste, water, order | Spaces maintained, systems running | "Sweep" — finds enlightenment in tidying |

A Villager can hold one Craft as their primary; secondary Crafts earn smaller share when picked up.

## Treasury split — v0.1 rule

Communal fund (cash + in-kind) distributes monthly. Three pools:

| Pool | % | Distributed by |
|---|---|---|
| **Base share** | 50% | Equal across active residents (presence + WPA signed) |
| **Craft share** | 30% | Hat Proofs filed that month (weighted by output) |
| **Mode share** | 20% | Mode Proofs (Hunter dollars in / Gatherer attributable conversions) |

**Why this split:** base keeps it dignified (everyone eats), Craft rewards daily contribution, Mode rewards the high-leverage acts that grow the pot. Tunable as the experiment runs.

Ledger lives on canonical `fp-credits-gateway`. Each Proof type maps to a credit grant. James + Atlás co-sign disputed splits.

## The satire layer

The Village is **earnest characters + knowing audience** (Borat / Office / Parks & Rec). The mockumentary works *because* the work is real. Treasury growing is the joke's punchline — it actually works.

Each Villager declares an archetype. Camera leans into it. The character is exaggerated; the labor is not.

## The deeper experiment

If treasury scales and the model catches on, this is a working answer to:

- **vs Capitalism:** profit isn't private; circulation is the point
- **vs Communism:** contribution is visible and rewarded; no equal-misery floor
- **The third path:** Coherent Treasury — Mode + Craft + Base = dignified, productive, scalable

Other Villages (Bali, Mexico, anywhere) can fork the role grid. Treasury can settle across them via Coherent Credit.
