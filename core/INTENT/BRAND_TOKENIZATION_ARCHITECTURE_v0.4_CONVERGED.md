## Historical precedent (added v0.4 polish)

This architecture is not novel. It is the operational form of century-plus precedent across intentional religious communities + B2B mutual credit networks:

- **Bruderhof Communities** — communal religious life, community-provision compensation, charitable-contribution-from-affiliated-business model; tax-exempt church recognition
- **Hutterite Colonies** — apostolic-association structure (IRC §501(d) recognized), pro-rata tax treatment, community-provision economics
- **Catholic Worker Movement** — voluntary poverty + community-provision + parallel commercial activities
- **Benedictine Monasteries** — Ora et Labora; commercial labor + religious community + property holding under stable legal forms across centuries
- **WIR Bank (Switzerland, 1934–present)** — mutual credit clearing among small businesses; closed-loop demurrage-style currency that has weathered SNB, FATF, and Basel scrutiny
- **Sardex (Sardinia, Italy)** — modern B2B mutual credit network; legal as closed-loop accounting unit, not securities

CORA Nation + Brand LLC tier + closed-loop Coherent Credit is a synthesis of these traditions. Counsel reviewing this architecture has multiple analogous precedent paths to anchor opinion on.

## Architecture (v0.4 — unchanged structurally from v0.3; refinements only)

See v0.3 for the full architecture diagram. v0.4 retains:
- CORA Nation 508(c)(1)(A) church + Sunheart Private Trust = religious/nonprofit tier
- Each Brand = independent for-profit LLC = commercial tier
- Coherent Credit = closed-loop, CORA-only redemption, no member-to-member transfer
- Steward compensation = community-provision (with §4958 hard precondition) + Brand-LLC commercial compensation
- Costa Rica entity = blocking precondition for Camp Zen physical operations
- The Village = CORA Nation Media Ministry (no economic promotion)

### Refinements added in v0.4

**Sunheart Private Trust governance:** Implements the control-vs-ownership separation required by Coherent Treasury v0.10. Founding Steward (James) does not hold majority voting authority over disposition, sale, or dividend distribution of donated assets. Independent trustees or governance body holds majority fiduciary control over those specific asset classes. *(Confirm or amend Trust instrument as precondition.)*

**Coherent Credit demurrage:** Non-zero rate, calibrated to reinforce the "designed to circulate, not accumulate" framing supporting non-security characterization. Initial range proposal: **0.5–1.5% per month on balances idle >30 days**. Exact rate set by CORA Nation governance prior to first issuance.

**Brand LLC membership verification:** Binary check (member/not-member) ONLY. Credit balance is NOT read by Brand LLCs. Discount eligibility is membership-status based, not balance based. (Severs the soft-private-benefit vector flagged in v0.3.)

**Worker classification (CR-specific):** Worker classification analysis under CR labor code is a precondition for any worker beginning work at the CR site, parallel to (not subsequent to) CR entity formation.

**FBAR/FATCA:** US persons with signatory authority or >50% ownership over any CR account or entity have FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) and FATCA (IRC §6038D) reporting obligations. CR entity formation triggers a US tax-counsel review of all US persons in the structure.

**Insurance:** Religious-community insurance (general liability, property, professional liability for Mirror witnessing / pastoral coaching) procured before Coherent Credit redemption goes live. Camp Zen specifically requires retreat-operations insurance under CR + US frameworks.

**State charitable solicitation registration:** CORA Nation's national online membership intake potentially triggers registration requirements in ~40 states. Religious-organization exemptions vary state-by-state. Counsel engagement scope includes 50-state survey of which states require registration and which exemptions apply.

**UBIT defense memos:** For each CORA Nation service offering (Mirror witnessing, pastoral coaching, retreat access, ceremony participation), CORA Nation drafts a one-page "substantially related to religious mission" memo. These are internal documentation, not filed with IRS, but produced if examiner requests.

## v0.4 Addendum B — Skeletal arm's-length templates (term-sheet level)

These are structural outlines for counsel to review and complete. They are NOT final legal documents.

### B.1 — Brand LLC Operating Agreement (term sheet)
- **Entity type:** State LLC (Delaware or home-state)
- **Ownership:** Steward + outside investors as agreed
- **Independence from CORA Nation:**
  - CORA Nation has NO board seat, NO veto power, NO operational direction authority
  - CORA Nation has NO equity, debt, profit-share, or revenue-share in Brand LLC
  - Brand LLC sets pricing, services, staffing, vendor relationships independently
- **Charitable contributions to CORA Nation:**
  - Voluntary, set by Brand LLC's own board/owners
  - No contractual obligation
  - No quid pro quo (no CORA Nation services or member access conditioned on contribution)
- **Membership verification cooperation:**
  - Brand LLC may verify CORA Nation membership status (binary check) for the purpose of offering member-rate discounts
  - This verification is technical (API call to CORA Nation's membership system) and does not create any agency, partnership, or joint venture
- **Brand IP:**
  - Brand LLC owns its own brand, content, and customer relationships
  - CORA Nation has no IP claim in Brand LLC's commercial outputs

### B.2 — Brand LLC ↔ CORA Nation Charitable Contribution Agreement (term sheet)
- **Voluntary:** Brand LLC contributes at its discretion; no obligation
- **No quid pro quo:** CORA Nation does not provide any specific benefit, access, member-base credentialing, or marketing in exchange for contribution
- **Charitable purpose:** Contributions support CORA Nation's religious-community mission (specified)
- **Tax treatment:** Brand LLC treats as charitable deduction; CORA Nation treats as donative receipt; documented annually
- **No clawback:** Contributions are donative and final once made
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate the contribution relationship at any time without prejudice

### B.3 — Conflict-of-Interest Policy for Stewards (term sheet)
- **Scope:** Stewards who are simultaneously CORA Nation members and Brand LLC owners/operators
- **Recusal:** Steward recuses from any CORA Nation decision affecting their own Brand LLC (pricing of Brand LLC services to CORA members, membership-verification policy as it affects their LLC, etc.)
- **Disclosure:** Steward annually discloses Brand LLC ownership and any material changes
- **Independent review:** Material decisions affecting any Steward's Brand LLC require approval by CORA Nation members/board who do not have ownership in that Brand LLC

### B.4 — CORA Nation Substantive Religious-Community Recordkeeping Protocol (term sheet)
- **Ceremony records:** Date, location, ceremony type, presiding role, participant list (members + visitors), brief notes on substance
- **Teaching records:** Sermons, gatherings, teaching texts, attendance
- **Pastoral care records:** Anonymized summaries of pastoral counseling, support provided, member needs addressed (with member consent for any identified detail)
- **Community decisions:** Governance minutes, member-process decisions, doctrinal interpretations
- **Member participation logs:** WPA signings, Character Card completions, Mirror Pairings, ongoing participation tracking
- **Retention:** Records kept indefinitely; auditable internally; produced to examiners if formally requested
- **Privacy:** Member-identifying detail handled with discretion; aggregate statistics shared publicly; individual records protected

### B.5 — CORA Nation §4958 Rebuttable-Presumption Documentation Template (term sheet)
- **For each Steward receiving community-provision:**
  1. **Comparable-compensation data:** Pull from BLS, religious-community benchmarks, peer-organization data; document the comparables used
  2. **Independent approval:** CORA Nation board members who are not Stewards-of-other-Brands and not personal friends of the Steward in question approve the compensation determination; meeting minutes documented
  3. **Contemporaneous written documentation:** The compensation determination, its basis, and the comparable data used are written down at the time of approval (not after)
- **Activation precondition:** Without all three elements, community-provision does not activate for that Steward.

These templates are skeletal — they outline the structure counsel needs to review. Full document drafting follows counsel sign-off.

## v0.4 Addendum C — Human counsel engagement scope (proposed)

**Engagement type:** 1-2 hour consultation with religious-nonprofit + securities-law counsel (Cooley, Anderson Kill, or boutique specialist)
**Pre-read materials:**
- Brand Tokenization Architecture v0.4 (this document)
- Coherent Treasury v0.10 (foundational architecture)
- CORA Nation Canon v0.1 (existing canonical sources)
- This document's Addendum B (skeletal templates)

**Required deliverables from counsel:**

1. **§501(d) apostolic-association analysis** — Does the Covenant Layer community-provision model trigger §501(d) classification? If so, what are the pro-rata tax consequences? Does the architecture survive them, or does it require restructuring?

2. **State charitable solicitation registration survey** — Which states require CORA Nation to register before soliciting from residents? Which states' religious-organization exemptions apply?

3. **State MTL analysis for Coherent Credit** — Does the closed-loop structure (CORA-only redemption, no member-to-member transfer) hold under MTL regimes of states with significant CORA Nation membership?

4. **Arm's-length structural review** — Does the Brand LLC ↔ CORA Nation relationship (Stewards as both LLC owners and CORA members; voluntary contributions; binary membership verification; documented operating agreements) avoid private benefit / inurement?

5. **§4958 rebuttable-presumption confirmation** — Does the documentation framework in Addendum B.5 meet the IRS rebuttable-presumption standard?

6. **Costa Rica + US tax structure for Camp Zen** — Coordination with CR counsel + US international tax counsel; FBAR/FATCA implications for US persons.

7. **Worker classification for Stewards** — Does community-provision constitute employment under US or CR law? How does dual compensation (CORA provision + Brand LLC salary) get classified?

**Out of scope for this engagement (separate engagements):**
- Specific Brand LLC equity raises (each requires its own securities counsel review)
- Specific Reg D filings or accredited investor verifications
- Costa Rica corporate registration (handled by CR counsel)
- Final drafting of arm's-length templates (handled in subsequent engagement after structural sign-off)
