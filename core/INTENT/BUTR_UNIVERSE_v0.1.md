## Questions for Counsel

This brand-architecture is being pressure-tested before any token launch. Specific questions:

### Q1 — Howey survivability of the token + patron system

Token mechanics: fair launch + revoke mint/freeze + fixed supply + entertainment-first language. But the structural pattern is:
- Holder buys BUTR on open market
- Uses BUTR to tip Milkmaids
- Top patrons receive butter gifts + leaderboard perks
- Token has utility as tipping medium + status signal

Does this structure survive Howey when:
- (a) tipping can also occur in USDC/SOL/other accepted coins (BUTR is not the exclusive tipping medium)?
- (b) butter gifts are framed as "gratitude" not "yield" + can be sent to any supporter, not just BUTR holders?
- (c) the language guardrails (no investment, no yield, no guaranteed rewards) are real and enforced?

Or does the substance-over-form analysis collapse the disclaimers — i.e., even with all the right words, the structural pattern "buy token → access community + perks + gifts" satisfies Howey?

### Q2 — Milkmaid creator-economy classification

What is the legal status of Milkmaids?
- Independent creators (1099)?
- Brand LLC employees (W-2)?
- Affiliates / contractors?
- §501(d) members (if BUTR operates under CORA)?

Tipping mechanics: BUTR holders tip Milkmaids in BUTR or in cash-equivalent. The Brand LLC takes a percentage? Milkmaids keep 100%? Platform fees?

Does the combination of (creator content + tipping in crypto + leaderboard rewards + butter gifts) trigger any of:
- Money transmission license requirements (the Brand acts as tipping conduit)
- Worker classification reclassification (Milkmaids may be employees in CA / NY / state-by-state)
- State adult-entertainment regulation (the "Sexy Milkmaid" / "flirty not explicit" zone)
- Platform liability for content (Section 230 + state laws)

### Q3 — Real product layer + state food law

Butter from "licensed creamery / commercial kitchen" — what state/federal regulations apply when:
- Butter is shipped interstate to top patrons?
- Butter is gifted at local events?
- Token holders receive butter (does this make it a sale rather than a gift)?
- FDA dairy labeling, state inspection, shipping cold chain?

### Q4 — Marketing language audit

The diagram lists explicit "avoid" terms: Investment language, revenue share, yield promises, "contribute now, receive tokens later," guaranteed rewards, buyback promises.

Are there OTHER terms / patterns that should be added to the hard-no list per Reves, Howey, and FTC consumer protection doctrine?

Specifically:
- "BUTR Churn Club" — does "club" + perks invoke membership securities analysis?
- "Patron tributes" — does "tribute" imply expected return?
- "Leaderboard rewards" — does ranking-tied gifting create implicit promise?
- Day-X countdown to $100M MC — does targeting a market cap publicly invoke price-prediction prohibition?

### Q5 — Standalone Brand vs operating under CORA Nation

Two architectural options:

**Option A — BUTR as standalone Brand LLC:**
- Own legal architecture
- Not bound by CORA's §501(d) constraints
- Not protected by CORA's nonprofit shield
- Standard meme-token + creator-economy company

**Option B — BUTR as Brand LLC under CORA Nation Brand Stack:**
- Arm's-length license from CORA for marks/community association
- Inurement constraints apply (Stewardship Council oversight, §4958 procedures, recusal)
- BUTR Brand-Stack-licensed → can route charity flow-through to CORA Heart-of-Gold fund
- Constrained by Brand Tokenization Architecture v0.4 CONVERGED hard-no list

Which is the cleaner shape for BUTR given:
- Token mechanic is core to BUTR's identity (v0.4 already killed tokens for CORA Brands)
- Tonal mismatch with CORA's apostolic/sacred substrate (sexy Milkmaid + crypto degen vs religious community)
- Founder is the same person (Sunheart) — affiliation will be inferred regardless

### Q6 — The "$100M MC" frame

Public targeting of a market cap is common in meme coins but creates a price-expectation signal. Even without "investment language," does Day-X churning toward $100M MC functionally communicate "buy now, value will rise"?

What is the legal exposure of publicly broadcasting an MC target?

### Q7 — Sexy Milkmaid persona

"Pin-up farm glam, flirty not explicit" — what's the legal line between this brand voice and:
- Adult-entertainment classification triggers (state-by-state)?
- Platform terms of service issues (X, TikTok, IG content moderation)?
- Brand association damage to CORA Nation if BUTR is under that umbrella?

### Q8 — Counsel engagement scope

If BUTR proceeds, does this Brand require its own human counsel engagement separate from CORA's? Or can BUTR's launch be folded into the existing v0.4 Addendum C counsel engagement scope?
