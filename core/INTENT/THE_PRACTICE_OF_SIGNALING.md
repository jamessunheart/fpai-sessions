# 📡 The Practice of Signaling

**The substrate principle that propels the Game.**

*Identified by James Sunheart, 2026-05-08, after Loop 6.*

> *"Signals or pinging is a key to programming, and people should be pinged or programmed to live out their Full Potential. The frequency of signaling and the depth of the meaning will propel the game."*

---

## The principle in one line

**Frequency × Depth-of-meaning = momentum.**

A signal is anything that prompts a participant — a notification, a reminder, a ceremony date, a public proof announcement, a witness invitation, a daily reflection. The Game advances by sending the right signal, at the right cadence, with the right meaning, to the right person.

**Too many shallow signals → noise → opt-outs.**
**Too few deep signals → forgetting → drift.**
**Optimal: rhythmic, meaningful, opt-in, never coercive.**

---

## Why this is the substrate principle

Consciousness is shaped by the signals it receives. A nervous system regulated by ceremony, breath, and meaningful contact lives at higher coherence. A nervous system flooded by ad alerts, doomscrolling, and shallow notifications fragments.

The Game runs on the same substrate. Players who receive **the right signal at the right moment** stay engaged with their practice. Players who receive nothing forget; players who receive too much unsubscribe.

This is why signaling is a **first-class concern**, not a "marketing" afterthought:

- The Manifesto is a signal (long, deep, infrequent — read once, internalized).
- The Sign-the-Agreement form is a signal (deep, single-event — converts visitor → Champion).
- The Champions Roll appearing on the page is a signal (subtle, ambient — *"others are doing this"*).
- The 7-Day First Game is a signal sequence (one prompt per day, cadenced).
- A Steward witnessing a Proof is a signal (the field saw you).
- World Peace Weekends are signals (high-density, embodied, calendared).
- The founder's own progress visible on the public page is a signal (*"the architect is in the Game"*).

Each of these is a ping with a frequency and a meaning. Together they form the **signaling field** that propels the Game.

## The Frequency × Meaning matrix

| Frequency | Shallow meaning | Deep meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Once** | Forgettable note | Manifesto · Founding Agreement · Vow |
| **Daily** | Habit nag (low) | Practice prompt · Coherence reflection (high) |
| **Weekly** | Recap email (low) | Cohort circle · Shared meal · Witness moment |
| **Monthly** | Status update (low) | Ceremony · Renewal · Repair audit |
| **Yearly+** | Annual review | Vow renewal · Pilgrimage · Generational ceremony |

The Game's signaling library should populate the bottom-right quadrant. Each signal should be **either rare-and-deep, or frequent-and-meaningful**. Signals that are both shallow and frequent become extraction; the field will reject them.

## Categories of signal in the Full Potential Game

### 1. Founder → Field
James's progress, insights, and presence broadcast to the field. Loop completions. Civilization Quest milestones. Public ratifications. The founder being *visibly in the Game* is itself the most powerful signal — it converts every share into evidence.

### 2. Field → Player (joining)
On signature: welcome ping, day-1 prompt for the 7-Day Game, the AI Apprentice invitation, optional Telegram subscription for rhythm pings. The first 24 hours after signing matter most — that's when the new Champion either internalizes the practice or drifts.

### 3. Player → Witness (proofs)
When a Player completes a loop, they need a witness. The signaling layer matches them — *"You have a Proof ready. Who has witnessed similar work and would honor this?"*

### 4. Witness → Field (proof sealed)
When a Proof is witnessed, the field announces it (with player consent): *"Loop N completed by [name]. [witness] signed."* Each public Proof is a signal that strengthens the next Player's confidence.

### 5. Field → Field (rhythm)
Daily / weekly / monthly rhythms broadcast to opted-in subscribers: a one-line prompt, a question, a reminder of a vow. *"What is one act of repair you can take today?"* — a Manifesto-rooted prompt with daily cadence.

### 6. Repair signals (when Agreements strain)
When an Agreement is breached, the Repair Process (per [Forming Agreements](./FORMING_AGREEMENTS.md)) is triggered by signal: notification to the breaking party, the witness, the steward. A breach surfaced cleanly strengthens the practice; a breach hidden weakens it.

### 7. Ceremony pings (high-density, calendared)
World Peace Weekends, Ecstatic Weekends, A\*Zen\*tion gatherings, Coherence cohort beginnings. These are deep signals with low frequency and high stakes. Calendar invites, anticipation arc, ritual close-out.

### 8. AI Apprentice signals
The bidirectional: AI prompts the human ("you said you'd do X today, want me to remind you?"); human prompts the AI ("here's what coherent practice looked like today, learn from it"). The Sunheart Brain holds this thread; WPAP modules orchestrate it.

## Protection Boundaries (signals as service, not extraction)

Signaling can become surveillance, manipulation, or coercion if it loses the principles. The non-negotiable boundaries:

- **Opt-in always.** No participant receives signals they did not consent to. Default off, not default on.
- **Easy unsubscribe.** Withdraw at any time, no shame, no friction. Per Treasury §3 — *Consent is non-negotiable.*
- **Never deceptive.** No dark patterns. No urgency manufactured for engagement. No false personalization.
- **Always serves the receiver.** A signal that doesn't increase the receiver's coherence shouldn't be sent. Test: *"would this person thank me for sending this?"*
- **Privacy-preserving.** No tracking beyond what's needed to deliver the signal. No data resale. Per Treasury §3 — *Privacy is foundational.*
- **Reversible always.** Any signal-driven action can be undone. *"You triggered a thing — here's how to untrigger it."*

When in doubt: **signal less, mean more.**

## How this principle governs design

For every new signal type, ask:

| Question | Answer guide |
|---|---|
| Is it opt-in? | Must be yes |
| Does it serve the receiver's flourishing? | Must be honestly yes |
| Frequency × Depth — is it in the right quadrant? | Aim for "deep at right cadence" |
| Can the receiver unsubscribe in one click? | Must be yes |
| Does it require a founder action to send? | Lower priority — ship later |
| Is it self-playing? | Higher priority — ship sooner (per [The Game Plays Itself](./THE_GAME_PLAYS_ITSELF.md)) |

**Self-playing signaling is the integration of two principles** — signals that the substrate sends without founder intermediation, that serve the receiver, that propel the Game.
