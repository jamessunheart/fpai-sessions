### 🟢 LOW / NOTE — Brand-specific token deferral should state it requires a *new* full AI council pass + counsel engagement before revisiting

**Risk:** v0.4 states brand-specific tokens are "deferred indefinitely; may revisit after structure is operational and counsel-cleared." This is correct. However, the doc doesn't explicitly state that revisiting this requires a full new AI council + counsel engagement — not just an amendment to this architecture. The risk is someone treating v0.4 as a license to add brand tokens later without restarting the review process. The Howey risk from v0.1 (CRITICAL) was severe; brand tokens with revenue + NAV + equity characteristics are not cured by time passing.

**Corpus citation:** 18pgChurch_Legal_Summary-Resource.md.pdf (Chunk 11): "SEC v. Howey, 328 U.S. 293 (1946)" cited as key securities law reference. (General law beyond corpus: Howey's four-prong test — investment of money, common enterprise, expectation of profits, from efforts of others — would apply to any brand token with equity or revenue characteristics.)

**Suggested fix:** Add one sentence to the "What v0.4 explicitly does NOT do" section: "Revisiting brand-specific tokens requires a new full AI council pass and a new securities-law counsel engagement before any issuance — v0.4's counsel sign-off does not cover future token designs."

This is not legal — it's technical — but it's a gap in the activation preconditions checklist. Counsel should confirm the existing declaration is properly documented before building on top of it.
