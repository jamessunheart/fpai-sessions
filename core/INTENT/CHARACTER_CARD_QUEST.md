# 🎴 CHARACTER CARD QUEST — The Full Potential Game

*Quest #1 · Onboarding · Estimated time: 5 min (signup) → progressive depth*

---

## What this is

Welcome to **the Full Potential Game** — a purpose-driven social network where players coordinate around quests, offers and needs match each other, and reputation is built through witnessed reality, not vanity metrics.

Your **Character Card** is your living node in the network. It has two layers:

- **Aspirational** — what you're playing for · you fill this · slow-shifts · syndicates to social media
- **Reality** — what actually shipped · your AI tracks this · refreshes weekly · witnessed by people you trust

And four **visibility tiers**, so you control who sees what:

🌐 **Public** — visible on profile, syndicates to LinkedIn / X / Instagram / Bluesky
👥 **Player** — visible to other Full Potential Game players
🤍 **Inner Circle** — visible only to people on your Witness Roster
🔒 **Sacred / Self-only** — only you and your AI

And four **levels** of depth, so you don't have to fill everything at once:

🟢 **L1 Signup** (5 min) · card goes live and discoverable
🟡 **L2 Player** (15 min) · matchable for collaborations
🔵 **L3 Matching Depth** (30 min, AI-led) · matchable for *teams*
🟣 **L4 Living Character** (ongoing) · AI-maintained, witnessed

---

## Three ways to play this quest

1. **🤖 Fastest — AI Port-In:** Paste the prompt below into your existing AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, whatever you use most). It drafts your card based on what it already knows about you. You refine. *5–10 minutes total.*

2. **✍️ Manual:** Use the template further down the document. Fill what feels alive, leave the rest for later levels.

3. **🪞 Hybrid:** AI drafts → you sharpen → witnesses confirm. Recommended.

# 🎯 What happens after you submit

1. **Your card goes live** at the visibility tiers you set
2. **Public layer syndicates** to your linked social profiles (with your permission)
3. **You become discoverable** to other players based on offers / needs / quests
4. **Your AI apprentice** (yours, not a system AI) uses your card as source-of-truth for representing you
5. **Your Witness Roster nominees get a notification** to confirm — once they confirm, they can vouch for your Field Score
6. **Reality layer activates** — your AI starts tracking what you ship, surfaces patterns gradually, refreshes the layer weekly

You're now playing.
