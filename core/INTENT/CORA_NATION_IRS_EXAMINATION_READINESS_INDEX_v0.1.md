## 1. Why this Index exists

If the IRS, a state charitable-organization regulator, or a court formally requests CORA Nation's records, the response is governed by Records & Privacy Protocol §7. This Index specifies **what file is produced** — the structured set of documents that demonstrates CORA Nation's substance as a religious community operating in compliance with §508(c)(1)(A), §501(c)(3) restrictions, and §4958 disciplines.

This Index serves three functions:

a) **Production map** — when an examiner requests records, the Counsel Liaison produces from this Index;
b) **Gap analysis** — current status of each component (in-place / drafted / missing) shows what's not yet ready;
c) **Build target** — the gaps in §3 are the v1.0 readiness deliverables.

The Index is an internal document; it is NOT itself produced to examiners.

### 2.13 UBIT Analysis Framework

§508(c)(1)(A) churches are exempt from federal income tax on income substantially related to their religious mission but remain potentially subject to Unrelated Business Income Tax (UBIT) under IRC §511 for income from trade-or-business activity not substantially related to the religious purpose. Counsel determines per-activity classification; this Index documents the framework CORA Nation maintains.

| CORA Nation activity | Substantially-related to religious mission? | UBIT analysis status |
|---|---|---|
| **Member contributions / donations** | Yes — religious-community-internal | ✅ Not UBIT |
| **Retreat fees from CORA Members** (Camp Zen retreats) | Yes — religious-community ceremonial/teaching activity for Members | 🟡 Likely not UBIT; Counsel to confirm |
| **Retreat fees from non-Members** (open retreats) | Mixed — depends on retreat character and audience | ❌ Counsel review required per offering |
| **Mirror Witnessing fees** (if Coherent Credit redemption translates to dollar value) | Yes if pastoral, depends on Counsel | 🟡 Counsel review required |
| **Coherent Credit issuance** (no cash inflow; redemption is religious-community service) | Yes — religious-community discipline | ✅ Not UBIT |
| **Brand LLC charitable contributions to CORA** | Yes — donative income | ✅ Not UBIT (donative) |
| **Sale of CORA-branded materials** (books, recordings, etc.) | Mixed — depends on volume, purpose, audience | 🟡 Counsel review per category |
| **Investment income** from Sunheart Trust holdings (if any) | Generally not UBIT for §508 churches | 🟡 Counsel review |
| **Property income from Zen Village substrate** (if any external rentals) | Likely not religious-mission-related | ❌ UBIT exposure — Counsel review |

**UBIT defense memos:** For each CORA Nation service offering with non-trivial revenue, CORA Nation drafts a one-page "substantially related to religious mission" memo per Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum C requirement. These are internal documentation, not filed with IRS, but produced on examiner request.

## 4. Examiner-Defense Themes

This Index supports defense against the following anticipated examiner concerns, with the documents that respond to each:

### 4.1 "Is CORA Nation a real religious community?"
**Defense:** §2.2 (doctrine — Canon v0.1's 13 sources) + §2.6 (ceremonial/sacramental records — Ecstatic Weekend, A*Zen*tion, Saturday Casual, Coherence Course) + §2.11 (lived practice substrate — Zen Village, real Members, Mirror Witnessing).

### 4.2 "Is CORA Nation properly self-declared under §508(c)(1)(A)?"
**Defense:** §2.1 (Declarations + Manifestos) + §2.3 (Bylaws Article I §1.02) + (with counsel) §501(d) apostolic-association analysis per Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum C.

### 4.3 "Is there private inurement?"
**Defense:** §2.3 (Conflict of Interest Policy) + §2.9 (§4958 documentation) + §2.10 (Brand LLC arm's-length docs) + Pastoral Leadership Structure §2.3 (Founding Steward authority limits) + Bylaws §3.03 (control-vs-ownership separation).

### 4.4 "Is CORA Nation operating a securities scheme through 'tokens' or 'allocation weight'?"
**Defense:** §2.4 (Brand Tok v0.4 — brand tokens deferred indefinitely; Coherent Credit closed-loop, no transfer, no purchase; Allocation Platform earned-not-purchased, no economic benefit to holder, recusal-enforced, hard-rule Bylaws §12.02) + Records Protocol §5 (privacy of leaderboard absolute balances and gift amounts, addressing the Counsel critique HIGH #4).

### 4.5 "Are Members investing in CORA Nation?"
**Defense:** §2.5 (PMA Membership Agreement §2.2 — explicit not-an-investment language; §3.7 — affirmation of no investment expectation; §8.6 — affirmation no investment solicitation) + Admission & Exit Procedures §11.4 (Brand LLC investor + Member overlap conditions).

### 4.6 "Are Brand LLCs operating as the religious organization's commercial arm in disguise?"
**Defense:** §2.4 (Brand Tok v0.4 architecture) + §2.10 (B.1 Operating Agreements + B.2 Charitable Contribution Agreements) + COI Policy §8 (Brand LLC tier separation) + Records Protocol §5.4 (binary-only membership verification).

### 4.7 "Is the Costa Rica substrate operated in compliance with CR law?"
**Defense:** Brand Tok v0.4 activation precondition (CR entity + CR counsel + worker classification + FBAR/FATCA) — currently 🟡 in progress per §3.3.

### 4.8 "Why aren't you filing Form 990?"
**Defense:** §508(c)(1)(A) churches are generally not required to file Form 990 (IRC §6033(a)(3)(A)(i)). State requirements vary — counsel to determine per Brand Tok v0.4 Addendum C state survey.

### 4.9 "Why do Stewards receive both community-provision AND Brand LLC compensation?"
**Defense:** §2.4 (Brand Tok v0.4 — community-provision under §4958 procedure; Brand LLC compensation arm's-length and separate) + COI Policy §6 + Pastoral Leadership Structure §4.5.

### 4.10 "Show us a Member who has been disciplined / restored / withdrew — how was it handled?"

## 5. Production Procedure

On formal examiner request, the Counsel Liaison:

1. **Engages external counsel** before responding (Records Protocol §7.2);
2. **Receives the request** and identifies what is asked for and what is privileged;
3. **Produces from the Examination File** per the relevant section of §2, asserting privileges as appropriate per §7.3 (privilege log);
4. **Maintains the production record** — what was produced, when, to whom;
5. **Reports to Stewardship Council + Pastoral Council** on examination status;
6. **Coordinates Member notice** where individual records are sought (§7.4);
7. **Documents the engagement** for future readiness.

## 6. Annual Readiness Audit

The Counsel Liaison shall, no later than thirty (30) days before each Annual Meeting:

e) Maintain the Examination File in production-ready form.
