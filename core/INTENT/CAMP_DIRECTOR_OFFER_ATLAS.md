### The non-negotiables

Six things from the blueprint that I want you to read before signing — because if you can't hold these, the role isn't right and I'd rather know now:

1. **The golden rule.** The spiritual ideal isn't the joke. The gap between ideal and human is. Communication, marketing, team culture — all rooted in this.
2. **No guest is a unit of revenue.** Every guest is a soul we honor. Efficiency serves that, never replaces it.
3. **The Sunheart Rule.** Protect my time. I do what only I can do. Everything else, you filter.
4. **Circulation economics.** Premium prices, value flowing, earned participation allowed. Never extraction.
5. **Field coherence over short-term revenue.** If a retreat would fill but break the field, you don't run it.
6. **Honesty over comfort.** Hard truths to team, guests, and me. The whole thing breaks without it.

If any of these are unclear or feel wrong, say so before saying yes.
