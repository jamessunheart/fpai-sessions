## What happens in the 90 minutes

**Open (10 min)** — Rapport, your intention, what "broken through" looks like for you.

**Diagnose (30 min)** — Deep look at where you are, what's moving, what's stuck. I ask the questions that surface the real pattern.

**Clarify (20 min)** — We name the one bottleneck explicitly. Root cause. Why it persists. What it costs you weekly.

**Design (20 min)** — The path through. AI, systems, leverage, people. Order matters; we sequence it.

**Commit (10 min)** — Your first 3 actions. The support you'll need. How we close the loop.

## FAQ

**How is this different from coaching?**
Coaching helps you with ongoing process. This is a single high-leverage diagnostic. One call, one map, one set of next moves. No subscription, no series.

**What if I'm not sure I have a bottleneck?**
Then you have a bottleneck you can't see, which is the most expensive kind. Book the call.

**What if my issue is personal, not business?**
Same framework. The Bottleneck Session works on life-systems as readily as on business-systems. Same root-cause pattern detection.

**What prep do I need to do?**
Five minutes — fill the intake form when you book. Three questions: where you are, where you want to be, what feels stuck.

**Can I bring my partner / co-founder?**
Yes for partners running a business together. Otherwise the session is most effective 1:1.
