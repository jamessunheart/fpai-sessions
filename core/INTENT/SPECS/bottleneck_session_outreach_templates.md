# Bottleneck Session — Outreach Templates v1

**5 variants for Day 3-7 of the 14-day launch.** Match template to relationship-tier. All under 80 words. WhatsApp/Telegram-optimized.

## A. Close warm — past client / direct relationship

> Hey [Name] — opening a small batch of Bottleneck Sessions this week. 90 minutes, we identify the one thing actually holding you back from your next move and map the fastest path through it (AI, systems, people). $500-1,500 depending on stage. You came to mind because of where you are right now. Want in?

## B. Warm — referral or business-network connection

> Hey [Name] — I'm running a focused diagnostic offer. 90 minutes, $500-1,500. We sit down, find the one bottleneck holding you back from your next level, and design the path through it using AI + systems + leverage. Limited slots opening this week. I'm offering a proper 90-min Bottleneck Session — same diagnostic energy, structured deliverable (a written breakthrough map you keep). $500-1,500 depending on stage. Opening a few spots this week. Curious?

## Tier matching matrix

| Tier | Template | Expected response rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Past client | A | 30-50% | Strongest — they've seen the work |
| Direct warm | A or B | 20-30% | Personal note · use A if you've helped them recently |
| Network referral | B | 15-25% | Lead with the structure of the offer |
| Free-help recipient | C | 25-40% | Reference what you helped with by name |
| Admired stranger | D | 5-10% | Don't pressure · plant + retreat |
| Loose tie | E | 2-5% | Spray-and-pray · low effort · 10/day |

## Daily outreach cadence (Day 4-7)

**10 messages per day.** Mix:
- 3 from Tier A/B (highest hit rate, slowest to draft — go first when energy is fresh)
- 4 from Tier C (mid-tier, personalize the reference)
- 3 from Tier D/E (cooler, copy-paste with name swap)

Track in spreadsheet/Airtable: name, tier, date sent, status (sent / replied / booked / declined / ghost). At Day 7, you should have:
- 60-70 messages sent
- 8-15 replies
- 3-6 bookings
- 1-3 paid sessions delivered or scheduled

## Reply-handling templates

### Reply: "Yes, interested" / curious
> Great — here's the booking link: [URL]. Pick a time that works, fill the short intake (3 questions), pay through the link. I'll see you on the call. Any questions before booking?

### Reply: "Tell me more"
> Sure. It's a 90-minute call where I run a focused diagnostic to surface the one constraint actually holding you back. You walk away with a written Bottleneck Breakthrough Map — your real bottleneck, root cause, top 3 priorities, 90-day plan, AI/systems/people leverage. $500 solo, $1,000 small team, $1,500 established business. Refund if it doesn't surface something useful. Link: [URL]

### Reply: "Not right now"
> All good — I'll keep you in mind if I'm running another batch. If anything shifts and you want to revisit, just reach out.

### Reply: "Too expensive"
> Fair. The session pays for itself if you act on 1 of the 3 priorities. But it's not for everyone. If price is the blocker, I'm probably not the right person for you right now — that's an honest answer, not a sales move. Reach out down the road if it makes sense.

### Reply: "Can we do this for free / discount?"
> No discount on the diagnostic — the pricing is intentional, it sets the seriousness of the engagement on both sides. If you want to chat informally about your situation, we can do that, but not under the Bottleneck Session structure.
