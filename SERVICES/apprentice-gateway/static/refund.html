Refund Policy

Champion Stack Apprentice · Full Potential · Effective 2026-05-19 · v1.0

Plain-English summary:
  • Full refund within 30 days. No questions.
  • Founding Tier: pro-rated refund within 90 days.
  • Cancel anytime; you keep access through your paid period.
  • Refunds go to original payment method, 5-10 business days.
  • Email james@fullpotential.com to request.

1. Standard refund — 30 days

Within 30 days of your first subscription purchase, request a full refund of all fees paid (subscription + any Founding Tier add-on).

  • No questions asked
  • No reason required
  • Request: email james@fullpotential.com
  • Processed within 3 business days; funds appear in 5-10 business days depending on bank

2. Founding Tier — pro-rated refund within 90 days

The optional Founding Tier one-time upgrade is refundable on a pro-rated basis within 90 days:

refund_amount = $497 × (90 − days_elapsed) / 90

Example: Founding Tier purchased, refund requested on day 30:

refund = $497 × (90 − 30) / 90 = $497 × 0.667 = $331.33

After 90 days, the Founding Tier is non-refundable.

3. Cancellation (separate from refund)

Cancel your monthly subscription anytime — separate from refund:

  • Stops future billing
  • Access retained through end of current paid period
  • Past charges are not automatically refunded (use refund flow if eligible)

To cancel: account settings or email james@fullpotential.com.

4. What's not refundable

  • Past monthly fees after 30 days from initial purchase (case-by-case if service-quality issue)
  • Founding Tier after 90 days
  • Fees on accounts that violated Terms of Service

5. Exceptional refunds

At our discretion, we may issue refunds outside this policy for:

  • Documented service outages affecting your specific use
  • Double-charges (always refunded)
  • Bona fide hardship (case-by-case)

6. How to request

  1. Email james@fullpotential.com with subject Refund request
  2. Include: account email, order date or Stripe receipt ID, optional reason
  3. We respond within 3 business days
  4. If approved, refund issued within 3 business days
  5. Bank processing: 5-10 business days

7. Effect on account

Issuing a refund:

  • Cancels active subscription
  • Closes Founding Tier benefits (if refunded)
  • Triggers brain-server data retention per Privacy Policy
  • Champion Game seat is preserved (Game state is permanent)

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us first. Chargebacks initiated without first emailing us may result in temporary account suspension while disputes are resolved. We work in good faith through normal refund processes.
