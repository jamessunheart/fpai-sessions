Privacy Policy

Champion Stack Apprentice · Full Potential · Effective 2026-05-19 · v1.0

Plain-English summary:
  1. We collect what you give us — goals, journals, messages, billing. We do not buy data.
  2. We store it on our own servers (the "brain server"). Not on a third-party SaaS.
  3. We use it only to give you the product. We do not sell your data, ever.
  4. You can export everything we have on you. You can delete it. Just ask.
  5. If you cancel, your private data is purged 90 days after lapse unless you request earlier deletion or export.
  6. Stripe processes payments. We never see card numbers.
  7. OpenAI / Anthropic APIs may process snippets of your data for AI features. They do not retain it under their commercial terms.
  8. If a regulator demands data, we tell you (unless legally gagged) before complying.
  9. Questions: james@fullpotential.com

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy covers Champion Stack Apprentice (the "Service") operated by Full Potential ("we", "us"). Contact: james@fullpotential.com.

2. What we collect

2.1 Data you provide

  • Account: name, email, optional profile fields, billing address (via Stripe)
  • Content: goals, journal entries, Character Card, Telegram messages forwarded to brain server, calendar items, structured state
  • Communications: support messages, audio if submitted

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Usage data: feature use, timestamps, error logs
  • Device data: IP address (session security only), browser, OS
  • Cookies: session only. No third-party advertising cookies.

2.3 From third parties

  • Stripe: payment status, last-4 of card, customer ID

3. How we use it

Only to:

  1. Operate the Service (Ember-coach context, dashboards, cross-tool sync)
  2. Process payments and fulfill subscriptions
  3. Communicate about the Service
  4. Improve the Service (aggregated, de-identified patterns only)
  5. Comply with legal obligations

We do not: sell data · use private content to train external models · share content with other users without your action · run ad targeting.

5. Storage + security

  • Primary storage on our own servers, encrypted at rest
  • Backups encrypted, 30-day retention, geographically separate
  • TLS 1.2+ in transit
  • API tokens stored outside the codebase; rotated on incident

We are a small operation. We make reasonable, documented security efforts proportionate to data sensitivity. No SOC 2 / ISO 27001 claim.

6. Retention

  • Active accounts: retained while subscribed + 90 days after lapse
  • After lapse + 90 days: vector memory + canonical files purged unless you request export or earlier deletion
  • Billing records: 7 years (US federal tax retention)
  • Support emails: 2 years
  • Warning emails sent 14/7/1 days before purge

7. Your rights

You may:

  • Export — request a JSON dump. We respond within 30 days.
  • Delete — request deletion (other than billing records we must legally retain). Honored within 30 days.
  • Correct — request correction of account info.
  • Object — opt out of specific uses.
  • Complain — file with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA): right to know, delete, and to designate an authorized agent. We do not sell data. We do not discriminate against users who exercise these rights.

EU/UK (GDPR best-effort): we operate to GDPR principles. Lawful bases: contract, consent, legitimate interest.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. International transfers

Servers are in the US. Using the Service from outside the US constitutes consent to US data processing.

10. Changes

Material changes announced via email at least 30 days in advance. Current version always lives at /apprentice/privacy.
