❓ Mission FAQ

Everything you need to know about completing missions for Full Potential AI

🚀 Getting Started

What are missions?

Missions are specific tasks that help build Full Potential AI's ecosystem. They range from coding and development to documentation, design, and research.

Each mission has:

  • Clear objectives - What needs to be accomplished
  • Specifications - Detailed requirements and steps
  • Estimated time - How long it should take
  • Reward - Compensation for completion
How do I claim a mission?

1. Browse available missions on the Mission Board

2. Click on a mission to see its full details and specifications

3. Click "Claim Mission" and enter your name/identifier

4. Start working! The mission is now assigned to you.

💡 Tip: Only claim missions you can complete within the estimated time. This helps keep the board moving!
Should mission work be included in my Upwork time?

Yes! If you're working through Upwork, mission work counts as billable time.

  • Track your time normally in Upwork
  • Reference the mission ID (e.g., M001) in your work diary notes
  • Missions are legitimate project work
📝 Note: When logging time, mention which mission you worked on (e.g., "M003 - Chat Orchestrator implementation")

🎯 Mission Claims & Assignments

If a mission is already claimed, can someone else do it?

Generally, no. Once claimed, a mission is assigned to that person. However:

  • Stale claims - If no progress after 48 hours, the mission may be released
  • Collaboration - You can ask to collaborate with the current assignee
  • Similar work - Ask if there's a related mission you can take

If you see a claimed mission with no activity, message the team - we may reassign it.

What if I can't finish a mission I claimed?

No problem! Just let us know:

  • Update the mission status to "Blocked" with a note
  • Or message the team to release the mission
  • Document any progress you made so the next person can continue

We'd rather you release a mission early than let it sit incomplete.

Can I work on multiple missions at once?

Yes, but be realistic. We recommend:

  • Maximum 2-3 active missions at a time
  • Focus on completing one before starting another
  • Don't claim missions you can't start within 24 hours

🏆 Rewards & Recognition

Will those who do more missions remain on the team?

Absolutely! Mission completion is a key factor in team decisions:

  • Consistent contributors get priority for ongoing work
  • Quality completions lead to more complex/higher-paying missions
  • Leaderboard recognition highlights top contributors
  • Long-term roles are offered to proven performers
🌟 Bottom line: The more missions you complete well, the more opportunities you'll receive. We invest in people who invest in the mission.
How are missions compensated?

Compensation depends on your arrangement:

  • Upwork contractors: Bill your normal hourly rate
  • Direct contributors: Per-mission bounties (listed on each mission)
  • FPAI Token holders: Additional token rewards for completion
What's the leaderboard?

The Leaderboard tracks top contributors by:

  • Number of missions completed
  • Quality of work
  • Speed of completion
  • Community contributions

Top contributors get recognition and first access to new opportunities!

📤 Submitting Work

How do I submit completed mission work?

Use the Harvester to submit your code:

  1. Go to the Harvester portal
  2. Enter the mission ID
  3. Paste your code or upload files
  4. Add notes about your implementation
  5. Submit for review

Your submission will be reviewed and merged if it meets the specifications.

What if my work needs revisions?

Revisions are normal! If your submission needs changes:

  • You'll receive feedback on what to adjust
  • Make the changes and resubmit
  • The mission stays assigned to you during revisions

We work collaboratively to get submissions merged.
