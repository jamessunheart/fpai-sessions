# The Apprentice Studio Manifesto

> **DRAFT — for human review before publication.**

## We exist because

Most AI is being built to replace humans.
We're training AI-native builders to build AI that *expands* humans — and the products to prove it.

## What we do

The Apprentice Studio is a living lab where small cohorts of builders use the best available AI tools to ship regenerative products inside the Full Potential AI ecosystem.

We are not a school.
We are not an accelerator.
We are a studio with apprenticeships.

## What we believe

**1. AI is a co-builder, not a tool.**
Every apprentice has Aria — a permanent AI collaborator and coach — alongside them. We are the first studio on Earth where AI is staff, not subject matter.

**2. Apprentices keep what they build.**
70% to the apprentice, 20% to the studio, 10% to a shared cohort pool. Anything else makes the program extractive — and an extractive program cannot teach regenerative work.

**3. Ship by week 8 or pivot.**
Curriculum hours are not the currency. Shipped products are.

**4. Three lenses for everything we ship.**
- *Regenerative* — creates more than it consumes.
- *Sovereignty-respecting* — users own their data and agency.
- *Consciousness-expanding* — makes humans more capable, not more dependent.

A project must hit two of three or it doesn't ship under the studio name.

**5. Small cohorts, brutal selection.**
Four to six apprentices per cohort. We reject the vast majority of applicants. Scarcity creates a reputation that marketing cannot.

## How it works

| Phase | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Kickoff retreat | 1 week onsite | Trust, vision, system setup, Aria pairing. |
| Build sprint | 8 weeks remote | Daily co-build, weekly demos, ship by week 8. |
| Demo retreat | 1 week onsite | Launch, public demo day, partner showcase. |

Total: 10 weeks. Hybrid by design. The retreat is a ritual; the work is distributed.

## What we are looking for

Builders who can ship.
Humans who care about what they build.
People who want AI to make us more, not less.

## What you get

- Free housing, meals, and infrastructure during retreats.
- Anthropic / OpenAI / Google API credits to burn freely.
- A permanent Aria instance as your AI co-builder.
- The full FPAI infrastructure stack to build on.
- Daily co-building with your AI mentor.
- Weekly 1:1 with Aria Coach on values + energy.
- Lifelong alumni access to studio infra and contracts.
- Majority equity in everything you build.

## What we ask

- 10 weeks of full-time focus.
- Brutal honesty about what's working and what isn't.
- Service to the next cohort after yours.
- Default-license back to FPAI on anything you build using the infrastructure.

## Where it leads

Cohort 1 ships products. Those products earn revenue. That revenue funds cohort 2.
The studio compounds. The alumni network compounds. The infrastructure compounds.

We are not building a school. We are building the first generation of builders who treat AI as a collaborator in the work of expanding what humans can be.
