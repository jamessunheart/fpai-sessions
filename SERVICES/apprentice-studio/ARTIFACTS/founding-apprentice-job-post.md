# Founding Apprentice — Apprentice Studio (FPAI)

> **DRAFT — for human review before posting externally.**

## Why this role exists

The Apprentice Studio is a new AI-native studio launching inside Full Potential AI. We're hiring **one founding apprentice** — a builder who will help design the program by doing the work, ship our first internal product, and become co-architect of cohort 1.

This is not a normal job. You are the first body in the room. What you build and how you work becomes the template for every apprentice who follows.

## What you'll do (60-90 days)

**Primary deliverable:** ship **Apprentice OS v0.1** — the internal toolkit every future apprentice will use to onboard, build, and demo inside FPAI.

That includes:

- Apprentice onboarding flow into the FPAI codebase + Aria pairing.
- Project tracking integrated with our agent infrastructure.
- Skills/learnings library that compounds across cohorts.
- Demo + showcase platform we use for cohort demo days.

**Secondary contributions:**

- Improve existing FPAI services (`aria-bridge`, `aria-command`, `aria-builder`, `sunheart-brain`) where you spot leverage.
- Document the studio's operating model from the inside as you live it.
- Co-write the cohort 1 curriculum based on what you actually learned.
- Help recruit cohort 1 (filter, interview, onboard).

## What you bring

**Required:**
- You have shipped real software. Not just side projects — something users have used.
- You are AI-native: you build with Claude / Cursor / agents as part of your default workflow, not as a novelty.
- You can hold a 60-day deliverable and ship it.
- You are honest about what's working and what isn't.
- You care about what AI is for, not just what it can do.

**Loved (not required):**
- Background in regenerative tech, consciousness research, somatic + tech crossover, or sovereign data systems.
- Open-source contributions in the AI agent / tooling space.
- Experience designing curriculum or running cohort programs.

## What we offer

- **Compensation:** $35k for 60-90 days (negotiable based on experience), plus equity in the Apprentice OS itself.
- **Path forward:** if it works, you transition to **Studio Lead** at full-time comp + equity in the studio's product portfolio.
- **Tools:** unlimited Anthropic / OpenAI / Google API credits. Full access to the FPAI infrastructure stack.
- **Aria:** your own AI collaborator, instanced for you.
- **Voice:** you co-design the program. This is not "execute someone else's vision."
- **Onsite or hybrid:** preference for onsite at the studio location for the first 30 days. Hybrid afterwards.
