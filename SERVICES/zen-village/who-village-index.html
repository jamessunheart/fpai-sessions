Zen Village · Chirripó, Costa Rica

what this is

A place built around one function — to remind the who that it is who. The land, the food, the sound, the silence are scaffolding. The reminding is what's real. Some weekends it arrives loud. Some weekends it arrives quiet. Either way, people leave carrying less of a someone than they came with.

the rhythm

  • Saturday Casual · weekly · drop-in Open doors, open fire, open conversation. Come as you are, leave when you're done.
  • A·Zen·tion · every two weeks · contemplative Silence, sitting, guided inquiry. A container for the kind of attention that changes things.
  • Ecstatic Weekend · every four to six weeks · full production Music, movement, ceremony, fire. The loud version of the same remembering.
  • Full Potential Retreat · monday through friday · by application Five days of diagnostic work, presence, and recalibration. You arrive with noise. You leave with signal. Inquire

the ground

Zen Village sits on 45 acres at the base of Chirripó, Costa Rica's highest peak, in the small mountain town of San Gerardo de Rivas. The nearest international airport is San José (SJO) — about three hours by car south on the Pan-American Highway. From there, twenty minutes uphill to the village.

Held by Sunheart and the ground team.
