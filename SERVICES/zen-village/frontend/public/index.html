What Your Days Feel Like

The Rhythm of a Week

There's no rigid schedule here. Just a natural rhythm that helps you slow down, come back to yourself, and leave clearer than you arrived.

🌅
Morning

Wake with the valley

Birds first, then light. Coffee on the porch or tea by the river. A walk through the trails if your body wants to move, or stillness if it doesn't. Breakfast from the garden. No alarms. Your nervous system remembers what morning is supposed to feel like.

☀️
Midday

Explore or rest

Swim in the river. Hike toward Chirripó. Help on the farm if you want your hands in the soil. Or do absolutely nothing -- read a book, nap in a hammock, sit with the dogs. This is your time. There is no wrong way to use it.

🧖
Afternoon

Soften

The wood-fired sauna heats up. The hot tub fills. The afternoon rain rolls through the valley and everything smells like earth. This is when the mind finally lets go of whatever it was holding. Conversations happen naturally -- or they don't. Both are fine.

🔥
Evening

Gather or go inward

Dinner cooked together or on your own. Fire pit under the stars. Sound healing in the Singing Dome if the night calls for it. Or just silence and the river. You'll sleep deeper than you have in months. The mountain air handles that.

🌿
Over time

Something shifts

By day three, the noise quiets. By day five, you start to hear yourself again. By the end of the week, you're not the same person who arrived. Not because we did something to you -- but because you finally had the space to come back to yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit

How do I get to Zen Village? +

From San José (SJO Airport): ~3 hours by car via the Pan-American Highway south to San Isidro de El General, then 20 minutes to San Gerardo de Rivas.

From Palmar Sur (PMZ Airport): ~2 hours north.

By Bus: Musoc bus from San José to San Isidro (~3.5 hrs, ~$8), then local bus or taxi to San Gerardo de Rivas (~30 min).

Self-drive: GPS coordinates work well. Any car can reach the Village Heart area. 4×4 is required for Escape Ridge cabins (El Nido & La Vista).

We can help coordinate airport transfers — just mention it in your inquiry.

What's included in my stay? +

Included in every stay:

  • Bed linens, pillows, and towels
  • Hot water showers
  • Electricity and lighting
  • Kitchen access (shared or private depending on unit)
  • River access and walking trails
  • Fire pit and communal gathering areas
  • Basic WiFi (perfect for messaging; this is a nature retreat — we encourage unplugging!)
  • On-site caretaker for questions and support

Available as add-ons: Sauna sessions, guided hikes, sound healing, ceremony space, transport coordination.

What are the check-in and check-out times? +

Check-in: 3:00 PM  |  Check-out: 11:00 AM

Early check-in or late check-out may be available depending on bookings — just ask when you inquire. We're flexible when we can be.

Is there a minimum stay? +

Individual cabins: 1-night minimum (2+ nights recommended to truly unwind).

Full property rental: 3-night minimum.

We offer weekly and monthly rates with significant discounts for longer stays.

What's the weather like? +

San Gerardo de Rivas sits at ~1,500m (4,900 ft) elevation — cooler and fresher than the coast.

Temperature: 15-25°C (60-77°F) year-round. Evenings are cool and perfect for sleeping.

Dry season (Dec-April): Sunny days, clear skies, ideal for hiking Chirripó.

Green season (May-Nov): Mornings are usually sunny; afternoon rain showers keep everything lush. Fewer crowds, lower prices.

Pack: Layers, a light rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes, and a swimsuit for the river.

Do I need a 4×4 vehicle? +

Village Heart & River Grove: Accessible by any car. Roads are paved most of the way.

Escape Ridge (El Nido & La Vista): Yes, 4×4 or quad is required. The hillside road is steep and unpaved — part of the adventure and what makes these cabins so secluded.

We can arrange transport to Escape Ridge cabins if you don't have a 4×4.

What about food and meals? +

Kitchen access: All cabins and homes have kitchen access (shared communal kitchen or private, depending on the unit).

Grocery stores: Small shops in San Gerardo de Rivas (10 min drive). Larger supermarkets in San Isidro (30 min).

Local restaurants: Several in the village serving traditional Costa Rican food (casados, gallo pinto, fresh fruit).

For retreats: We can arrange a private chef or catering — inquire for pricing.

Can I bring pets? +

We consider pet requests on a case-by-case basis. Zen Village has resident animals and natural wildlife, so not all pets are a fit. Please reach out in advance and we'll discuss.

Is it safe? +

Yes. San Gerardo de Rivas is a small, peaceful mountain village. Costa Rica is one of the safest countries in Central America, and this region is particularly tranquil.

The property has an on-site caretaker, and the community is close-knit. Standard travel common sense applies — as it would anywhere.

What's nearby? +
  • Chirripó National Park — Costa Rica's highest peak (3,820m). World-class 2-day hike.
  • Cloudbridge Nature Reserve — Free-access cloud forest trails, 10 min walk.
  • Natural hot springs — Several in the area.
  • Waterfalls — Multiple river waterfalls within hiking distance.
  • Birdwatching — Home of the resplendent quetzal and 400+ species.
  • Coffee farms — Tours available in the region.
  • San Isidro de El General — Full-service town 30 min away (hospital, shopping, restaurants).
Do you speak English? +

Yes! Our team speaks fluent English and Spanish. We welcome guests from everywhere.

I want to host a retreat. Where do I start? +

We love hosting retreat facilitators! Visit our Host Your Retreat page for full details on pricing, what's included, and to submit an inquiry.

Short version: Full property rental starts at $5,000 for a weekend. You get exclusive access to all 45 acres, all 10 structures, and all amenities. Yes, you can bring your own facilitators, chef, and team.

Can I visit before booking? +

Absolutely. We offer day passes ($20) that include sauna, river, and communal area access. It's a great way to experience the vibe before committing to a longer stay. You can also watch our video tour for a virtual preview.
