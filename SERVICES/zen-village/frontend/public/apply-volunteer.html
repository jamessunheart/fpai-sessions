Show up for the gathering. Bring your hands. Be part of the field.

We welcome volunteers for events, retreats, ceremonies, and weekend gatherings — including World Peace Gathering. This is for people who want to contribute their time around specific moments, not necessarily live on the land long-term.

If you'd rather live here and trade skills for stay (2+ weeks, on-site housing, full residency), apply for the Work Exchange lane instead.

What we need help with

  • Kitchen & Food — prep, cooking, serving, cleanup, nutrition planning
  • Land & Garden — planting, weeding, harvesting, composting, trail work
  • Build & Maintenance — carpentry, repairs, painting, construction projects
  • Hospitality — guest check-in, housekeeping, hosting, airport coordination
  • Fire & Sauna — tending the fire, sauna preparation, hot tub maintenance
  • Events — setup, breakdown, run-of-show support during retreats & gatherings
  • Stewardship — keeping spaces beautiful, energy clear, flow coherent

What we ask for

  • A minimum stay of 2 weeks (longer preferred)
  • 20–25 hours of contribution per week, 5 days a week
  • Respect for shared space, the land, and the community
  • Willingness to step into what's needed — not just what you prefer
  • Good energy. Genuine presence. Humility with your hands.
