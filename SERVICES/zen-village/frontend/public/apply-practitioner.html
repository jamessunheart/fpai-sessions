Hold the field. Bring your medicine. Land in a real container.

We host practitioners across modalities — breathwork, somatic therapy, bodywork, yoga, meditation, coaching, ceremony, plant medicine spaces, sound, energy work, ancestral healing.

Some come for a weekend. Some for a season. The strongest fits hold weekly slots inside our rolling Coherent Retreat rhythm — guest 1:1 sessions, group containers, integration support.

If you're more about music, dance floor, performance, ceremony as ecstatic experience, the Artist lane is closer to your work.

What we're looking for

  • Real practice. Years in your craft, not weekends in a workshop.
  • Container literacy. You've held groups before. You read the room. You finish a session whole.
  • Modality clarity. You know what you do, what you don't, and where you refer.
  • Audience compatibility. Our guests are mostly founders, creators, couples, and high-capacity humans on reset. Your work meets them where they are.
  • Ethical baseline. Trained where training matters. Insured where insurance matters. Honest about scope.

What you'd hold here

  • 1:1 sessions with retreat guests on a fixed weekly slot
  • Group containers — morning movement, breathwork, integration circles, ceremony
  • Optional: weekend events (e.g., World Peace Gathering) and themed gatherings
  • Co-creation with our team on retreat curriculum and integration tools

What you receive

  • Beautiful 45-acre container with river, sauna, fire, dwellings, and gathering halls
  • A dwelling on the land for the duration of your residency
  • Three meals a day, mostly plant-forward, made on site
  • Compensation negotiated per fit — fee, revenue share, or trade depending on container
  • An audience that arrives ready and grateful for your work
