Earn your stay. Live on the land. Be part of what's being built.

Not everyone has spare cash — but everyone has something to contribute. The Zen Village Work Exchange is a real co-creation: trade your time, skills, and presence for housing, food, and access to the rhythms of the village.

This isn't a free vacation, and it isn't a gig. It's a residency in a working sanctuary — kitchens to keep alive, land to tend, fires to hold, retreats to support, build projects to ship. People who do this well become extended family.

Where we need contribution

  • 🌱 Farm & Permaculture — planting, harvesting, food forest, composting, regenerative work
  • 🛠️ Build & Maintenance — carpentry, repairs, painting, creative builds, upkeep
  • 🍳 Kitchen & Hospitality — prep, cooking, serving, hosting, guest support
  • 🔥 Fire & Sauna — tending sauna, hot tub, fire circles, ceremonial heat
  • 🧘 Wellness & Teaching — yoga, breathwork, meditation, movement (share your practice)
  • 📸 Content & Creative — photo, video, writing, design — tell the story of this place
  • 💻 Tech & Digital — web, automation, systems, ops — keep the village humming
  • 🌿 Stewardship — keeping spaces beautiful, energy clear, the field coherent

What we ask for

  • A minimum stay of 2 weeks (longer preferred — the rhythm rewards depth)
  • 20–25 hours of focused contribution per week, 5 days on
  • Respect for shared space, the land, and the community
  • Willingness to step into what's needed — not just what you prefer
  • Good energy. Genuine presence. Humility with your hands.

What you receive

  • A bed in shared housing on the land
  • Three meals a day (mostly plant-forward, made on site)
  • Access to sauna, river, hot tub, trails
  • Participation in retreats, ceremonies, and gatherings as the rhythm allows
  • Real apprenticeship — work alongside people building something rare
