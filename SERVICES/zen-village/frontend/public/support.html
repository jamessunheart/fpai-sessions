Why It Matters

Where your support lands.

Land stewardship. 45 acres of regenerative care: trails, food forest, river edges, sauna, the gathering hall. The fire keeps burning because someone tended it.

Scholarships & Zen Credits. Every donation seeds Zen Credits granted to people who can't otherwise attend retreats and gatherings. You quietly bring someone to the river.

The work itself. Open-source rituals, integration tools, recordings, and Coherent Retreat curriculum that other communities can borrow. We share what works.

The ones holding it. Practitioners, fire-keepers, kitchen, the village team — paid as humans, not gigs.
