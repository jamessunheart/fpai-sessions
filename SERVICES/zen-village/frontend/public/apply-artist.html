Bring your medicine in sound, color, movement, and form.

The land sings differently when artists live on it. We host musicians, DJs, sound healers, painters, sculptors, dancers, performance artists, ceremony holders who work through art.

Some come for a weekend gathering. Some land for a season and let the village become studio, stage, and gallery.

If your work is more therapeutic, somatic, or healing-focused (1:1 sessions, group breathwork, etc.), the Practitioner lane is closer. If you're more content / film / story, see the Creator lane.

What we're looking for

  • A real body of work. Not a future plan — actual recordings, paintings, performances, footage we can listen to and watch.
  • Performance/exhibition experience. You've held a room or filled a wall. You know what your art does to people.
  • Field sensitivity. Your art reads the moment. You know when to lift the floor and when to drop it.
  • Collaborative spirit. You're open to artists from other lanes — a DJ holds the room while a painter works live, a movement artist closes a breathwork.

What you might bring

  • Ecstatic dance / morning movement music
  • Sound journeys, kirtan, devotional sets
  • Live music for ceremony, dinner, fire circle
  • Visual installations on the land — light, sculpture, textile
  • Painting / drawing residencies that culminate in a small show
  • Performance pieces for events (e.g., World Peace Gathering)
  • Workshops in your craft for our guests and community

What you receive

  • A dwelling on the land for the duration
  • Three meals a day, plant-forward, on site
  • Access to studio, sound system, gathering halls
  • An audience and a real room for your work
  • Compensation negotiated per fit — fee, revenue share, or trade depending on the container
