Your Journey to Community Support

Follow these steps to become an eligible member who can receive community blessings

1

Register & Complete Profile

Create your WhiteRock membership account with your full information.

✓ What you'll do: Email, name, address → Profile complete
2

Sign the 508(c)(1)(A) Disclosure

Read and acknowledge the charitable gift disclosure — required for legal compliance.

⚠️ Important: You must scroll to the bottom AND check the box. This protects both you and the ministry.
3

Submit Tithes & Earn CORA

Make sacred contributions to support the community. You'll earn CORA vitality credits at milestones.

💝 Tithe → Earn CORA
🤝 Service → Earn CORA
✨ Engage → Stay Active
30

Wait 30 Days as a Member

New members must be part of the community for at least 30 days before requesting blessings.

⏳ Why? This ensures commitment to the community and prevents abuse of the blessing system.
🙏

Request Community Blessing!

When you meet all eligibility requirements, the "Request Blessing" option appears in your dashboard.

✓ Eligibility Checklist:
☑️ Profile complete
☑️ Disclosure signed
☑️ Member 30+ days
☑️ Community capacity not paused

Types of Community Blessings

Discretionary support for community members in need — paid directly to vendors, not members.

🏠

Housing Support

Rent assistance and housing-related blessings paid directly to landlords and vendors.

🏥

Medical Assistance

Help with medical expenses, paid directly to healthcare providers.

⚡

Utilities Support

Assistance with electricity, water, and essential services.

🎓

Education Aid

Support for educational expenses and skill development.

How the Blessings System Works

A transparent, compliant process from request to disbursement

1

Submit Request

Eligible members submit blessing requests with category, amount, and vendor details.

2

Committee Review

Distribution Committee reviews with full member context. Compliance flag required for approval.

3

Vendor Payment

Blessings paid directly to vendors (landlords, hospitals, utilities) — not to members.

4

Full Audit Trail

Every action logged for 508(c)(1)(A) compliance verification.

How to Earn CORA

💝 Tithes — Contributions at milestones
🤝 Service — Verified community hours
✨ Engagement — Admin recognition
